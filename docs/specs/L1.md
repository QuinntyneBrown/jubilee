# L1 - High-Level Requirements

**Traces to:** [L0-001](L0.md), Jubilee's mission and its single level-0 requirement. Every
requirement below exists to serve it:

> Abiding in Jesus and for love of Him, Jubilee equips Christian business people in Toronto to
> prosper with purpose and resource His Kingdom.

Jubilee is Toronto's Christian marketplace: a directory of Christian-owned businesses
where members find a business, message it, book a meeting, ask for a quote or request a
product, and the business confirms or declines. Jubilee does not take payment; members pay the
business directly.

**Sources.** These requirements are derived from the HTML mocks in `docs/mocks/` (39 pages,
21 dialogs, 7 notification types, 271 states, see `docs/mocks/manifest.json`) and the Magic
Patterns design reference those mocks were built from. Where the mocks show a rule (a limit, a
time window, a message), the requirement states it exactly. Where the mocks are silent on a
production concern (security, performance, operations), the requirement sets a production-grade
default.

**Roles.**

| Role | Who |
|---|---|
| Visitor | Anyone not signed in. |
| Member | A signed-in account holder. Any member can buy. |
| Owner | A member who owns one or more listings: businesses, or spaces they rent out. |
| Renter | A member who asks to rent a space through a space request. |
| Author | A member who wrote a review of a listing whose owner confirmed or completed a request of theirs (L1-031). |
| Jubilee team | Jubilee staff who look after listings and reports, in the admin application (L1-027), with the `moderator` role. |
| Administrator | Jubilee staff with the `admin` role, who hold the administrator-only operations. |

---

## L1-001: Accounts and authentication
The system must let people create a free account, confirm their e-mail address, sign in and out
with an e-mail and password or with Google, recover a forgotten password, and keep a signed-in
session that expires safely.

## L1-002: Member profile and account management
The system must let a member view and change their profile, choose which e-mails they receive,
download their data, and permanently delete their account.

## L1-003: Directory discovery
The system must let visitors and members discover businesses and spaces through a public
welcome page, a browse home with featured businesses, products and a shelf of rooms and places to
stay, and category pages with filters (a term filter for spaces), sorting and pagination.

## L1-004: Search
The system must let anyone search businesses, services, products and spaces by keyword and see grouped,
explained results, with help when nothing matches.

## L1-005: Business, shop and product pages
The system must present each live listing as a public listing page (a service business, a
shop or a space), each product as a detail page, and let people share or report a listing.

## L1-006: Saved list
The system must let a member save businesses and products to a private list and find them again.

## L1-007: Member requests (bookings, quotes and orders)
The system must let a member send a business a booking request, a quote request or an order
request, and the owner of a space a space request, without paying, follow each request through its lifecycle, and cancel an open request.

## L1-008: Business request handling
The system must let an owner see the requests customers send to their business and confirm or
decline each one, with the customer told of the outcome.

## L1-009: Messaging
The system must provide private one-to-one conversations between a member and a business, with
an inbox, search, delivery status and retry.

## L1-010: Notifications
The system must tell members and owners about events that matter to them through an in-app
notification centre, transient toasts, persistent banners and e-mail, according to their
preferences.

## L1-011: Business onboarding
The system must let a member list a business or a space for free by describing what it offers,
agreeing to the statement of faith, and publishing it, live straight away, with the ability to save
a draft.

## L1-012: Church reference verification (removed)
Removed. Jubilee no longer asks an owner for a church reference (ADR 0041), and since ADR 0057
no listing waits for verification. The number is not reused.

## L1-013: Owner listing management
The system must give owners a workspace to monitor their listing, edit listing details (a space's rental
details included), manage products and services, and pause or resume the listing.

## L1-014: Jubilee team administration and moderation
The system must give the Jubilee team a list of published listings, a screen for each with the
ability to take a listing down when it misrepresents a business, a way to handle reports about
listings, and the choice of featured businesses. Jubilee does not verify listings before they go
live (ADR 0057). These screens live in the admin application (L1-027), not in the app.

## L1-015: Trust, policy and help content
The system must publish the statement of faith, terms of use, privacy notice and help content,
and never claim that a listing is verified (ADR 0057).

## L1-016: System pages and error handling
The system must handle missing pages, forbidden access, server errors, offline use, expired
sessions and planned maintenance with clear pages that never lose the user's work and that carry
a support reference.

## L1-017: Connectivity resilience and offline use
The system must keep working usefully on poor or no connection: show cached content, queue
outgoing messages, tell the user about connection state, and recover automatically.

## L1-018: Consistent interaction states and feedback
Every page, dialog and form must present the same well-defined states (loading, empty, error,
invalid, busy, success and the screen's domain states) with the same behaviour, so the product
feels predictable and no action is lost or duplicated.

## L1-019: Security
The system must protect accounts, data and operations against the OWASP Top 10 and abuse,
enforce role-based authorization on every request, and validate all input on the server. Staff
operations answer only a staff session, signed in through single sign-on with a second factor,
from an allowed network (L1-027).

## L1-020: Privacy and data protection
The system must collect only the data the marketplace needs, protect it in transit and at rest,
keep a member's home church private, never sell data, and comply with Canadian privacy
law (PIPEDA).

## L1-021: Performance and scalability
The system must be fast on mid-range phones and mobile networks and must scale beyond the launch
directory to thousands of businesses and members without degrading.

## L1-022: Accessibility
The system must conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA and be fully usable by keyboard and screen reader.

## L1-023: Responsive design
Every screen must work across all viewport sizes: extra small (<576px), small (>=576px), medium
(>=768px), large (>=992px) and extra large (>=1200px), with no horizontal scrolling and no lost
functionality.

## L1-024: Visual design system and theming
The application must implement the Jubilee design system from the mocks (tokens, components,
light and dark themes) consistently, with all colours, sizes and durations coming from tokens.

## L1-025: Reliability, observability and operations
The system must be highly available, observable, recoverable from failure and safely deployable,
with auditable records of sensitive actions.

## L1-026: Marketing site
The system must present Jubilee to people who do not use it yet on a public marketing site,
separate from the app: one page that says what Jubilee is and why to trust it, plays the pitch
film, and sends members to the app and owners to list a business. Until Jubilee opens, the
page is in its launching-soon state (L1-030) and sends nobody to the app.

## L1-027: Admin application
The system must give Jubilee staff their own application, the admin application, at
`https://admin.jubilee.to`: separate from the app and the marketing site, used only by the
Jubilee team and administrators, and holding the listings, moderation and featured screens. Staff
sign in to it through single sign-on with a second factor, never with a password; a staff session
is separate from a member session; and the admin application and the staff operations of the API
answer only callers on an allowed network.
## L1-028: Rooms and rentals
The system must let an owner list a space (a room, suite, apartment, house or hall) in the
category "Rooms & rentals" under one of four terms (Short stay, Long term, Room, Roommate), let
visitors and members find spaces and filter them by term, and let a member send the space
request that fits its term, which the owner confirms or declines. Jubilee takes no payment, and
a space's exact address reaches only the member whose request the owner accepted.

## L1-029: Member feedback
The system must let a member tell Jubilee how the app is going from anywhere in it: a rating, a
bug report or kudos in a few seconds from a launcher on every screen, one question right after
sending a request or submitting a listing, and a feedback page for the full story. A member sees
their own past feedback with its status and any reply from the team, and nobody else's.

## L1-030: Launching soon
**Traces to:** L0-001

Before Jubilee opens, the marketing site (L1-026) must say that Jubilee is launching soon, and
must not say or imply that anyone can browse, message or book today. It must let an owner send
their business's details, so that the Jubilee team can arrange for the listing to be on Jubilee
on launch day, and let anyone leave an e-mail address to be told when Jubilee launches. While
the site is in this state no element of it sends a visitor to the app. What is collected is
personal data, collected with express consent and kept only as long as it is needed (L1-020).

## L1-031: Reviews
**Traces to:** L0-001

The system must let a member whose request the owner of a business, shop or space confirmed or
completed write one honest review of that listing: a rating of 1 to 5 stars, the product, service
or stay it is about, and what happened. Everyone sees a listing's rating and reviews on its page
and its average on its cards. The author can edit or remove their review, the owner can answer it
once in public, and the Jubilee team can take down a review that breaks the review rules,
telling the author why. A review is shown under the author's first name and last initial and
nothing else of them. Reviews serve the mission by keeping Jubilee's measures honest ("A false
balance is an abomination to Yahweh, but accurate weights are his delight." Proverbs 11:1):
members choose a business on what others truly found, and a business's good name (Proverbs 22:1)
is earned in the open. Nothing in Jubilee asks a member for a good review or promises a business a
good rating, and a review is never tied to a payment, which Jubilee does not take.
