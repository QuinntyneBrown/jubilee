# L0 - Mission

Jubilee has one level-0 (L0) requirement: its mission. It is the motive of everything Jubilee
builds, writes and decides.

## L0-001: Mission

> Abiding in Jesus and for love of Him, Jubilee equips Christian business people in Toronto to
> prosper with purpose and resource His Kingdom.

Quote the mission word for word; do not paraphrase or shorten it.

**How everything points back to it.**

- Every level-1 requirement in [`L1.md`](L1.md) traces to L0-001, every level-2 requirement in
  [`L2.md`](L2.md) refines a level-1 requirement, and every detailed design, mock, journey and
  test traces to a level-2 requirement. Nothing is built that cannot be traced back to the
  mission.
- A proposed feature, requirement or decision that does not serve the mission is not added. One
  that works against it is removed.
- Where two ways of serving a requirement are open, choose the one that serves the mission
  better, and say so in the design or the ADR.

**Reading the mission.**

The passages of Scripture each phrase rests on are in [`../scripture.md`](../scripture.md).

| Phrase | What it asks of Jubilee |
|---|---|
| Abiding in Jesus and for love of Him | The reason comes before the product. Jubilee is honest, trustworthy and generous in what it builds and how it speaks, because of whom it serves. |
| equips Christian business people in Toronto | Jubilee serves Christian business people, and the members who look for them, in Toronto. It gives them tools; it does not stand between them. |
| to prosper with purpose | The businesses are meant to do well, and for a reason beyond themselves. |
| and resource His Kingdom | What is gained is for the Kingdom: the church, the community and the people it serves. |
