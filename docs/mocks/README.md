# Jubilee mocks

Static HTML mocks for every page, dialog and notification of Jubilee, Toronto's Christian
marketplace, in every state. They are the design reference for the Angular frontend: the app
keeps the mocks' BEM classes and accessible state semantics.

- **97 screens, 555 mocks:** 53 pages (343 states), 36 dialogs (174 states), 8 notifications (38 states).
- Every mock is responsive (360, 768 and 1280 px), themed light and dark through tokens, and
  keyboard-accessible.
- **23 e-mails** in [`emails/`](emails/index.html) (L2-179, ADR 0038): each as the HTML a mail
  client shows at 600 px, a dark rendition and its plain-text part. They are built from the layout
  the backend embeds by `node tools/email-mocks/build.mjs`; change the layout or the script, not
  the files. They are not screens, so they are not in `manifest.json` or the matrix below.

- **The static server error and maintenance pages** (`frontend/projects/jubilee/public/static/`, L2-115 AC3,
  L2-117 AC1, ADR 0056) are built from `pages/server-error/default.html` and
  `pages/maintenance/default.html` by `node tools/static-pages/build.mjs`: change the mock or the
  script, then run it.

- **`assets/og-image.html`** draws `og-image.png`, the 1200 × 630 link-preview image of every shared
  listing (L2-038 AC4, ADR 0055), from the tokens and the lockup. Screenshot it at 1200 × 630 and
  copy the result to `frontend/projects/jubilee/public/og-image.png`.

- **A listing's address** (L1-032, ADR 0071) is drawn where the owner sees it: "Your address" on the
  publish success states of `pages/list-business/`, on the "Your listing" card of
  `pages/my-business/` (with its note on the paused `statement-lapsed` state), as a read-only field
  in `pages/edit-listing/`, and as the link of `dialogs/share-listing/`. The Magic Patterns design
  does not draw it.

- **The marketing page is in its launching-soon state** (L1-030, ADR 0044): `pages/marketing-home/`
  shows "Launching soon", the business pre-registration form and the launch notification form in
  every state (`business-*`, `notify-*`), and has no link to the app. The Magic Patterns design
  (`docs/design-reference/magic-patterns/`) has no launching-soon frame, so these states come from
  the mocks alone until a frame exists. The fourteen states are one page with one form changed;
  change all of them together.

## How to open

Open [index.html](index.html) from disk; no server or network is needed.

- The bar at the bottom of each mock links its sibling states and toggles the theme (or press `t`).
- `?theme=dark` or `?theme=light` forces a theme; `?chrome=0` hides the mock bar and notes so the
  page looks exactly like the product.

## Shared assets

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `assets/tokens.css` | Design tokens: ink and cream neutrals, gold accent (always with ink text), green for links and success; Archivo for headings, Inter for text; light and dark themes. The only place colours, sizes and durations live. |
| `assets/ui.css` | The component kit shared by every mock. The Jubilee components (top bar, phone tab bar, hero band, media tiles, business and product cards, directory rows, inbox, timeline) are in the second half. Extend it; never fork per mock. |
| `../design-system/assets/marketing.css` | The marketing site's stylesheet (`pages/marketing-home`): the `mkt-*` blocks, light only. It holds the launching-soon blocks too: `mkt-badge`, `mkt-form`, `mkt-confirmation` and the busy `mkt-btn`. Its photos are in `docs/assets/photos/` and it bundles Inter, so it opens from disk. |
| `assets/mock.css`, `assets/mock.js` | The mock bar, annotation notes and theme switch. Not product UI. |

Both themes pass the design-system contrast checker (WCAG AA). Gold on cream is 1.6:1, so gold
controls carry a darker edge (`--color-accent-border`) and gold is never used for text.

## Open questions and assumptions

- **The admin application (L2-188, L2-272 – L2-284) is drawn from the Magic Patterns design
  "Jubilee Admin"** (Magic OS prototype with the frames Overview, Records, Record, the jump palette
  and the phone drawer). It is schema-driven, and so are its mocks: `admin-overview`,
  `admin-records` and `admin-record` are **generated** by `node tools/admin-mocks/build.mjs` from
  the design's 51 tables (`tools/admin-mocks/entities.mjs`, ported word for word with their sample
  rows). Change the script and run it again; never edit the generated files by hand. It also
  rewraps the dialogs `take-down-listing`, `report-action` and `publish-policy` over the record
  they open from. Staff sign-in (`admin-sign-in`) and the moved page (`admin-moved`) are still
  drawn by hand. The design's rows keep its own dates (2025) and staff, not today's cast.
- **What the admin design adds that Jubilee has not decided (each a decision to confirm before it is built):**
  1. *Listing submissions* (approve or reject) and the listing status "in review" put back a review
     before publishing, which ADR 0057 removed. The tables are mocked; L2-276 AC1 shows them only
     once a requirement exists.
  2. *Access requests* and an invite-only beta do not match the launching-soon model (L1-030).
  3. *Staff* holds the roles owner, admin, moderator, support and read only, and looks editable;
     L2-193 keeps staff accounts in the CLI with `moderator` and `admin`. L2-284 follows L2-193:
     the Staff table is read-only and the five roles need an ADR.
  4. *Consent records* say they are never edited, yet the design offers Edit and Delete on them;
     L2-281 AC5 keeps them (and Statement agreements and Audit records) from deletion.
  5. *Messages* are open to staff in the design; L2-284 AC3 opens one only while its conversation
     has an open report (L2-063 AC3, L2-150), so `admin-record/messages` shows a reported message.
  6. *Link tokens* imply sign-in by e-mailed link, which Jubilee does not have.
  7. *Members* show a church; L1-020 keeps a member's home church private. The field should leave
     the table or be shown only with a reason.
  8. *Delete* is offered on any record; L2-281 keeps a deleted record for 30 days and audits it,
     and retention rules (L2-148) may forbid deleting more.

No `docs/specs` exist yet, so the screen list was derived from the product description and the
Magic Patterns design reference. These assumptions shaped it; each one is a decision to confirm.

1. **No payment on Jubilee.** Buying is a request: a member books a service, asks for a quote or
   requests a product, and the business confirms or declines. The member pays the business
   directly. If Jubilee should take payment, a cart, checkout, orders and payouts need adding.
2. **Owners publish their own listings; nobody verifies them first (ADR 0057).** A listing is
   live as soon as its owner publishes it, and no page carries a "Verified" mark. The Jubilee
   team looks through published listings in the admin application (`admin-records/listings`,
   `admin-record/listings`) and takes down any listing that misrepresents a business.
3. **Anyone can join and buy; only listing a business requires the statement of faith.** The
   statement-of-faith, terms and privacy wording is draft copy for the owner to replace.
4. **A listing can hold both products and services.** The form asks for the main one first.
5. **Ten categories from the design reference, and two the product added** ("Rooms & rentals",
   ADR 0042, and "Software platforms", ADR 0072). Photographers, speakers, consultants and
   furniture makers fit under Media, Learning, Business & tech and Shops; say if they should have
   their own categories.
6. **Photography is represented by tinted icon tiles** (`.media`) so the mocks open from disk
   with no network. Real photos replace them in the app.
7. **Archivo is bundled; Inter falls back to system fonts.** Headings and the wordmark load
   Archivo from `docs/design-system/assets/fonts/`, so they match the design reference from disk.
   Body text uses Inter only where it is installed, because mocks do not load web fonts.
8. **No map** in this set. Reviews came with ADR 0066 and the e-mails with ADR 0038.
9. **Buyer screens show Joanna before she owns a listing** ("List your business" in the top bar);
   seller screens show Naomi ("My business").
10. **A software platform has no frame in the design reference (ADR 0072).** Its screens are drawn
    with the components that exist: the business card and the business page's blocks, the reviews
    blocks, the choice cards, the checkbox and the form fields. They add no component block and no
    rule to the shared stylesheet. The four cards of "What do you offer?" use the two-column
    `choice-cards` (two rows of two) in place of `choice-cards--3`.
