Privacy

We collect only what the marketplace needs to work, and we never sell it.

What we keep

  • Your name, e-mail, and optional phone, neighbourhood and home church name and address. Your home church is shown to no one.
  • Messages and requests you send, so both sides can see them.
  • For businesses: listing details.

Your choices

Change what we e-mail you in notification settings, and download or delete your account from your account.

Privacy contact and your rights

To see or correct the personal information we hold about you, write to our privacy contact at privacy@jubilee.example. We answer within 30 days, as Canada’s privacy law (PIPEDA) requires.
