# Estimates: onboarding

| Design | Effort | Complete |
|---|---|---|
| describe-listing | 3 days | 85% |
| describe-space | 5 days | 100% |
| save-draft | 5 days | 90% |
| submit-for-verification | 4 days | 85% |

Done in journey 10 (ADR 0018): drafts and their expiry, the form with its live preview, the item dialog, submission, and the statement of faith page. Since ADR 0041 a submitted listing goes straight to "Ready to review".
Deferred: re-agreement on a new statement version (L2-075 AC3), the full product and service editors (journey 16), the "Members can" action and the highlight field.

describe-space (ADR 0042): List a space with its terms, fields, checks and live preview, its draft and submission, and the verification review of a space are done with tests. Excludes deferred work (see ADR 0042): hourly rent and the sign-up intents.
