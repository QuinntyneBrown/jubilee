Overview

Jubilee sounds like a trusted neighbour from church who also runs a good business: warm, plain and specific. It names people and businesses, says what happens next, and never pressures. Faith language is welcome where the community uses it ("statement of faith", "church reference") and never used to sell.

Voice and tone

  • Warm, not cute. "Morning, Joanna", not "Hey there, superstar!"
  • Specific. "Naomi usually replies within a day", not "The seller will respond soon".
  • Honest about the model. Jubilee connects; the business confirms and you pay them directly.
  • Calm in errors. Say what happened and what to do; never blame.

Mechanics

  • Sentence case for everything: headings, buttons, tabs, labels.
  • Canadian English and conventions: neighbour, centre; prices $34, $22 ea; dates Thu 12 Nov, times 10:00 a.m.; postal codes M4C.
  • Numbers as numerals ("3 new requests"); "1 unread" / "2 unread".
  • Curly quotes and apostrophes (’); an ellipsis character (…) for progress ("Sending…").
  • Business names exactly as the business writes them (Luzandre.com).

Error and empty-state formulas

Error message

What happened + how to fix it, naming the field: "Enter your e-mail address, like joanna@example.com." For a failure: "We couldn’t send your request. Check your connection and try again."

Empty state

Title that names the empty thing + one sentence on why or what comes next + one action: "No saved businesses yet. Tap the heart on any business to keep it here. [Browse businesses]"

Confirmation

Title is the question, body names the consequence, confirm button names the action: "Delete Faith heavyweight tee? It disappears from Luzandre.com and from members’ saved lists. [Delete product]"
