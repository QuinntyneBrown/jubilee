Elevation

Four shadow levels, a surface ladder that lightens as it rises in dark, and nine named z-layers.

WCAG 2.2 AA

  • Shadow levels

Overview

Four shadow levels and a focus shadow. Jubilee is mostly flat: cards sit at level 1 with a hairline border, hover lifts to 2, menus and toasts float at 3, dialogs and sheets at 4.

Surface hierarchy

In light, surfaces are white on cream and shadows carry depth. In dark, shadows are nearly invisible on ink, so each level also lightens its surface: canvas (ink-950) → surface (ink-900) → raised (ink-800). A dialog reads as raised because of its surface colour, not its shadow.

Z-order

LayerTokenUsed by
--z-basepage content
--z-raisedfocused button in a group
--z-stickytop bar, tab bar, table headers
--z-dropdownmenus
--z-overlaymodal backdrop
--z-modaldialogs, drawers
--z-popoverpopovers
--z-toasttoast region
--z-tooltiptooltips, focused skip link

A component never writes a raw z-index; it picks a layer token. Toasts sit above dialogs so a failure toast stays visible while a dialog is open.

Usage

  • Pair a shadow with a border on surfaces that sit on cream; shadows alone vanish in dark.
  • Do not stack shadows on nested surfaces; only the outermost floats.
  • The modal backdrop is --color-bg-backdrop (ink at 60% light, black at 70% dark).
