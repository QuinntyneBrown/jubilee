Foundation

Shape

Pills for actions, growing radii for containers, three border widths and the fixed sizes for avatars, badges and dots.

WCAG 2.2 AA
  • Overview
  • Radius scale
  • Border widths
  • Fixed sizes
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

Jubilee is round. Actions and selection controls are pills (--radius-full): buttons, chips, badges, search, avatars. Containers grow their radius with their size: inputs and menus 10px, cards, media tiles and dialogs 16px, sheets and the CTA band 20px.

Usage

  • A nested element's radius is the parent's minus the gap between them; the scale steps are chosen so this works with --space-1–--space-2 insets.
  • Do not mix pill and square actions in one row.
