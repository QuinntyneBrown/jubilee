Merchandise head

The head of a piece of Jubilee merchandise: the kicker, the name with its one accent word, the price with its currency, and what it is.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newmerch-headL2-263

Overview

  • At the top of the purchase column. The name is the page’s h1.
  • The price is the server’s, in whole dollars when it has no cents.

Anatomy

  1. Kicker“Jubilee tee · Black”.
  2. Titleh1, Archivo bold at 110% width, 36 px and 48 px from 640 px; one accent word in --color-fg-accent.
  3. Price“$25” and “CAD” in the muted colour.
  4. TextWhat it is, in a short paragraph.

Variants

Jubilee tee · Black

Prosper with purpose.

$25 CAD

Heavyweight black cotton with the line from our business card across the chest, “purpose” in gold. The J mark and jubilee.to sit on the upper back, so the conversation can start from behind you in line.

The Purpose Tee (the title is the page’s h1).

Sizes

One size; the title grows at 640 px.

States

None: it is read, not used.

Responsive behaviour

The title balances its lines and wraps on a phone (“Prosper with / purpose.”).

Theming

The accent word is green in light and gold in dark (--color-fg-accent).

Accessibility

The accent is a span inside the h1: the heading reads as one sentence. Colour carries no meaning here.

Content

  • The price is the shirt’s price before tax; the page says “Tax is added at checkout.” once, under the button.
  • No promise of prosperity: the title is the print, not a claim (docs/scripture.md).
