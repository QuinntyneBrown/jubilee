Launch form

The launch form is the panel that holds a form on the marketing page: a place for a notice, the fields, one action and a line of small print. It holds the business pre-registration form and the launch notification form.

Overview

Use it for the two forms of the launching-soon page and nowhere else. Each is a section of the page (#pre-register, #notify), not a dialog: the form is what the page is for, and a link must be able to land on it (ADR 0044).

  • The panel adds no control of its own. Its fields are the existing Form field, Text field, Select, Textarea and Checkbox; its notice is an Alert or the error summary; its action is a Marketing button.
  • Once a form is sent, the Launch confirmation takes its place.
  • Sending a form creates no account and lists nothing, and the page says so beside the form.
  • The rules every Jubilee form follows (labels, messages, the summary, busy) are in Forms; this page says only what the panel adds.

Anatomy

  1. Notice.mkt-form__notice: an alert, or the error summary when the form is invalid. Left out when there is nothing to say; an empty one takes no space.
  2. Fields.mkt-form__fields: the fields in one column, --space-5 apart. A .form-grid.form-grid--2 row puts two short fields side by side from 640px. The consent checkbox is the last field.
  3. Actions.mkt-form__actions: one Marketing button, type="submit", at the start of the row.
  4. Note.mkt-form__note: one line of small print saying what the details are used for, 13px in --color-fg-subtle.
  5. Panel.mkt-form: the <form> itself, named with aria-label. --radius-lg, a hairline ring drawn as a shadow, --space-5 of padding.

Sizes

One size. The panel fills its column; its padding steps once, at 640px (see Responsive behaviour).

States

The panel itself does not change; a state is what its notice, its fields and its button show. The launch notification form is drawn for each state; the business pre-registration form behaves the same and is drawn where it differs.

  • The form fits at 360, 576, 768, 992 and 1280px without sideways scrolling (L2-215 AC8). Every grid child may shrink (min-width: 0), and a long e-mail address scrolls inside its field.
  • A field that needs room keeps a full row at every width: “Business name”, “About your business” and the consent checkbox.
  • The button does not stretch. It stays at the start of the actions row.
  • A link that lands on the section stops with the heading clear of the sticky top bar (scroll-margin-top on every .mkt [id]).

Theming

The marketing site is light only: marketing.css defines no dark values and the page sets data-theme="light" on <body class="mkt">. Every example on this page stands in an element with class="mkt" and data-theme="light", so it looks the same whichever theme these docs are in.

The paper tone exists for contrast with the section, not as decoration: a white panel on the white section would have only its ring to mark it. Fields stay white (--color-bg-surface) in both tones; read-only fields are --color-bg-surface-sunken.

Accessibility

Role and name

A native <form novalidate> named with aria-label: “Business pre-registration” or “Launch notification”. With a name it is a form landmark, so the two can be told apart and reached directly. novalidate turns the browser’s bubbles off; Jubilee’s own messages replace them and the required and maxlength attributes stay for assistive technology.

Labels

Every field has a visible <label for>. Whether it is needed is written in the label, “(required)” or “(optional)”, never shown by an asterisk or by colour. Hints and messages are tied to their field with aria-describedby. A placeholder is an example, never the label.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the summary’s links (when shown), the fields in reading order, the checkbox and the button.
SpaceTicks or clears the checkbox.
EnterIn a text field or on the button, sends the form. On a summary link, moves focus to that field.

Focus

  • The shared 2px ring on every field, link and the button.
  • Invalid: focus moves to the summary (tabindex="-1", role="alert").
  • Rate limited, challenge failed: the alert has role="alert" and is announced; focus stays on the button so that trying again is one press.
  • Still sending: the alert has role="status", so it is announced without interrupting; the form stays busy and nothing asks the visitor to act.
  • Busy: the button is not disabled, so focus is not lost while the form is sent.
  • Sent: focus moves to the confirmation.

Colour is not the carrier

An invalid field has a red border, aria-invalid="true", a warning sign and a sentence. An alert has an icon, and its words say what happened. Busy is a spinner, aria-busy and read-only fields. Links in the summary keep their underline (.mkt form a), since the page’s other links have none.

  • The consent checkbox is never ticked for the reader. It is a full sentence that starts with “Yes” and says how to stop (L2-218, CASL).
  • A field message says what to do and ends with a full stop: “Enter your e-mail address.”, “Tick the box so that we may e-mail you.” The same line in the summary has no full stop.
  • An error says what happened and that nothing is lost. It does not blame the reader and does not show a code.
  • The note says what the details are used for, and nothing more. The page beside the business form says: “Sending this form does not list your business yet.”
  • Nothing in or beside a form says or implies that Jubilee is open, and nothing promises customers, growth or success for sending it. Jubilee never promises prosperity (docs/scripture.md, “What Jubilee never says”).

Do and don't

  • Do use the paper tone on the white section and the surface tone on the paper.
  • Don’t put a second button, a “Cancel” or a “Reset” in the actions. There is nothing to cancel.
  • Don’t open either form in a dialog, and don’t add a third form to the page.
  • Don’t disable the button until the form is valid. Let it be sent, then say what to fix.
