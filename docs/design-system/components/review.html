Review

One review: the author’s initials, first name and last initial, and the date; the stars and what it is about; the text as written; the actions the viewer may take; and the owner’s one public reply, set off by a rule.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newarticle.reviewL2-226L2-229L2-233L2-234L2-236L2-239

Overview

  • An article named by the author’s name.
  • .review--mine is the member’s own: a tinted ground, “Your review” beside the name, “Posted {date}”, Edit and Remove (L2-229 AC7).
  • The text keeps its line breaks (white-space: pre-line) and is never read as markup (L2-136).
  • No photo, no church, no neighbourhood and no Helpful count (ADR 0066).

Anatomy

  1. HeadInitials in .review__avatar, .review__author (h3) and .review__meta (date, “Edited”).
  2. Rating lineSmall Stars, and .review__item.
  3. Text.review__text, at most 42rem wide.
  4. Reply.review__reply: “Response from {owner}” · date, and the text, behind a thick rule.
  5. Actions.review__actions: small buttons.

Sizes

One size; its width is the list’s, the text held to 42rem.

States

The review itself has no state; its buttons follow Button. Offline, Edit, Remove, Reply and Report are disabled and say “Needs a connection. It works again when you’re back online.” (L2-240 AC5).

Responsive behaviour

  • One column at every width; the rating line wraps, the item last.
  • Long names and items break anywhere rather than overflow.

Theming

Initials on --color-bg-inverse; the reply rule --color-border-strong; the own review on --color-accent-subtle, outlined in forced colours.
