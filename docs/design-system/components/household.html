Household

Who lives here: the household of a Room or Roommate space, as the owner wrote it, shown above the host card.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newL2-205 AC2

Overview

Shown only for a Room or a Roommate, and only when the owner wrote something. It is a paragraph: the owner's own few lines (ADR 0042 keeps the household as text, not the design's list of people).

Anatomy

  1. Title.household__title: "Who lives here", --text-h3.
  2. Text.household__text: the household text, ruled above and below.

Variants

Who lives here

Two women in our late 20s, a nurse and a grad student. Looking for a third who’s tidy, friendly and up for a weekly house dinner.

Roommate: who you are, and who you’re looking for.
HTML
<div style="max-width: 24rem; width: 100%;"><section class="household" aria-labelledby="r3-hh"><h2 class="household__title" id="r3-hh">Who lives here</h2><p class="household__text">Two women in our late 20s, a nurse and a grad student. Looking for a third who’s tidy, friendly and up for a weekly house dinner.</p></section></div>

Sizes

One size.

States

Static. Absent for Short stay and Long term, and when the text is empty.

Responsive behaviour

In the page's side column from 1024px; under it, below what's included and the house rules. The text wraps; nothing truncates.

Content

  • First person, a few honest lines: who, ages if they want, pets, rhythms of the house.
  • The form's hint says it: "Renters choose people as much as rooms. A few honest lines go a long way."
