Reviews section

The “Reviews” section of a business, shop, space or product page, after its content and before “More in …”: the heading and “Write a review”, the Rating summary beside the list, the list’s toolbar, the first three reviews and “Show all”.

Overview

  • “Write a review” shows only to an eligible member (L2-226 AC1); a member who is not sees “You can review {name} after they confirm a request of yours.”; the owner sees neither.
  • The list shows the first three of the sort and filter in force; “Show all {n} reviews” shows the rest in place.
  • The member’s own review stands above the toolbar, outside the filter and the sort.
  • Sorting and filtering happen in the app: one request answers every review (ADR 0066).

Anatomy

  1. Head.reviews__head: h2 “Reviews” and the write action.
  2. Summary.reviews__summary, sticky from 768px.
  3. Toolbar.reviews__toolbar: what the filter shows, “Clear” and the “Sort reviews” select.
  4. Listul.reviews__list, a rule between reviews.

Reviews of a software platform

A platform takes no requests, so a review of one rests on the member’s statement of use (L2-258, ADR 0072). With reviews, one reviews__notice under the head says “Reviews here are from members who say they have used {name}. That is their own word. Jubilee does not check it.”; “Write a review” sits in the head as for a business. With no reviews, the empty block says “Used {name}? Your review helps the next family decide.” and carries the same line as a second reviews__empty-text, above “Write a review”. The item of each review is “Their platform”. Nothing in the section carries a “Verified” mark.

Sizes

One size; it takes the page container’s width.

States

The section’s states are its variants: list, filtered, empty, loading and error, and the own review (see Review). The status line (role="status", visually hidden) announces “Showing {n} reviews” when the filter or the sort changes.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 768px: one column, the summary above the toolbar; the toolbar wraps the sort under the filter text when it must.
  • From 768px: a 260px summary column (300px from 1024px) with 48px between; the summary stays in view.
  • The empty state is a column under 640px and a row from 640px.
  • Nothing scrolls sideways at 360px (L2-240 AC1).

Theming

A hairline top rule (--color-border-default) opens the section; the empty state’s rule is --color-accent; the error is the danger Alert.

Accessibility

A section named by its h2; the reviews are articles under h3s, so a screen reader can jump review to review. The sort is a native select named “Sort reviews”. “Show all” moves focus to the first review it added. The loading section is aria-busy with a status “Loading the reviews…”.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabWrite a review, the five bars, Clear, the sort, each review’s actions, Show all.
↑ ↓In the sort, choose the order.

Content

  • Heading “Reviews”; action “Write a review”.
  • “Tap a star bar to filter” / “Showing {n} reviews with {s} stars” and “Clear”; “No {s}-star reviews written up yet.”
  • Sort: “Most relevant” (the last 12 months first, then longer text, then newer), “Newest”, “Highest rated”, “Lowest rated”.
  • “Show all {n} reviews”.
  • Empty: “No reviews yet” and “Worked with {name}? Your review helps the next family decide.” (space “Stayed with this host? …”, product “Bought {product}? …”).
  • Error: “We couldn’t load the reviews.” and “Try again”.
  • No line asks for a good review or says a rating helps or harms the business (L2-227 AC6).
