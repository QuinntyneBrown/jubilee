Footer

The footer closes every browsing page with who Jubilee is and the four pages members look for when they want to trust it: the statement of faith, help, terms and privacy.

Overview

Every page with a top bar ends with the same footer. Auth, error, system pages and the full-height conversation view have none (sign in, sign up, verify email, 500, offline, maintenance, session expired, conversation).

  • Left: "© 2026 Jubilee · Toronto’s Christian marketplace".
  • Right: Statement of faith, Help, Terms, Privacy as plain links in a .cluster.
  • The statement of faith comes first because it is what a member is agreeing to; legal pages follow.

Anatomy

  1. Tagline<span> in --text-body-sm, --color-fg-muted.
  2. Links.cluster of underlined links inheriting the muted colour.
  3. Container.footer: surface, hairline top border, --space-8 vertical and --layout-margin side padding, wrapping flex row.

Variants

One footer. Pages that should not have one leave it out rather than using a smaller variant.

Sizes

One size: --space-8 vertical padding and --text-body-sm.

States

The footer is static; its links have link states, forced with data-state. Links are never disabled.

Responsive behaviour

  • The row wraps: below about 600px the links drop under the tagline, left-aligned.
  • Links wrap within the cluster at 360px; none truncate.
  • With the phone tab bar visible, the body's bottom padding keeps the footer above it.

Theming

Surface, border and muted-text tokens; flips with the theme. The footer is never on the brand band: the ink bar at the top is the only band in the page chrome.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A page-level <footer> (contentinfo landmark), outside <main>. See APG landmark regions. The link group is short enough not to need its own <nav>; if it grows, wrap it in <nav aria-label="Footer">.

Content

  • The tagline is fixed copy: "© 2026 Jubilee · Toronto’s Christian marketplace"; the year updates.
  • Four links, in this order: Statement of faith, Help, Terms, Privacy.
  • No social icons, newsletter forms or sitemaps.
