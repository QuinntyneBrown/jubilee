Segmented choice

One choice of a few, drawn as segments on a sunken track: the fit of the tee. The segments are native radios; the chosen one sits on a raised pill that slides to it, and a note says what it means.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newsegmented-choiceL2-264

Overview

  • Use it for two to four short, equal options where the choice changes what follows (the fit changes the sizes). For longer options use Radio group; for views of one page use Tabs.

Anatomy

  1. Legend“Fit”, the group’s name.
  2. Track--color-bg-surface-sunken, --radius-lg, 4 px inside.
  3. PillRaised (--color-bg-surface-raised, --shadow-1), behind the chosen option.
  4. OptionA label around a hidden native radio.
  5. NoteWhat the chosen option means; the fieldset is described by it.

Sizes

One size: 44 px options.

States

Option: default (muted text), hover and chosen (default text; the pill under the chosen), focus (the focus ring around the option). The pill slides over --duration-base; under reduced motion it moves at once.

Responsive behaviour

The options share the width equally at every size and never wrap; the note wraps.

Theming

Track and pill change with the theme; the chosen option is raised in both.

Both themes

Accessibility

Native radios with one name: Tab enters the group at the chosen option, the arrow keys move and choose. The chosen option is said by the radio’s state, the raised pill and the text colour, never by colour alone; in forced colours it has an outline.

Content

  • Option labels are one or two words (“Youth”, “Women’s”, “Men’s”).
  • The note is one or two sentences about the chosen option.
