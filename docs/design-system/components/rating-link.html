Rating link

Under a listing’s or a product’s name: the stars filled to the average, the average and “{n} reviews”, one link that moves the page to its Reviews section. With no reviews it is the plain line “No reviews yet”.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newrating-linkL2-228L2-231

Overview

  • On the business, shop, space and product pages, straight after the h1.
  • It shows from the first review; the card rule of 3 does not apply to the page itself (ADR 0058).

Anatomy

  1. StarsA Stars, aria-hidden: the text says the rating.
  2. Average.rating-link__average; hidden words make it “Rated 4.6 out of 5,”.
  3. Count.rating-link__count, muted, underlined on hover.

No reviews yet

None: a plain paragraph, not a link.
HTML
<p class="rating-link rating-link--none">No reviews yet</p>

Sizes

One size: small text under the page’s h1, 44px high.

States

VariantDefaultHoverFocus
LinkRated 4.6 out of 5,5 reviewsRated 4.6 out of 5,5 reviewsRated 4.6 out of 5,5 reviews

The focus ring is the global link ring. The target is 44px high (--target-comfortable).

Responsive behaviour

It wraps between the stars and the count under 360px rather than overflow; the target stays 44px high.

Theming

Text --color-fg-default, count --color-fg-muted; the stars follow Stars.
