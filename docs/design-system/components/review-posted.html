Review posted

The posted state of the Write a review screen: a round check, “Thanks, your review is live.”, what happens next, the review in short between two rules, and the two ways on.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newreview-postedL2-228
  • Overview

  • Variants
  • Sizes

  • Responsive behaviour

Overview

  • The heading is the page’s h1 and takes focus when the form gives way to it (L2-228 AC4).
  • The text is cut to three lines; the whole review is on the listing page.

Anatomy

  1. Mark.review-posted__mark: 56px, --color-success-solid, fades in.
  2. Title and text.review-posted__title (h1) and .review-posted__text.
  3. Summary.review-posted__summary: stars and word, “{item} · {name}”, three lines of text.
  4. Actions.review-posted__actions.

Sizes

One size, at most 36rem wide; the title steps up from 768px.

States

None: it is the success state of the screen.

Responsive behaviour

  • At most 36rem wide; the title is 3xl, 4xl from 768px.
  • The actions wrap under each other at 360px.

Theming

The mark is --color-success-solid with --color-fg-inverse; the summary’s rules --color-border-default.

Accessibility

The h1 has tabindex="-1" and receives focus; the stars are named “5 out of 5 stars” and the word is visible. The mark is decorative. Under reduced motion it does not fade.
