Message thread

Messages is where members and businesses talk: a list of threads on the left, the open conversation on the right, and a composer at the bottom. On phones the list and the conversation are two screens.

Overview

In the app these parts are jb-message-bubble, jb-request-card and jb-attach-photo in the components library; the conversation's "More" menu is documented under Menu.

Use these parts only for one-to-one conversations between a member and a business. System events (a confirmed booking, a new listing) go to notifications; a booking, quote or order request made between the two is shown in the conversation as a .request-card, which says its status and links to the request.

  • Inbox (.inbox): a framed two-pane layout from 1024px (22rem list + chat); below that, .inbox--list shows only the list and .inbox--chat only the conversation.
  • Thread (.thread): avatar, name, time and a one-line snippet. .thread--unread bolds it and adds a gold dot; aria-current="page" marks the open one.
  • Chat (.chat): a head with back, avatar, name, reply-time hint and a link to the listing; a .chat__log list with day separators; the composer.
  • Bubble (.bubble): theirs on the left (subtle surface), .bubble--me on the right (inverse), .bubble--pending while sending and .bubble--failed with Try again and Delete. A bubble may hold one photo (.bubble__photo), with or without text.
  • Request card (.request-card): the kind of request, its title, one line of detail, a status Badge and the link "View request". It sits in the log at the time the request was sent and always shows the present status.
  • Composer (.composer): the photo control (.attach), a one-row auto-growing textarea and a gold icon Send button.

Anatomy

  1. Avatar.avatar with the business initials, aria-hidden (the name follows).
  2. Name.thread__name, --text-label, truncated.
  3. Time.thread__time, caption, muted: time today, "Yesterday", weekday + date this week, then date.
  4. Snippet.thread__snippet, one line, muted, prefixed "You:" when the last message is yours.
  5. Current rowaria-current="page": --color-accent-subtle fill.
  6. Unread.thread--unread: bold name and snippet in --color-fg-default, an 8px .thread__dot and hidden "Unread.".

Request card

  1. Card.request-card: a list item of the log, full width up to 28rem, --color-bg-surface, hairline border, --radius-lg, --shadow-1.
  2. Kind.request-card__kind, overline, muted: "Booking request", "Quote request" or "Order request".
  3. Title.request-card__title, semibold; long titles wrap.
  4. Detail.request-card__meta, one muted line: when and how, the date needed by, or pickup and price.
  5. StatusA .badge in .request-card__foot; the word says the status, the colour repeats it.
  6. Link"View request", at the end of the foot; it wraps under the badge when the card is narrow.

Photo in a bubble

  1. Photo.bubble__photo: a button named "View photo" holding one thumbnail, 16rem wide, cropped to 4:3, --radius-md. It comes first in the bubble.
  2. TextOptional. A message with a photo needs no text.
  3. Meta.bubble__meta: the time, "Sending…" or the failure line, as for any bubble.

Composer with a photo

  1. Preview.attach__preview: the chosen photo as a square the height of the textarea (--control-height-md), in place of the "Attach a photo" button.
  2. Remove.attach__remove: a round 24px button, "Remove photo", on the preview's top corner.
  3. SendSends the photo with the text, or the photo alone.

Variants

The inbox on a wide screen, with the failed-send state open. Resize below 1024px and the list disappears in favour of the conversation (.inbox--chat).

  • Sending: the message appears at once (optimistic), the composer clears, the Send button sets aria-busy, and "Sending…" is replaced by the time when the server confirms, with no layout shift.
  • Failed: the bubble switches to the danger surface and its meta line says what happened and offers Try again and Delete. The alert sits on the meta line, not on the list item.
  • Loading: thread skeletons in the list; bubble-shaped skeletons in the log; the composer stays usable. Empty conversation: a compact Empty state ("Say hello to Trevor") with suggested-question chips. Error: an error empty state in the chat pane with Try again.
  • Photo chosen: the preview replaces "Attach a photo" until the message is sent or "Remove photo" is pressed; Send is available with no text. Photo on its way: the pending bubble shows the photo and "Sending…"; if it fails, the failed bubble keeps the photo and offers Try again. A photo that cannot be read or made small enough never reaches the composer: an error Toast says so.
  • Photo unavailable: when the thumbnail fails to load the bubble asks once for a new link; if that fails too, .bubble__photo--missing takes its place. It is not a button.
  • Request status: a card shows the status the request has now, not the one it had when it was sent; when the status changes while the conversation is open the badge changes in place.
  • New message: a message that arrives while the conversation is open is added to the log and announced ("New message from Trevor"); no toast is shown for the open conversation.
  • Disabled composer is reserved for a business that has left Jubilee; a line above it explains, and the thread stays readable.

Responsive behaviour

  • From 1024px the inbox is two columns: list 22rem with a hairline right edge, chat the rest. Both panes share the 32rem minimum height.
  • Below 1024px only one pane shows: .inbox--list on Messages, .inbox--chat on a conversation, whose head gains the Back button.
  • Bubbles cap at 85% of the log width on phones and 32rem on desktop; long words and links break (overflow-wrap: anywhere).
  • A photo is 16rem wide and never wider than its bubble (max-width: 100%); on a 360px phone it fills the bubble and keeps its 4:3 crop.
  • A request card stretches across the log up to 28rem. Its foot wraps: on a narrow card "View request" moves under the badge.
  • The photo control does not shrink (flex: none); the textarea takes the width that is left.
  • The composer stays at the bottom of the chat pane; on phones it sits above the tab bar.

Theming

Mine uses the inverse pair (--color-bg-inverse / --color-fg-inverse): ink on cream in light, cream on ink in dark, so your messages always have the strongest contrast. Theirs uses --color-bg-subtle. Failed uses the danger surface trio. The current thread and the unread dot use the gold-subtle and gold-edge tokens. The request card is a surface (--color-bg-surface, hairline border, --shadow-1) so it reads as a document and not as something either person said; its badge takes the status pairs of the theme. The dashed frame and text of an unavailable photo use currentColor, so they follow the bubble they are in. "Remove photo" is a raised surface with the default foreground in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The thread list is a <ul> of links with aria-current="page" on the open one (APG link pattern). The chat log is a labelled <ul aria-label="Messages">; in product give it role="log" so new incoming messages are announced politely. The composer is a <form> with a labelled textarea and a submit button. A photo is a <button> around an <img>; "Attach a photo" and "Remove photo" are buttons. A request card is a list item of the log holding text, a badge and a link. A message that arrives while the conversation is open is announced by a polite live region outside the log (role="status", visually hidden: "New message from Trevor").

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabList: thread to thread. Chat: Back, View listing, More, any link or button in the log (View photo, View request, Try again, Delete), then Attach a photo (or Remove photo), the textarea and Send.
EnterOn a thread: opens it. In the textarea: a new line (the textarea grows).
Ctrl/⌘+EnterSends from the textarea. The Send button is always there too.
Enter / SpaceActivates Send, Try again, Delete, View photo, Attach a photo and Remove photo. Attach a photo opens the device's file chooser.

Focus

Opening a thread on a phone moves focus to the chat heading. After sending, focus stays in the textarea. After Delete on a failed message, focus moves to the textarea. After a photo is chosen, focus moves to "Remove photo"; after it is removed, back to "Attach a photo". The shared ring appears on threads, buttons, the photo and the textarea.

Labelling

Each bubble starts with a visually hidden sender ("Trevor: ", "You: "). Unread threads add a hidden "Unread." before the snippet; the gold dot is aria-hidden. The textarea is labelled "Message Legacy Financial"; the send button is "Send". The pending meta is a role="status" ("Sending…"), the failed meta a role="alert".

The photo button is named "View photo" (aria-label); its image has the alt text "Photo from {name}", "Photo from Trevor" or "Photo from you", so the log says whose photo it is. The preview in the composer has the alt text "Photo to send". The icon buttons are named "Attach a photo" and "Remove photo"; their icons are aria-hidden. An unavailable photo is the text "Photo unavailable", not an image. A request's status is always a word in the badge ("Awaiting reply", "Confirmed", "Declined", "Cancelled", "Completed"); the colour only repeats it.

Motion

No animation on bubbles; new messages appear in place. The busy Send button shows the spinner (--duration-spin), which keeps turning under reduced motion because it is status.

Touch

Thread rows are at least 64px tall and full width. Send is 44px. Try again and Delete in the failed meta are inline text buttons with a 24px minimum height (--target-min), separated by a dot and space. "Attach a photo" is a 44px icon button. "Remove photo" is 24px by 24px (--target-min), the WCAG 2.2 minimum target; it overhangs the preview's corner by --space-2, clear of the textarea and Send, so no other target falls inside its 24px circle. The photo in a bubble is a target of up to 16rem by 12rem.

Content

  • Snippets are the last message, one line; prefix "You: " when it is yours, the sender's first name when the business has several people ("Trevor: ").
  • Times: "9:14 a.m." today, "Yesterday", "Sat 7 Nov" this week, "28 Oct" otherwise. Day separators: "Today · Tue 10 Nov".
  • Failure copy says what happened and what to do: "Not sent. Check your connection." Then the actions. Never "Error".
  • The head hint sets expectations in plain words: "usually replies within a day".
  • Placeholder is "Write a message"; it is a hint, not the label.
  • Photo labels are fixed: "Attach a photo", "Remove photo", "View photo", "Photo unavailable". Alt text is "Photo from {name}" in a bubble and "Photo to send" in the composer. A photo on its way says "Sending…" like any message. A message that is only a photo has the snippet "Photo" in the thread list.
  • A request card's kind is "Booking request", "Quote request" or "Order request"; its title is the request's own title; its detail is one line joined with " · " ("Thu 12 Nov, 10:00–10:30 a.m. · Video", "Needed by Fri 27 Nov", "Pickup · $24.00").
  • Status words are fixed: "Awaiting reply", "Confirmed", "Declined", "Cancelled", "Completed". The link is always "View request".
  • The arrival announcement is "New message from {name}", with the sender's first name.

Added on 11 November 2026 (ADR 0039): the photo in a bubble, the photo control and the request card. The request card replaces .chat__card.card, which borrowed .overline, .row__title and .row__meta and set its foot's margin inline; it is now one block with its own parts. No mock draws .bubble__photo--missing or the "Completed" status; both are documented from the CSS and ADR 0039.

Drift fixed during extraction: the failed bubble puts role="alert" on the <li>, breaking list semantics; the system moves it to .bubble__meta. The chat log is a plain list with no role="log", so incoming messages are silent; the system adds it. The unread dot sits inside the snippet, which is a block with .truncate; as an inline span its width and height do not apply, so the dot can collapse — .thread__dot needs display: inline-block (proposed). Snippets carry inline display: block like directory taglines. Loading bubbles use inline widths (60%, 45%…), and the chat head wraps its title in an inline flex: 1; min-width: 0 div. .bubble--pending (0.7) and .bubble__meta (0.8) use opacity on text, which lowers contrast below the token pairs; keep the meta short and never put essential text only in a pending bubble.
