Host card

Who is renting to you: "Your host", the owner's name and the about text when given.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newL2-205 AC1
  • Overview

  • Variants

Overview

One per space page, in the side column after the household. It names the person, not a business, in their own words.

Anatomy

  1. Label.host-card__label: "Your host" (the section's heading).
  2. Name.host-card__name.
  3. About.host-card__about: left out when empty.

Variants

Your host

Hannah Lee

Without about text.
HTML
<div style="max-width: 24rem; width: 100%;"><section class="host-card" aria-labelledby="hv"><h2 class="host-card__label" id="hv">Your host</h2><p class="host-card__name">Hannah Lee</p></section></div>

Sizes

One size.

States

Static.

Responsive behaviour

Full width of its column; under 1024px it follows the page content.

Content

  • The name as the owner gives it ("Paul & Marta Kowalski").
  • The about text is the owner's; keep it to two or three sentences.
