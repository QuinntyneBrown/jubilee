Stat

A stat is one number that matters to a business owner this week, with what it counts and how it moved: 312 listing views, up 18% on last week. Stats sit at the top of My business in a grid of four.

Overview

Use stats for two to four headline numbers at the top of a dashboard. Each answers "how am I doing?" at a glance and links to the detail when there is one. For a breakdown use a Table; for progress towards a goal use a Progress bar.

  • Stat (.stat): label, value, optional delta. Usually inside a .card.
  • Delta up (.stat__delta--up): success colour, for a change the owner wants.
  • Delta down (.stat__delta--down): danger colour, for a change that needs attention.
  • Neutral note (.stat__delta): muted text that advises rather than measures ("Reply within a day to keep your badge").
  • Stat grid (.stat-grid): two columns on phones, four from 1024px.

Anatomy

  1. Label.stat__label: what is counted and over what period, --text-label in --color-fg-muted.
  2. Value.stat__value: --text-h2 with tabular figures.
  3. Delta.stat__delta: --text-body-sm; an arrow (hidden from assistive tech) plus words that say the change.

Sizes

One size. In a .stat-grid the card padding drops to --space-4 so four fit across. A stat outside a card (in a sidebar) keeps the same type.

States

Stats are static: no hover, focus, active or disabled. If a stat links to its detail, the whole card is a link and takes the link’s focus ring. Data states:

Responsive behaviour

  • .stat-grid: two columns from 360px, four from 1024px (--layout-breakpoint-lg).
  • Labels wrap to two lines at 360px; keep them under about 24 characters. Values never wrap.
  • Four stats is the maximum; on phones an odd count leaves a gap, so use two or four.

Theming

Only semantic tokens: the card surface, --color-fg-muted labels, the default ink value, --color-success-fg and --color-danger-fg deltas. Dark mode swaps them; both deltas stay at 4.5:1 on the surface.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain text in a card; no role. A grid of stats can be a <ul> if order matters, or a <section> with a heading. There is no APG widget; when a stat updates live, announce it through a polite role="status" region (APG landmark regions).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches a stat only when its card is a link.
EnterFollows that link.

Focus

Linked stat cards take the shared focus ring around the card.

Labelling

Reading order is label, value, delta ("Listing views, 7 days, 312, 18% on last week"). Arrows are aria-hidden and the words carry the direction ("up 18%" or "down 9 points"), so colour and arrow are never the only signal.

Motion

Values do not count up. Skeletons shimmer and stop under reduced motion.

Touch

Not interactive unless linked; a linked stat card is far larger than 44px.

Content

  • Labels name the measure and the period: "Listing views, 7 days", "Open requests", "Saved by members".
  • Deltas compare to a named period and use words: "18% on last week", "4 this week", "2 new since yesterday".
  • Round values a person can act on: 312, not 312.4; percentages without decimals.
  • A neutral note gives a next step ("Reply within a day to keep your badge"), not a judgement.
