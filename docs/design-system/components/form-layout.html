Form layout

Form layout arranges fields into something a member can finish: one column of fields, pairs where they belong together, actions at the end, and one summary that lists every problem.

Overview

A form is a .form (column, --space-6 gaps) of fields and fieldsets, ending in .form-actions. Short related pairs share a row in .form-grid--2 from 640px, or in .form-grid--2-lg from 1024px only. On submit with errors, an error summary appears at the top and takes focus. See the Forms pattern for validation timing and flows.

  • Page form: a card on the page, width capped by .container--narrow or a split with a preview (edit listing, list your business).
  • Dialog form: fields in .dialog__body (.stack), actions in the sticky .dialog__footer; see Dialog.
  • Auth form: one column in the card of the Auth layout, with large block buttons. Log in and Sign up sit in its split frame and put the Google button first.
  • Auto-save settings: no actions; each control saves and an inline status reports it.
  • Item list: .item-list for things added inside a form (services, order items) with remove buttons and an Add link.
  • Danger zone: a .card.danger-zone at the end of account settings for irreversible actions.

Anatomy

  1. Error summary.alert.alert--danger.form-summary, role="alert", tabindex="-1"; a 4px danger edge and one link per problem.
  2. Field stack.form: fields --space-6 apart.
  3. Two-column row.form-grid--2 from 640px, or .form-grid--2-lg from 1024px; gap --space-4.
  4. Form actions.form-actions: right-aligned, wraps, status at the start, primary last.

Responsive behaviour

  • .form-grid--2 is one column under 640px and two from 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm).
  • .form-grid--2-lg is one column under 1024px and two from 1024px (--layout-breakpoint-lg). Sign up uses it for Email and Password, which pair only once the form is beside the brand panel.
  • Form actions wrap; when they do, the status takes its own line and buttons keep primary last. In dialogs on phones the footer stays sticky.
  • The edit-listing preview moves under the form below 1024px.
  • Item lists keep the price and remove button on the row's end; the name wraps.

Theming

Layout has no colour of its own. The summary reads the alert tokens (--color-danger-bg, --color-danger-fg, --color-danger-solid edge). The danger zone only swaps the card border to --color-danger-border. Item lists use --color-bg-surface with a strong outer border and default dividers.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <form> with novalidate (Jubilee shows its own messages) and an accessible name when there are several on a page. The error summary follows the APG alert pattern and also receives focus. Fieldsets group related fields; the danger zone is a labelled section.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabThrough fields in visual order, then the actions (secondary before primary).
Enter in a text fieldSubmits the form.
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to that field (the link targets the control's id).
Esc in a dialog formCloses the dialog; unsaved text prompts first.

Focus

On submit with errors, focus moves to the summary (tabindex="-1"), which is announced. After a successful save, focus stays on the primary button and the status is announced. After removing an item from an item list, focus moves to the next item's remove button, or to Add.

Labelling

Summary title counts the problems (“Fix 2 things to continue”); each link repeats the field's error in short. Status lines are role="status". Remove buttons name the item (“Remove Wedding day coverage”).

Motion

The summary appears without animation and the page scrolls to it smoothly unless reduced motion is set (scroll-behavior: auto). The saving dot pulses over --duration-loop and stops under reduced motion.

Touch

Actions are 44px buttons with --space-3 between them. Item-list remove buttons are 32px with the row's padding around them, keeping 24px spacing.

Content

  • Summary title: “Fix N things to continue”. Links: the action, not the field name (“Enter your hours”).
  • Primary action names the result: “Save listing”, “Publish listing”, “Create account”.
  • Standing status explains when changes apply: “Changes are published when you save”.
  • Danger-zone copy says exactly what is lost and who is told.
