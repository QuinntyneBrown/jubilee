Event feature card

The first matching event in start order, under “Next up · {n} events”: its photo, its topic, its title, “Hosted by {host}”, its description, its day and time, its place, the RSVP button and “{price} · {n} going”.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newevent-featureL2-302 AC1

Overview

  • Exactly one per list: the next event that matches the filters. Every other event is an Event row.
  • The title is the link to the event page (L2-306); the card itself is not a link, because it holds the RSVP button.
  • The photo has an empty alt. An event with no photo shows its topic’s artwork (L2-308 AC6); the mocks draw it as a gold Media tile.

Anatomy

  1. Photo.event-feature__media on a .media tile: 16:9 above, full height beside.
  2. TopicA Topic tag.
  3. Title.event-feature__title, an h3 holding the link.
  4. Host.event-feature__host: “Hosted by {host}”.
  5. Description.event-feature__blurb.
  6. When.event-feature__when in .event-feature__facts, a dl with hidden terms.
  7. WhereAn Event place.
  8. ActionA large RSVP button in .event-feature__actions.
  9. Count.event-feature__count: “{price} · {n} going”.

Sizes

One size; the RSVP button in it is always --lg. The body padding grows from --space-5 to --space-8 at 576 px.

States

The card is static. The title link underlines on hover; the RSVP button has its own states. While the list loads, a 16:9 skeleton block takes the card’s place (L2-303 AC3).

Loading: the photo’s block (pages/events/loading).

<div style="width: 100%; min-width: 0;"><span class="skeleton skeleton--rect event-feature__media" aria-hidden="true"></span></div>

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 768 px: one column, the photo above at 16:9.
  • From 768 px: the photo takes the left 1.15 fr beside the text (1 fr) and fills the card’s height.
  • The actions wrap: the count goes under the button when they do not fit.

Theming

The card is a surface (--color-bg-surface) with a hairline edge and --radius-xl, in both themes.

Accessibility

Structure

An <article> named by its title (aria-labelledby). The day and the place are a dl whose terms (“When”, “Where”) are visually hidden, so they are announced but not drawn.

Keyboard

Two stops: the title link, then the RSVP button.

Content

  • When: “Thursday, Nov 13 · 7:30 PM”, the day spelled out.
  • Count: “Free · 143 going”, “$15 · 12 going”, or “{price} · Full”.
  • The description is the host’s words, shown as text (L2-312 AC2).
