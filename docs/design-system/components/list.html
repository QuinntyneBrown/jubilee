Component · Data display

List

Lists show a run of similar records one under another: services with a price and a Book button, requests waiting for a reply, items being added to a listing. They are the default way Jubilee shows a collection on a phone.

Overview

Use a list when each record is read on its own, top to bottom, with at most one or two actions. Use a Table when people compare records across columns, cards when a picture leads, and the specialised rows (Directory row, Message thread, Notification item) for those surfaces.

  • Rows (.rows / .row) are Jubilee’s main list: a title, muted meta lines, an optional price and actions, divided by hairlines. Used for services, products, requests and bookings.
  • Stacked rows (.row--stack) move the actions onto their own full-width line below 640px. Use them whenever a row has two buttons.
  • Free price (.row__price--free) colours "Free" in the success tone; every other price is the default ink.
  • Kit list (.list / .list__item) is the generic container. In the mocks .list wraps directory rows and message threads; .list__item is the plain item with an avatar, title and meta.
  • Item list (.item-list) is the bordered, compact list inside forms where the person adds and removes entries (services on List your business, line items on an order request).

Anatomy

  1. Leading avatar (optional)A 40px Avatar with initials, aria-hidden because the title already names the person.
  2. Title.row__title: semibold --font-size-md, wraps rather than truncates (overflow-wrap: anywhere).
  3. Meta lines.row__meta: one or two muted lines in --text-body-sm. Join facts with " · ".
  4. Actions.row__actions: small buttons, secondary before primary, each with an aria-label naming the row.
  5. DividerHairline --color-border-default under each row; .rows adds one on top.

Sizes

Rows have one size: --space-4 vertical padding, which with a 32px button gives a row of about 64px. Kit list items use --space-3 × --space-4; item-list entries are compact (--space-2 × --space-3) because they sit inside a form field. Do not add a dense row; use a table when density matters.

States

Static rows have no states of their own; their buttons do. Interactive list items (links) have default, hover, focus, active and current. Forced here with data-state and aria-current.

Responsive behaviour

  • Rows are single-line flex rows from 640px up. Below 640px .row--stack wraps: the actions take flex-basis: 100% and each button grows to share the line. Two actions too long to share it, such as “Sign out of other devices” beside “Sign out” on Account, wrap and take a line each.
  • Titles wrap; meta lines may truncate with .truncate in kit lists and threads, never in service rows where the meta is the duration.
  • Prices never wrap (white-space: nowrap).
  • Item lists keep their compact layout at every width; the remove button stays at the end of the line.

Theming

Lists use semantic tokens only: --color-border-default dividers, --color-fg-muted meta, --color-bg-subtle hover, --color-accent-subtle current, --color-success-fg for free prices. Item lists add a --color-border-strong outline because they behave like a form field.

Dark mode swaps the tokens; "Free" stays readable at 4.5:1 on both surfaces.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <ul> of <li>; screen readers announce the count ("list, 4 items"). Interactive items are links inside the <li>. Do not add role="listbox": these are navigation and content lists, not selection widgets. The relevant guidance is the APG landmarks and link pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to each interactive item or row action in order.
EnterFollows an item link.
Enter, SpaceActivates a row action or remove button.

Focus

Interactive items take the shared ring on the whole item. Row actions take the button ring. After a remove, focus moves to the next sensible control; it is never lost to the page.

Labelling

The title is the item’s name. Row actions carry an aria-label naming the row ("Confirm request from Samuel Park", "Remove Baptism or dedication"). Decorative avatars are aria-hidden. A current item uses aria-current="page".

Motion

Lists do not animate. Hover background changes are instant.

Touch

Interactive items are at least 44px tall. Row buttons are 32px with 8px between them; stacked rows make them full width on phones. Item-list remove buttons are 32px.

Content

  • Titles name the thing: "Discovery meeting", "Custom church team tee × 24". Use × for quantities.
  • Meta joins facts with " · ": "Order · Samuel Park · 8:15 a.m.", "30 min · In person or video".
  • Prices: "Free", "Ask" or a CAD figure. Never "$0" or "Contact us".
  • Order lists by what the person needs next: oldest unanswered request first, services in the order the business set.

One known CSS limitation, reported rather than patched: .list__item:last-child removes the divider from every item when the class sits on a link inside an <li> (each link is its own last child), so interactive kit lists lose their dividers; the demos show this.
