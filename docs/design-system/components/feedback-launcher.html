Feedback launcher

The launcher is the way into the feedback panel from every screen a signed-in member sees inside the app’s bars. It floats at the bottom right and opens a short dialog.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<button>L2-210

Overview

One launcher per screen, placed by the app shell, never by a page. It shows when the screen has the top bar and the tab bar and a member is signed in. A visitor, the bare screens (sign in, join, the error pages) and the feedback page itself show none.

  • Phone (under 768px): a round 44px icon button named “Give feedback”, above the Tab bar.
  • Tablet and desktop (from 768px): a 44px pill with the icon and the word “Feedback”.
  • It opens the feedback panel, a Dialog in the corner placement. See Member feedback.

Anatomy

  1. IconThe feedback icon: a speech bubble with a heart, --icon-size-md. The only content under 768px.
  2. Label.feedback-launcher__label, “Feedback”, --text-label semibold. Hidden under 768px.
  3. Pill--color-bg-inverse with --color-fg-inverse, a hairline --color-border-inverse edge, --radius-full and --shadow-3.

Variants

One launcher. The round button and the pill are the same element at two widths; see Responsive behaviour.

Sizes

One size: 44px tall at every width (--control-height-md), which is the touch target.

States

Forced with data-state. .feedback-launcher--static takes it out of its fixed position for documentation only.

VariantDefaultHoverFocusActive
Launcher

There is no disabled or busy state: when feedback cannot be given the launcher is not rendered. While the panel is open the page behind it, launcher included, is inert.

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 768px: a 44 × 44px circle, --space-4 from the right edge and --space-6 above the tab bar (--layout-tabbar-height plus the safe-area inset). It never covers the tab bar.
  • From 768px: the pill, --space-6 from the right and the bottom.
  • It shares the bars’ layer (--z-sticky): dialogs, their scrim and toasts cover it.
  • It is not printed.

Theming

The launcher is drawn on the inverse surface so it reads against the page in both themes: ink with white text in light, cream with ink text in dark. The hairline --color-border-inverse edge keeps it visible where it passes over the ink brand band.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <button type="button"> that opens a modal dialog. Focus returns to it when the dialog closes.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the launcher after the page content and the tab bar: it is last in the shell’s order.
Enter / SpaceOpens the feedback panel.

Focus

The shared 2px ring, offset 2px, following the pill.

Labelling

The accessible name is “Give feedback” at every width (aria-label); it contains the visible word “Feedback”, so speech input works (2.5.3). The icon is hidden from assistive technology.

Contrast

Label on the pill
Focus ring on the page

Motion

The press scales the pill to 97% over --duration-fast; under reduced motion it does not move.

Touch

44 × 44px at least, at every width.

Content

  • The label is “Feedback”. The name is “Give feedback”. Neither changes per screen.
  • No count, dot or badge on the launcher: it never asks for attention.
