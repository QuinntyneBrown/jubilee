Space card

A space card shows one room or rental in a grid: the cover, the term, the rent, the title, two key facts, the neighbourhood and when it is free. Rent, term and where come first, because that is what renters scan for.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newLink cardL2-203

Overview

Use a space card wherever a list shows spaces: the "Rooms & places to stay" shelf on Browse, the Rooms & rentals category, search results and "More rooms & rentals". Businesses keep the Business card; a space never uses it, and a space card never shows a business. The card is one link to the space page; it never carries the exact address (L2-203 AC4).

Anatomy

  1. Media and badge.space-card__media wraps a media tile (the cover photo, or the category tile) and the term badge .space-card__badge: "Short stay", "Long term", "Room" or "Roommate wanted".
  2. Rent.space-card__price in Archivo bold, wide; .space-card__unit reads "/ month" or "/ night" in the muted text face.
  3. Title.space-card__title, the card's accessible name: wraps and is clamped to two lines.
  4. Facts.space-card__facts: the first two key facts joined by " · ".
  5. Meta.space-card__meta holds .space-card__area (pin and neighbourhood, muted) and .space-card__available ("Available now", "From Dec 1", or a short stay's text as given) in semibold success green.

Responsive behaviour

  • The rent is --font-size-lg on a phone and --font-size-xl from 640px.
  • The meta line wraps: the availability drops under the neighbourhood in a narrow cell.
  • Long titles wrap to two lines and are then clamped; the full title stays the accessible name.

Theming

Only semantic tokens: the tile tints come from the media tile, the badge is --color-bg-surface with --shadow-1, the unit and neighbourhood --color-fg-muted and the availability --color-success-fg. Dark mode swaps them.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

One <a> per card, no nested controls. aria-labelledby points at the title so the link's name is the title; aria-describedby points at the badge, the rent and the neighbourhood so the term, rent and place are announced as text (L2-203 AC3). The term is never carried by colour: the badge says it.

Motion

Only colour and shadow transitions on hover, at --duration-fast; nothing moves, so reduced motion needs no change.

Touch

The whole card is the target, far larger than 44 by 44px.

Content

  • Title: what it is and where, as the owner wrote it ("Furnished room in a family home, Leslieville").
  • Rent in whole Canadian dollars with a thousands separator: "$1,850 / month", "$120 / night".
  • Badge: "Roommate wanted" for a Roommate; the other terms as named.
  • Availability: "Available now" when the owner wrote "Now", "From {date}" for monthly terms, the text as given for a short stay.
