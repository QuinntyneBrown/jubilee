Rating inline

The rating of a card: one gold star, the average to one decimal and the count in brackets, “★ 4.6 (5)”. It shows only when the average rests on 3 reviews or more.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newrating-inlineL2-230

Overview

  • On business, space and product cards, after the tagline (L2-230).
  • With fewer than 3 reviews the card shows nothing: no stars, no “0.0”, no “New” (ADR 0058).
  • The visible part is aria-hidden; a visually hidden line says “Rated 4.6 out of 5 from 5 reviews”, read as part of the card’s link.

Anatomy

  1. Star.rating-inline__star, filled --color-accent.
  2. Average.rating-inline__average, semibold, tabular figures.
  3. Count.rating-inline__count, muted, in brackets.

Sizes

One size: the card’s small text.

States

None of its own: it is part of the card link, whose hover underlines the name and whose focus ring surrounds the card.

Responsive behaviour

It never wraps (white-space: nowrap); “★ 4.6 (137)” is under 90px at every width.

Theming

The star is --color-accent; the average --color-fg-default; the count --color-fg-muted. In forced colours the star is CanvasText.

Accessibility

The hidden line is the whole name; the star, the average and the count are hidden so they are not read twice. The card’s link is announced “Legacy Financial, Build a Legacy…, Rated 4.6 out of 5 from 5 reviews, Free first meeting, Verified”.

Content

  • Average to one decimal, rounded half up from the stored counts (L2-230 AC4).
  • Count in brackets, no word: “(5)”, “(137)”.
  • Accessible name: “Rated {average} out of 5 from {n} reviews”.
