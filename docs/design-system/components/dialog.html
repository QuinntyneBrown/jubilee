Dialog

A dialog interrupts the page to ask for one decision or a few details: delete a product, book a meeting, join to message a business. It is Jubilee's most used overlay: 21 dialogs in 71 state mocks.

Overview

Use a dialog when the person must answer before they carry on and the question fits on one short screen: a confirmation, a booking or quote request, a share sheet. Do not use one for long forms (listing editor pages are pages), for messages that need no answer (use a Toast), or for navigation menus on desktop (use a Menu; the user menu becomes a sheet only on phones). See Dialogs and overlays for choosing between overlays.

  • Destructive: delete product, delete service, delete account, decline, cancel, unpublish. Danger icon, plain consequences, a Keep … button that gets focus and a red confirm. Use role="alertdialog".
  • Form: book a meeting, request a quote, order request, request changes, new message, report, product and service editors. Info icon, fields in the body, primary confirm last.
  • Information: sign-in required, share listing, sign out, filters, user menu. Usually the small panel; often only links or a single action.

Anatomy

  1. Icon (optional).dialog__icon--danger|info|success|warning: 40px circle (--avatar-size-md), status background and icon colour.
  2. Title<h2 class="dialog__title">, --text-h3, referenced by aria-labelledby.
  3. DescriptionOne muted paragraph referenced by aria-describedby: the consequence, in plain words.
  4. CloseGhost icon button, aria-label="Close". Escape does the same.
  5. Footer.dialog__footer: ruled, sticky, buttons right-aligned, confirm last.
  6. Backdrop and panel.backdrop dims the page with --color-bg-backdrop; .dialog__panel is the raised surface (--shadow-4, --radius-lg, sheet corners --radius-xl).

Corner placement

.dialog--corner places the panel in the bottom right corner from 640px, 24rem wide, --space-6 from the edges; under 640px it is the bottom sheet every dialog is. It is still a modal dialog: the scrim stays at every width, focus is held inside, and Escape, the close button and the scrim close it. Use it for the feedback panel only: a dialog a member opens from a fixed launcher in that corner. .dialog__footer-start is a slot at the start of the footer; it holds a link button (“Full form”) and takes no room when empty.

  • Default: initial focus goes to the safe button in destructive dialogs (Keep product), to the first field in forms and to the primary in success dialogs (View request).
  • Busy: the confirm button sets aria-busy="true" and shows the spinner; Cancel is disabled; fields become readonly (text) or disabled (selects, radios) so the values stay readable. Escape and Close are ignored until the request ends.
  • Invalid: each bad field gets aria-invalid="true" and a .field__error joined by aria-describedby; focus moves to the first one. The dialog does not shake or close.
  • Failed: a danger Alert and banner with role="alert" appears at the top of the body, the fields keep what was typed, and the confirm label becomes Try again where it was a plain verb.
  • Success: the same panel swaps to a success icon, a past-tense title and the next step. Confirmations that need no next step simply close and show a toast instead.
  • Hover, active and disabled belong to the buttons and fields inside; see Button and Form field.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm) the panel is a bottom sheet: full width, top corners --radius-xl, slides up. From 640px it is centred with --space-6 around it and scales in.
  • The panel never exceeds the viewport height minus --space-12; the body scrolls and the footer stays sticky at the bottom with its own background.
  • Footer buttons wrap when they do not fit; the confirm stays last. On narrow phones two buttons usually sit side by side; three wrap.
  • The user menu is a menu anchored to the avatar from 640px and this sheet below it; filters are inline chips from 768px and this sheet below it.

Theming

The panel is --color-bg-surface-raised, one step above cards, so a dialog over a card still separates. The backdrop is --color-bg-backdrop, a translucent ink in both themes. Status icons read the status tokens and stay legible in dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="dialog" with aria-modal="true", following the APG dialog (modal) pattern. Destructive confirmations use role="alertdialog" (APG alert dialog) so the consequence is read straight away. The page behind gets inert; the native <dialog> element with showModal() gives all of this for free and is the preferred build.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles through the dialog's controls only; focus never reaches the page behind.
EscCloses the dialog like Cancel (mocks mark this with data-dismiss-on-escape). Ignored while busy.
EnterSubmits a form dialog from a single-line field; it never triggers a destructive confirm on its own.
CloseFocus returns to the control that opened the dialog, or to the next sensible place if that control is gone (a deleted row).

Focus

On open, focus moves into the panel: to the safe action for destructive dialogs, the first field for forms, the primary for success. The shared ring is used inside; .dialog__panel :focus-visible keeps its offset so it is not clipped by the panel edge.

Labelling

aria-labelledby points to the title, aria-describedby to the description. Titles name the object ("Delete “Morning mug, set of two”?"), so the dialog makes sense when it is the only thing announced. The close button is aria-label="Close".

Motion

The backdrop fades in over --duration-base; the panel slides up (sheet) or scales from 96% (centred) over --duration-slow with --ease-enter. Reduced motion zeroes both through the tokens; the dialog simply appears.

Touch

Footer buttons are 44px tall with --space-3 between them; the close button is a 44px icon button. The sheet's footer sits above the phone's home indicator.

Content

  • Titles are questions for confirmations ("Delete your account?", "Sign out of Jubilee?") and verb phrases for forms ("Book a meeting with Legacy Financial").
  • The description states the consequence and what is kept: "The 9 past requests for it stay in your records. This can’t be undone."
  • Buttons pair a specific confirm with a specific escape: Delete product / Keep product, Sign out / Stay signed in. Never OK / Cancel.
  • Failure copy says what happened, what was kept and what to do: "Nothing was sent and what you typed is still here. Try again."
  • Success copy says what happens next and who does it: "Trevor usually confirms within a day."
