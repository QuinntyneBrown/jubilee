Marketing button

The marketing button is the marketing site’s action. As a link it moves the page to a section; as a button it sends one of the two launch forms.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<a> or <button>Marketing siteL2-215L2-127

Overview

Use it on the marketing site only. The app’s action is the Button; the two do not mix on one page.

  • A link (<a href="#…">) when it moves the page: “Launching soon” in the top bar, “Tell me when it launches” in the opening, “Send your business’s details” in the section for businesses. While Jubilee is launching soon every one of them moves within the page; none leads to the app (L2-215 AC6).
  • A button (<button type="submit">) when it sends a form: “Send my details” and “Tell me when it launches” in the Launch form. This is new with the launching-soon state, as is the busy state.
  • One per section. A second, lesser action beside it is a text link (.mkt-link, “Watch the film”), not a second button.

Anatomy

  1. Spinner.mkt-btn__spinner: a 16px ring before the label, shown only while the button is busy. Hidden from assistive technology.
  2. Label17px semibold, on one line. It stays as it is while the button is busy.
  3. Container.mkt-btn: ink with paper text, a 12px radius, --space-6 at the sides.
  4. IconOptional .mkt-icon after the label of a link: an arrow that points the way the page will move. Hidden from assistive technology.

Busy

A form’s button is busy from the moment a valid form is sent until the answer arrives (L2-216 AC4, L2-217 AC3, L2-127).

Busy: “Send my details” while the business pre-registration is being sent.
HTML
<button class="mkt-btn" type="submit" aria-busy="true"><span class="mkt-btn__spinner" aria-hidden="true"></span>Send my details</button>
Busy: “Tell me when it launches” while the sign-up is being sent.
HTML
<button class="mkt-btn" type="submit" aria-busy="true"><span class="mkt-btn__spinner" aria-hidden="true"></span>Tell me when it launches</button>
  • The label is kept. It is not replaced by “Sending…” and the button does not shrink to a spinner, so its name and its place do not change under the reader.
  • The button is not disabled: it keeps focus and stays in the tab order. A second press sends nothing; the form ignores it.
  • The spinner takes the label’s colour (currentColor, with the rest of the ring at 35%), so it works on ink and on gold.
  • Only a <button> is ever busy. A link moves the page at once and has nothing to wait for.

There is no disabled state. A form’s button is always available, and what is missing is said by the form when it is sent (see Launch form).

Responsive behaviour

  • The button keeps its size at every width and is as wide as its label. It does not stretch to the column on a phone.
  • In the opening it stands in a row with “Watch the film”; the row wraps when the two do not fit.
  • In a form it stands at the start of .mkt-form__actions, under the fields, at every width.
  • The pill stays in the top bar at every width, when the section links beside it are hidden under 1024px (L2-215 AC2).

Theming

The marketing site is light only: marketing.css defines no dark values and the page sets data-theme="light" on <body class="mkt">. Every example on this page stands in an element with class="mkt" and data-theme="light", so it looks the same whichever theme these docs are in.

Ink is --jb-mkt-ink (--color-bg-brand) and the label is --jb-mkt-paper (--color-bg-canvas): the two fixed grounds of the page, set as custom properties on .mkt. Gold reads --color-accent with --color-fg-on-accent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The element says what it does. A move within the page is a link with an href; sending a form is a native <button type="submit"> inside the <form>. Neither takes a role.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the button in reading order.
EnterFollows the link, or sends the form.
SpaceSends the form (a <button> only).
Enter in a fieldSends the form, as the button does.

Focus

The shared ring, 2px with a 2px offset, following the button’s corners. On the ink section the ring is the on-brand colour so that it shows. A link that moves the page puts focus on its target section; reduced motion makes the move a jump (L2-215 AC7).

Labelling

The name is the visible label. The arrow and the spinner are aria-hidden="true". While it is sending, the button has aria-busy="true" and the same name.

Busy is not carried by the spinner alone

The spinner is a visual cue. Assistive technology is told through aria-busy, the fields becoming read-only, and then the confirmation or the alert that takes the form’s place or stands above it.

Motion

Under reduced motion the colour and the press no longer animate. The spinner keeps turning: it is a status, and --duration-spin is never zeroed.

Touch

The default button is about 54px tall, above the 44px Jubilee asks for. The pill is 40px tall: it passes the WCAG 2.2 AA minimum of 24px (2.5.8) but is under Jubilee’s own 44px (L2-162, L2-215 AC8). It is the size the Magic Patterns reference draws for the top bar; see Sources.

Content

  • A label says what happens, in the reader’s words: “Tell me when it launches”, “Send my details”, “Send your business’s details”. Sentence case, no full stop.
  • The top bar’s label is “Launching soon”. It states where Jubilee is and leads to the form.
  • While Jubilee is launching soon no label says or implies that anyone can use Jubilee today: no “Start browsing”, “Open Jubilee”, “Join now” or “Get started” (L2-215 AC5).
  • No label promises an outcome: not “Grow your business” and not “Get more customers”. Jubilee never promises prosperity (docs/scripture.md, “What Jubilee never says”).
  • The busy label is the label. Do not write “Sending…”.
