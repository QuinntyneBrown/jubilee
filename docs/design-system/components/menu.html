Menu

A menu offers a short list of actions or places behind one trigger. On desktop it drops from the trigger; under 640px the same list opens as a bottom sheet.

Overview

Use a menu when one trigger offers three to eight related choices and showing them all would crowd the view: the account menu under the avatar, More actions on a product row on phones, More in the header of a conversation. Use a Select for a form value, Tabs for sections of one page, and a Popover for content that is not a list of commands.

  • Navigation menu: items are links (a.menu__item), like the account menu.
  • Action menu: items are buttons; destructive items sit last after a divider and use .menu__item--danger.
  • Selection menu: menuitemradio items with aria-checked show a tick (sort order).
  • Anchored menu: .menu-anchor wraps the trigger and its .menu, which drops from the trigger's start edge; .menu--end aligns it to the trigger's end edge instead. The conversation menu ("Mute" or "Unmute", "Report conversation") is anchored to "More" this way.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerButton with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded; here the avatar.
  2. Panel.menu: raised surface, hairline border, --radius-md, --shadow-3, --z-dropdown.
  3. Item.menu__item: an optional leading icon in --color-fg-muted, then the label; 32px tall on pointer devices, --radius-sm hover fill.
  4. Divider.menu__divider separates the destructive group.
  5. Danger item.menu__item--danger in --color-danger-fg; always last.

Variants

Most menus here are shown in place (position: static); in product they are absolutely positioned under the trigger inside .menu-anchor, as the two anchored examples at the end of this section are.

Anchored to its trigger

Inside .menu-anchor the menu sits directly under the trigger, as wide as its longest item (width: max-content, at most 20rem or 90% of the viewport). A trigger at the end of a row, like "More" in the conversation header, takes .menu--end so the menu opens toward the page instead of off its edge, and scales in from the top right.

Sizes

One size on pointer devices: 32px items (--control-height-sm), minimum width 12rem. In the phone sheet the same items are 44px (--target-comfortable). An anchored menu is as wide as its longest item, between 12rem and min(20rem, 90vw).

States

Item states, forced with data-state. Active keeps the hover look; the menu closes on release.

Open and closed

Closed: the trigger has aria-expanded="false" and the list is not rendered. Open: aria-expanded="true", the panel scales in from the trigger over --duration-fast (from the top left, or the top right with .menu--end) and focus moves to the first item. Choosing an item, Esc or a tap outside closes it.

Responsive behaviour

  • From 640px the menu drops from its trigger, aligned to the trigger's start edge, and flips above when there is no room below. A trigger at the end of a row uses .menu--end, which aligns the menu to the trigger's end edge.
  • The conversation menu stays anchored at every width: its two items fit beside the header on a phone, and max-width: min(20rem, 90vw) keeps it on screen.
  • Under 640px the same items open in a bottom-sheet Dialog with 44px rows and a Close button, as the account-menu mock shows. The sheet traps focus; the dropdown does not.
  • Row actions that fit as icon buttons on desktop (Edit, Delete) collapse into one More actions menu on phones.

Theming

The panel is --color-bg-surface-raised with --shadow-3; in dark the raised surface is lighter than the page and the shadow deepens. Hover uses --color-bg-subtle; danger items use --color-danger-fg, which lightens in dark. A leading icon is --color-fg-muted in both themes, so the label stays the stronger of the two.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Trigger is a button with aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and aria-controls; the list is role="menu" with menuitem / menuitemradio children and role="none" on the lis. Follows the APG menu button pattern. The phone sheet is a dialog with a plain list of links, not a menu. In the app the menu is jb-menu, built with Angular CDK Menu (@angular/cdk/menu): the CDK supplies the roles, the roving focus, the arrow keys and the return of focus to the trigger, so none of it is hand-written.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter, Space, ↓ on triggerOpens and focuses the first item. ↑ opens on the last.
↓ / ↑Moves between items, wrapping; disabled items are focusable but do nothing.
Home / EndFirst / last item.
Enter / SpaceChooses the item and closes; focus returns to the trigger (or moves to the dialog it opened, as "Report conversation" does).

TabCloses and moves on in page order.

Focus

Roving focus: the focused item gets the subtle fill plus the 2px ring drawn inside the item. The ring is needed because --color-bg-subtle on the raised surface alone is below 3:1.

Labelling

The trigger's name says whose menu it is (“Account menu for Joanna Adeyemi”, “More actions for Faith heavyweight tee, charcoal”); the menu is labelled by the trigger or an aria-label. Disabled items say why in their label. Where a screen has one such trigger, its name may be the plain "More", as in the conversation header; the menu is then named for its subject (aria-label="Conversation"). Leading icons are aria-hidden: the label carries the meaning.

Motion

Scales in from 96% with a fade over --duration-fast and --ease-enter; reduced motion zeroes the duration so it appears at once.

Touch

By touch a menu is a tap: a tap on the trigger opens it, a tap on an item chooses it and a tap outside closes it; nothing depends on hover or a long press. On touch the sheet's 44px rows apply. Dropdown items are 32px tall, full width and adjacent; their combined area keeps each target above 24px. The "More" trigger is a 44px icon button.

Content

  • Items are verbs or destinations in sentence case: “Edit product”, “Your requests”, “Sign out”.
  • Put the object in the trigger label, not in every item.
  • Destructive items name the loss and open a confirmation dialog; they never act immediately.
  • At most eight items; more means the content belongs on a page.
  • The conversation menu reads "Mute" (or "Unmute" once muted) and "Report conversation". An item that switches something names what choosing it will do, and the state itself is said elsewhere in words: the header's "Muted" badge.
  • A leading icon repeats the label; use icons on every item of a menu or on none.

Drift fixed during extraction: in the account, product and sort mocks the dropdown form is never drawn, only described in a mock note; the phone sheet sets style="min-height: var(--target-comfortable)" on all seven items and reuses .menu__item inside a .list. None of those mocks carries aria-haspopup or aria-expanded; the avatar trigger is a link to the sheet page. In the CSS, .menu__item:focus-visible removes the outline and relies on --color-bg-subtle, which is below 3:1 on the raised surface; the system documents the ring as required (CSS fix proposed).
