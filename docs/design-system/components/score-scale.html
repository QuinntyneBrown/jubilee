Score scale

A score scale asks for a number from 1 to 5: five native radios drawn as square buttons. It scores each part of Jubilee on the feedback page and carries the question of the moment check-in.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablefieldset of native radiosL2-211L2-212

Overview

Use it where a number is the answer and the question says what the ends mean. For the one overall rating, use the Star scale.

  • In a row: .score-rows lists the parts of Jubilee, each with a name, a detail and its scale.
  • Wide: .score-scale--wide shares a row of at most 24rem equally, in the Moment check.
  • On the feedback page pressing the chosen score again clears it; in the check-in choosing another score replaces the first.

Anatomy

  1. Name.score-rows__name, 16px medium.
  2. Detail.score-rows__detail, --text-body-sm, muted.
  3. Option.score-scale__option: a 44px square label with a hairline --color-border-strong edge around a hidden radio and its number.
  4. Chosen option--color-accent with a --color-accent-border edge and a bold number.

Sizes

One size: each option is 44px tall (--control-height-md), which is the touch target. The wide variant changes only the width.

States

Forced with data-state on one option.

Responsive behaviour

  • Five squares and four gaps are 252px, so the scale fits a 360px screen with room to spare.
  • In .score-rows the scale sits under the name below 640px and at the end of the row from 640px.
  • The wide variant fills its container up to 24rem (384px).

Theming

An option is a --color-bg-surface square with a --color-border-strong edge. The chosen one is --color-accent with ink text and a --color-accent-border edge, like a selected Chip, and its number turns bold so colour is not the only carrier.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <fieldset> with a visually hidden <legend> that names what is scored, holding five <input type="radio"> with one name. One group of five choices to assistive technology.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves into the group: to the chosen option, or the first.
← ↑ / → ↓Moves to the option before or after and chooses it.
SpaceChooses the focused option; on the feedback page it clears a chosen one.

Focus

The shared 2px ring, offset 2px, around the focused square.

Labelling

The legend names the thing scored and the range (“Finding businesses, from 1 to 5”); in the check-in it is the question and the meaning of the ends. Each radio’s name is its number.

Motion

Background and edge change over --duration-fast; nothing moves.

Touch

Each option is at least 44 × 44px; the gaps are 8px.

Content

  • The options are the numbers 1 to 5, never words.
  • Say what the ends mean near the scale: “Very hard” and “Very easy” under the check-in, “Skip any you haven’t used.” above the rows.
  • The parts of Jubilee are fixed: Finding businesses, Listing your business, Messages and requests, Trust and verification.
