Button group

A button group joins related buttons into one pill so they read as one control: the stats range on My business, the ways a service is offered, the arrows that reorder a listing photo.

Overview

Use a button group when two to four buttons act on the same thing and belong side by side. Each segment is a full Button; the group only removes the inner corners and overlaps the borders.

  • Toggle set: each segment is an independent on/off (aria-pressed), like Video call, In person, Phone in the service editor.
  • Single-choice switch: exactly one segment is pressed and the view changes immediately (7 days / 30 days / 12 months on the My business overview). It changes a view, not form data.
  • Action pair: two momentary actions on one object, such as moving a photo left or right. No pressed state.
  • When the choice is form data that gets submitted, use a Radio group instead. When the toggles filter a list, use filter chips, as the category mocks do.

Anatomy

  1. Pressed segmentGold fill (--color-accent), ink label, --color-accent-border edge, lifted to --z-raised so its edge sits on top.
  2. SegmentA standard .btn; inner corners square, left border overlapped by --border-width-hairline.
  3. Group.btn-group with role="group" and an aria-label; outer corners take --btn-group-radius (pill).

Variants

Pick the variant by what pressing does.

Toggle set: segments switch independently. Each carries its own aria-pressed.
HTML
<div class="btn-group" role="group" aria-label="Offered as"><button class="btn" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Video call</button><button class="btn" type="button" aria-pressed="false">In person</button><button class="btn" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Phone</button></div>
Single-choice switch: pressing one releases the others. Script keeps exactly one aria-pressed="true".
HTML
<div class="btn-group" role="group" aria-label="Stats range"><button class="btn btn--sm" type="button" aria-pressed="false">7 days</button><button class="btn btn--sm" type="button" aria-pressed="true">30 days</button><button class="btn btn--sm" type="button" aria-pressed="false">12 months</button></div>
Action pair with icon-only segments; each names the action and its object.
HTML
<div class="btn-group" role="group" aria-label="Reorder cover photo"><button class="btn btn--icon" type="button" aria-label="Move cover photo left"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path d="m15 6-6 6 6 6"/></svg></button><button class="btn btn--icon" type="button" aria-label="Move cover photo right"><svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true" viewBox="0 0 24 24"><path d="m9 6 6 6-6 6"/></svg></button></div>

Sizes

Groups take the button sizes. Small sits in card headers and toolbars; medium in forms and dialogs. Never mix sizes inside one group.

Small 32px, medium 44px, large 48px (--control-height-sm/md/lg).
HTML
<div class="btn-group" role="group" aria-label="Stats range, small"><button class="btn btn--sm" type="button" aria-pressed="false">7 days</button><button class="btn btn--sm" type="button" aria-pressed="true">30 days</button><button class="btn btn--sm" type="button" aria-pressed="false">12 months</button></div>
<div class="btn-group" role="group" aria-label="Offered as, medium"><button class="btn" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Video call</button><button class="btn" type="button" aria-pressed="false">In person</button><button class="btn" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Phone</button></div>
<div class="btn-group" role="group" aria-label="Preview as, large"><button class="btn btn--lg" type="button" aria-pressed="true">Phone</button><button class="btn btn--lg" type="button" aria-pressed="false">Desktop</button></div>

States

States are the button states; the forced state sits on the middle segment. Pressed is the group's own state.

Both themes

  • Pressed uses gold with an ink label, the same look as a selected chip, so “on” reads the same everywhere in Jubilee.
  • Focus raises the focused segment so the full ring shows over its neighbours.
  • Disabled applies to one segment (an option not available for this service), never to the whole group; hide the group instead.

Responsive behaviour

  • Groups never wrap: a wrapped group loses its joined edges. If it does not fit at 360px, use a select or radio group below 640px.
  • Three segments of up to 12 characters fit a 360px phone at medium size. Longer labels mean the group is the wrong control.
  • Inside a card header the group keeps its size and the header wraps around it (.cluster--between).

Theming

The group sets --btn-group-radius (default --radius-full) and re-maps the pressed segment's button tokens. A square re-skin sets the radius token on .btn-group; nothing else changes.

.btn-group .btn[aria-pressed="true"] { --btn-bg: var(--color-accent); --btn-fg: var(--color-fg-on-accent); --btn-border: var(--color-accent-border); z-index: var(--z-raised); }

In dark the gold stays gold and the label stays ink; unpressed segments use the dark surface and strong border.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A div with role="group" and an aria-label naming what the buttons control. Toggles are native buttons with aria-pressed, per the APG button pattern (toggle button). A toolbar's arrow-key model (APG toolbar) is not used: groups are short, and Tab through each segment is what members expect.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through each segment in order.
Enter, SpaceActivates the segment; toggles flip aria-pressed; a switch presses this one and releases the others.

Focus

The shared 2px ring on :focus-visible; the focused segment rises to --z-raised so no neighbour covers it.

Labelling

The group's aria-label names the setting (“Offered as”, “Stats range”). Segment labels stay constant; the pressed state is announced from aria-pressed, never by changing the label to “Video call (on)”.

Motion

Background and border transition over --duration-fast; reduced motion zeroes it through the token. Pressing never animates the segment's width.

Touch

Medium segments are 44px tall; small ones are 32px and sit apart from other targets by at least 8px, which keeps 2.5.8 target spacing.

Content

  • Segment labels are nouns or short noun phrases in sentence case: “Video call”, “30 days”. One to three words.
  • Name the group in its aria-label and, where the context does not make it obvious, in a visible label above it.
  • Icon-only segments carry full aria-labels (“Move cover photo left”).

Drift fixed during extraction: no mock page renders .btn-group; it existed only in the kit CSS, with --radius-md corners, a raw -1px overlap, z-index: 1 and an accent-subtle pressed look that disagreed with gold selected chips. The system makes it a pill through --btn-group-radius, overlaps by --border-width-hairline, raises with --z-raised and presses gold like a selected chip. The toggle filters that do appear in the mocks (category page) correctly use chips with aria-pressed in a labelled group.
