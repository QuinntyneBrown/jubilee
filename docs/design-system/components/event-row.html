Component · Data display

Event row

The rest of the list, by day: a heading per day (“Friday, Nov 14”) and under it a row per event with its start time, its title as a link, its topic, “{host} · {price}”, its place and its RSVP button.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newevent-day event-rowL2-302 AC2

Overview

  • Days are in start order; within a day, events are in start order. A day in a later year carries the year (“Saturday, Jan 9, 2027”, L2-302 AC3).
  • The row is not a link: its title is. The RSVP button is the row’s only other control.
  • Past 50 events, “Show more events” (.event-day__more) appends the next 50 and moves focus to the first new row (L2-302 AC5).

Anatomy

  1. Day heading.event-day__title, an h3 with a strong rule under it.
  2. Time.event-row__time, tabular figures.
  3. Title.event-row__title, an h4 holding the link to the event page.
  4. TopicA Topic tag.
  5. Host and price.event-row__meta: “{host} · {price}”.
  6. PlaceAn Event place, compact.
  7. ActionAn RSVP button in .event-row__action.

Responsive behaviour

  • From 576 px: three columns, the time (84 px), the text and the button, centred.
  • Under 576 px: the time (64 px) beside the text, and the button under the text in the second column.
  • The venue is cut with an ellipsis; the title and the meta wrap.

Theming

The day rule is --color-border-strong, the rows are separated by --color-border-default; the meta is muted.

Accessibility

Structure

Each day is a <section> named by its h3; its rows are a <ul>. The titles are h4 under the day, so a screen reader can move from event to event by heading.

Keyboard

Tab reaches the title link, then the RSVP button, row by row in reading order.

Content

  • Day: “Friday, Nov 14”; with the year when it is not this year.
  • Time: “6:30 PM”, Toronto time; the note under the list says “All times Eastern.”
  • Meta: “{host} · {price}”, the price “Free”, “Free, {note}” or “${amount}” (“$15”).
