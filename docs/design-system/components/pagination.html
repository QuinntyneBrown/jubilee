Pagination

Pagination splits a long list of businesses into pages of 16 and tells the member how far through they are: "Showing 1–16 of 312".

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable<nav> + <ul>category/long mock

Overview

Use pagination on category and search results longer than one page (16 cards). Short lists show everything; message threads and notifications load older items on scroll instead. Only the long category mock paginates today.

  • A result count on the left (.text-caption) and the pager on the right, in a .cluster--between row.
  • The pager shows previous, the first pages, an ellipsis, the last page and next. The current page is a gold pill with the accent border.

Anatomy

  1. Result count.text-caption: "Showing 1–16 of 312".
  2. Previous‹ labelled "Previous page"; aria-disabled="true" and no href on page 1.
  3. Current pagearia-current="page": --color-accent fill, --color-accent-border edge, ink label.
  4. Ellipsis<span aria-hidden="true">…</span>, not focusable.
  5. Page link32px pill (--control-height-sm, --radius-full), muted label, subtle background on hover.

Variants

One pager. On page 1 previous is disabled; on the last page next is.

Sizes

One size: --control-height-sm (32px) pills. Pagination sits under content and never needs a larger form.

States

Forced with data-state where no attribute exists. Current uses aria-current; disabled uses aria-disabled without href.

Responsive behaviour

  • At 360px the count sits above the pager (the cluster wraps) and the pager keeps all seven items in 250px.
  • Never drop to only previous/next: the page count is how members judge a long category.
  • Beyond 7 visible items, collapse middle pages into one ellipsis on each side of the current page.

Theming

Muted and subtle tokens flip with the theme; the current page stays gold with an ink label (--color-fg-on-accent) and the darker gold edge (--color-accent-border) that keeps it at 3:1 against the page.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<nav aria-label="Pagination"> with a list of links; the current page has aria-current="page". There is no APG pattern for pagination; it follows landmark regions and the link pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through live arrows and page links; the disabled arrow and the ellipsis are skipped.
EnterLoads that page and moves focus to the results heading.

Focus

The shared ring around the pill. After a page loads, focus goes to the results heading, not back to the pager, and the count updates.

Labelling

Arrows are labelled "Previous page" and "Next page"; numbers "Page 2". The count text gives the total.

Motion

None; page changes are instant.

Touch

Pills are 32px, above the 24px minimum, spaced --space-1. They are below the 44px comfortable size; the count and arrows give enough margin that mis-taps are rare, but do not shrink them further.

Content

  • Count reads "Showing 1–16 of 312" with an en dash, no "results".
  • Arrows are glyphs with labels; never "Prev" or "Next »" text.
  • Never paginate fewer than 17 items.
