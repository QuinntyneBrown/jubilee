Lockup

The lockup is the Jubilee mark followed by the wordmark: the J with its gold bar, then lowercase “jubilee” in Archivo. It is how a screen without a top bar says whose it is.

Overview

Use the lockup where Jubilee names itself outside the shell: above the card and on the brand panel of the Auth layout. The mark and the wordmark travel together, at one of three sizes, and are never stretched or re-spaced to fit. For the mark alone use the brand icon from Iconography; in a sentence write “Jubilee” as a word.

  • Default (.lockup): a 30px wordmark beside a 24px mark, the brand link the bare pages have always had.
  • Large (.lockup--lg): 48px, centred above the card of Log in and Sign up under 1024px.
  • Extra large (.lockup--xl): 52px, on the brand panel of those two screens from 1024px.
  • On ink (.lockup--on-ink): the same lockup in --color-fg-on-brand, for --color-bg-brand surfaces.
  • Link or text: an a named “Jubilee home” where it leads to the welcome page, a span where it only signs the screen.

The top bar (.topbar__brand) and the ten default bare pages (.auth-brand) still write the mark and the word with their own classes; they move to this block with issue #59 (ADR 0038).

Anatomy

  1. Marksvg.brand-mark, aria-hidden: the J in currentColor with its bar in --color-accent, in both themes and on ink.
  2. Wordmarkspan.lockup__word: lowercase “jubilee” in Archivo bold (700) at 110% width with −0.03em tracking. Real text, read as “jubilee”.

The scale has no 52px step, so the extra-large size is composed from two tokens; no token was added for it (ADR 0038).

States

The lockup is a link or plain text; it has no states of its own. Focus is forced here with data-state. Hover and active change nothing: the lockup is not underlined and .lockup:hover restates its colour, so the page's link hover does not reach it. It is never disabled: where there is nowhere to go it is a span.

Responsive behaviour

  • The lockup does not resize with the viewport. The Auth layout swaps one for another: .lockup--lg above the card under 1024px, .lockup--xl on the panel from 1024px.
  • The mark and the word stay on one line at every width. The extra-large lockup is about 230px wide, so it fits a 320px screen with the page margin.
  • Sizes are in rem and em, so the lockup grows with the reader's text size and keeps its proportions.

Theming

The J and the word are currentColor: --color-fg-default on the page, so ink in the light theme and cream in the dark one. The bar is --color-accent everywhere. On ink, .lockup--on-ink sets --color-fg-on-brand, which is the same in both themes because the brand surface is ink in both. The lockup reads tokens only; it has no component tokens to override.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A link (<a href>) or plain text (<span>); no ARIA. The mark is aria-hidden. As a link the lockup carries aria-label="Jubilee home", as on Log in and Sign up, where it leads to the welcome page (L2-187); as text it is read once, as “jubilee”. See the APG link pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the lockup when it is a link. On the bare pages it is the first stop after the skip link.
EnterFollows the link.

Focus

The shared 2px ring at a 2px offset. On ink the ring must be --color-focus-ring-on-brand, as on every brand surface: the light theme's ring is ink and measures 1.00:1 on --color-bg-brand. The kit sets that colour inside .hero, .band and .topbar--ink; a rule for the brand panel of the auth layout is open (see Sources).

Labelling

The link's name, “Jubilee home”, says where it leads and contains the word a sighted visitor reads, so speech input still finds it (2.5.3); keep “Jubilee” in any name you give it. Do not put the lockup in a heading: the screen's h1 is the title of its task.

Measured with the contrast checker: the wordmark is 17.25:1 on the page and 19.32:1 on a surface in light, 15.49:1 and 14.24:1 in dark, and 19.32:1 on ink in both. The gold bar is 11.15:1 on ink. On the light page it is 1.55:1 (1.73:1 on a surface), which is allowed: it is part of the logotype, and the J and the word carry the name.

Motion

None.

Touch

As a link the default lockup is 45px tall and the two large ones over 50px, all above the 24px minimum (2.5.8).

Content

  • The wordmark is always lowercase “jubilee”. In a sentence the product is “Jubilee”.
  • It is always Archivo bold (700) at 110% width with −0.03em tracking, read from --font-weight-bold, --font-stretch-wide and --letter-spacing-tighter. Never 800, never a fallback face: Archivo ships with the system so that Windows does not substitute a heavier one.
  • Nothing is attached to it: no tagline, no page title, no “Toronto”.
  • The mark comes first and the word second, on one line.

Drift and open items: the mark and the wordmark are drawn in three places (.lockup, .auth-brand, .topbar__brand) until issue #59 is closed. Open in the kit: no rule gives the brand panel of the auth layout the on-brand focus ring, so the lockup's ring is ink on ink there in the light theme; and .lockup has no data-state hover hook, which is why hover is described rather than shown.
