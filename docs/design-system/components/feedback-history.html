Component · Data display

Feedback history

“Your feedback” lists what a member has sent, newest first: each rating, bug report and kudos with the icon of its kind, a title, its date and reference, its status in words and the team’s reply.

Overview

Use it on the feedback page, beside the form from 1024px and under it below. It shows the member’s own feedback and nobody else’s. Moment check-ins are not in it.

  • The status is always a word: Received, Read by the team, Being worked on or Fixed.
  • A bug report shows its reference (FB- and six characters) with its date.
  • A reply from the team shows under the item it answers.

Anatomy

  1. Heading.feedback-history__heading, “Your feedback”, --text-label semibold.
  2. Kind icon.feedback-history__icon: star, bug or heart in a 32px --color-bg-subtle disc. Decorative; the title carries a hidden kind.
  3. Title.feedback-history__title, 16px medium. A bug’s title is the first line of what happened, held to 80 characters.
  4. Date and reference.feedback-history__meta with a <time>, --text-body-sm, muted.
  5. Status.feedback-history__status: the word, semibold in the text colour.
  6. Reply.feedback-history__reply with a 2px --color-accent leading edge.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 1024px the history follows the form; from 1024px it is the second column of the page’s split.
  • Titles and replies wrap; a long word breaks rather than widening the column.
  • Nothing in an item is interactive, so rows have no minimum target height.

Theming

Items sit on the page with --color-border-default hairlines between them. The icon disc is --color-bg-subtle. The reply’s gold edge is decoration: a hidden “Reply from Jubilee:” says what it is.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section> named by its heading, holding a <ul>. Items are not links.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the list: nothing in it takes focus. In the error state, reaches “Try again”.

Focus

Only “Try again” takes focus, with the shared ring.

Labelling

Each title starts with a visually hidden kind (“Bug report:”, “Kudos:”, “Rating:”), so the icon is not the only carrier. The status is text; colour never tells it. The date is a <time datetime>. The loading list is aria-busy="true" and named “Loading your feedback”.

Motion

None, apart from the skeleton’s shimmer, which stops under reduced motion.

Touch

No targets in the list. “Try again” follows the alert’s action size.

Content

  • Titles: “Rated Jubilee {n} of 5”; the first line of a bug report; “Kudos to the Jubilee team” or “Kudos to {business}”.
  • Dates follow the list format: the time today, “Yesterday”, “Sat 7 Nov” within a week, “28 Oct” before.
  • Empty: “Nothing yet. What you send shows up here, with our reply.” Error: “We couldn’t load your feedback.”
  • A reply ends with who wrote it: “Miriam, Jubilee”.
