Checkbox

A checkbox turns one independent option on or off: keep me signed in, agree to the statement of faith, each filter in a list. The whole label is the target.

Overview

Use a checkbox for an option that is independent of the others, or for picking several from a short list. Use a Radio group for exactly one of several, and a Switch for a setting that applies the moment it changes.

  • Single: one statement (“Keep me signed in”, the agreement on sign-up).
  • Group: a fieldset with a legend and one checkbox per option (filters, request changes).
  • With description: a second line in small inside .choice__text.
  • Indeterminate: a parent “Select all” that is partly on.

Anatomy

  1. Box16px (--checkbox-size) with --radius-xs (4px) and a hairline --color-fg-subtle edge; checked fills green (--color-success-solid) with a --color-fg-on-success tick, white in light.
  2. Label.choice__text, --text-body-sm in --color-fg-secondary: the accessible name; the whole label.choice is clickable and at least 24px tall.
  3. DescriptionOptional small in --color-fg-muted.

Sizes

One size: a 16px box in a row at least 24px tall (--target-min). Do not scale the box; scale the label text with its context.

States

Forced with data-state on the input; real pages use :hover, :focus-visible, checked, disabled, indeterminate and aria-invalid. Active keeps the hover look: the change happens on release.

Both themes

Offers
Offers
  • Hover darkens the box edge to --color-fg-default.
  • Indeterminate fills green like checked and shows a bar instead of a tick.
  • Invalid thickens the box edge in --color-danger-solid, and the field error under the label says what to do (“Agree to the statement of faith to join.”).
  • Disabled greys box and label; used while a form submits.

Responsive behaviour

  • Groups stay one per line at every width; never put checkboxes in columns that read across.
  • Filter groups shown as checkboxes in the phone sheet become filter chips inline from 768px (same state, different control).

Theming

Checked uses a --color-success-solid fill and edge with a --color-fg-on-success tick: green with a white tick in light, a lighter green with an ink tick in dark. It is no longer gold. Unchecked uses --color-bg-surface and a --color-fg-subtle edge.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <input type="checkbox"> inside a <label>; groups in <fieldset>/<legend>. See the APG checkbox pattern (including mixed state).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to each checkbox in turn.
SpaceToggles. Enter does not toggle; in a form it submits.

Focus

The shared 2px ring around the box (:focus-visible).

Labelling

The label wraps the input, so it names it and enlarges the target. Groups take their name from the legend. Errors link via aria-describedby on the input and aria-invalid="true".

Motion

Fill and edge transition over --duration-fast (150ms); reduced motion zeroes it.

Touch

The label row is at least 24px (--target-min) and the full label is clickable. In the filters sheet rows are spaced so each is effectively 44px.

Content

  • Labels are positive statements: “Keep me signed in”, not “Don’t sign me out”.
  • Agreement labels name what is agreed to and link it.
  • Legends are questions or nouns: “What needs to change?”, “Area served”.
