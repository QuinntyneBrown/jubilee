Checkbox

A checkbox turns one independent option on or off: keep me logged in, agree to the statement of faith, each filter in a list. The whole label is the target.

Overview

Use a checkbox for an option that is independent of the others, or for picking several from a short list. Use a Radio group for exactly one of several, and a Switch for a setting that applies the moment it changes.

  • Single: one statement (“Keep me logged in”, the agreement on Sign up).
  • Sentence with links: the label is a sentence that links what it mentions; it wraps as one paragraph beside the box.
  • Group: a fieldset with a legend and one checkbox per option (filters, request changes).
  • With description: a small inside .choice__text, set as a block under the label.
  • Indeterminate: a parent “Select all” that is partly on.

Anatomy

  1. Box20px (--icon-size-md) with --radius-xs, strong border; checked fills gold with an ink tick.
  2. Label.choice__text: the accessible name; the whole label.choice is clickable and at least 24px tall.
  3. DescriptionOptional small in --color-fg-muted, a block --space-0-5 under the label.

Variants

Single (Log in). Not ticked until the visitor ticks it: a ticked default would give every visitor a 30-day session, on a shared computer too (ADR 0038).
HTML
<div><label class="choice"><input type="checkbox"><span class="choice__text">Keep me logged in</span></label></div>
A sentence with links (Sign up). .choice__text is plain text, so the sentence wraps under itself and never under the box. Each link is its own stop after the box; pressing one follows it and does not tick the box.

Sizes

One size: a 20px box in a row at least 24px tall (--target-min). Do not scale the box; scale the label text with its context.

States

Forced with data-state on the input; real pages use :hover, :focus-visible, checked, disabled, indeterminate and aria-invalid. Active keeps the hover look: the change happens on release.

Indeterminate shows one look in the matrix because data-state takes one value; in product it combines with real hover, focus and disabled.

Both themes

Offers
Offers
  • Invalid thickens the box edge in --color-danger-solid, and the field error under the label says what to do (“Agree to the statement of faith to join.”).
  • Disabled greys box and label; used while a form submits.

Responsive behaviour

  • Groups stay one per line at every width; never put checkboxes in columns that read across.
  • Filter groups shown as checkboxes in the phone sheet become filter chips inline from 768px (same state, different control).

Theming

Checked uses --color-accent fill with a --color-accent-border edge and --color-fg-on-accent tick, so the gold reads on cream and on the dark surface. Unchecked uses --color-bg-surface and --color-border-strong.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <input type="checkbox"> inside a <label>; groups in <fieldset>/<legend>. See the APG checkbox pattern (including mixed state).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to each checkbox in turn.
SpaceToggles. Enter does not toggle; in a form it submits.
Tab in a sentence with linksThe box first, then each link in the sentence.

Focus

The shared 2px ring around the box (:focus-visible).

Labelling

The label wraps the input, so it names it and enlarges the target. In a sentence with links the whole sentence is the name, link words included; the links open without losing what was typed in the form (L2-001). Groups take their name from the legend. Errors link via aria-describedby on the input and aria-invalid="true".

Motion

Fill and edge transition over --duration-fast; reduced motion zeroes it.

Touch

The label row is at least 24px (--target-min) and the full label is clickable. In the filters sheet rows are spaced so each is effectively 44px.

Content

  • Labels are positive statements: “Keep me logged in”, not “Don’t log me out”.
  • Agreement labels name what is agreed to and link it, in one sentence with a full stop: “I am 18 or older and agree to Jubilee’s statement of faith and terms.”
  • Legends are questions or nouns: “What needs to change?”, “Area served”.
