Review subject

At the top of the Write a review screen: the listing’s cover photo or its category tile, 48px and rounded, its name and, under it, its category or, for a space, “Hosted by {owner}”.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newreview-subjectL2-226

Overview

  • It is not a link: “Back to {name}” above it leads back.
  • Name and meta are cut with an ellipsis on one line each.

Anatomy

  1. Media.review-subject__media with a tint modifier, or an img.
  2. Name.review-subject__name.
  3. Meta.review-subject__meta: category or host.

Sizes

One size: a 48px tile.

States

None: it is static.

Responsive behaviour

One row at every width; the text cuts with an ellipsis rather than wrap.

Theming

The tile tints follow the Media tile: --color-success-bg, --color-accent-subtle, --color-info-bg.
