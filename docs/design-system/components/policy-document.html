Policy document

A policy page as members read it: the statement of faith, the terms of use or the privacy notice, with its title, lead, sections and "Last updated {date}" (L2-107 to L2-109). The admin application's editor shows the draft in the same block (L2-247).

Overview

The block policy-document sets a policy page in reading measure. Its text is plain: paragraphs, "- " bullets and links to Jubilee pages or an e-mail address, parsed before it reaches the block; it never renders markup (ADR 0030). Help is not a policy document: it is an Accordion.

Anatomy

Terms of use

These terms cover using Jubilee as a member and as a listed business. Jubilee introduces buyers and businesses; the agreement to buy is between the two of you.

Requests and payment

Bookings, quotes and order requests are sent to the business, which confirms or declines them. Jubilee does not take payment or hold funds; you pay the business directly.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Variants

Page (the default): the title is the page's h1 and sections are h2. Preview (level 2): inside the editor of the admin application, whose own h1 names the page, the title is an h2 and the sections h3; the look is the same, so the preview is what members read.

Privacy

We collect only what the marketplace needs to work, and we never sell it.

What we keep

  • Messages and requests you send, so both sides can see them.
  • For businesses: listing details.

Your choices

Change what we e-mail you in notification settings.

Last updated 10 November 2026

Sizes

One size: the block is at most the reading measure wide (--layout-measure).

States

The block has no state of its own. Its links take the states of Link; the page around it shows loading and error.

Responsive behaviour

The title is set at --font-size-3xl under 768px and --font-size-4xl from 768px, as every page title.

Theming

Text, lead and date read their colours by role, so the block follows both themes.

Accessibility

Structure

An article with one heading for the page and one per section, in order, so a reader can move by heading. Each section heading carries its anchor as id, so a link can open it.

Content

  • The date reads "Last updated 2 November 2026".
  • Scripture is quoted word for word with its reference; the statement of faith is the owner's text.
  • No page says that a business is verified (ADR 0057).
