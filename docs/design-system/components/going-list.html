Component · Data display

You’re going

The side column’s list of the upcoming events a member is going to: a heading with their number, then a row per event with its month over its date, its title, “{time} · {area}” and a chevron, the whole row a link to the event page.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newgoing-listL2-305

Overview

  • For a member only. A visitor sees no “You’re going”, only Running something? (L2-305 AC4).
  • In start order. An RSVP adds a row at once, and taking it back removes it (L2-304 AC2, AC3).
  • The rows lead to the event page, where a member going finds the address or the stream link.

Anatomy

  1. Heading.going-list__title, an h2.
  2. Count.going-list__count.
  3. Date.going-list__date: __month (“NOV”) over __day (“13”).
  4. Title.going-list__name, cut with an ellipsis.
  5. Time and area.going-list__meta: “7:30 PM · Trinity Bellwoods”, or “{time} · Streamed”.
  6. ChevronDecorative.

Sizes

One size: the 300 px side column from 1024 px, the width of the page under it.

States

A row underlines its title on hover and draws the focus ring on focus. A row that enters gets data-state="entering": a 180 ms fade and slide (--duration-base, --ease-enter), played only when motion is allowed.

Responsive behaviour

From 1024 px it sits in the side column, which stays in view as the page scrolls (Events layout); under 1024 px it follows the list. Title and meta are cut with an ellipsis, never wrapped.

Theming

Hairline dividers and muted meta in both themes.

Accessibility

Labelling

Each row is one link named “{title}, {day} at {time}” (“Worship Night on Ossington, Thursday, Nov 13 at 7:30 PM”); its drawn date and text are aria-hidden, so the name is read once and in full.

Touch

Each row is at least 44 px tall.

Motion

A row entering slides down and fades in; under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce it only fades (L2-305 AC2).

Content

  • Heading: “You’re going” with the number.
  • Meta: “{time} · {area}”, or “{time} · Streamed” for a stream alone.
  • Empty: “RSVP to an event and it shows up here with its address or stream link.”
