Quantity stepper

A whole number from a minimum to a maximum, with “One fewer” and “One more” around it.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newquantity-stepperL2-265L2-266

Overview

  • Beside “Add to bag” (56 px) and on each bag line (--sm, 44 px). The maximum is 10 or what is left, whichever is lower.

Anatomy

  1. Grouprole="group" named “Quantity” (on a bag line, “Quantity of Men’s L”).
  2. Buttons“One fewer” and “One more”, 44 px wide.
  3. ValueAn output, announced as it changes.

Sizes

Default 56 px tall; --sm 44 px. Both buttons are 44 × 44 px or larger.

States

Button: default, hover, focus (an inset ring), disabled at the minimum or maximum.

Responsive behaviour

It keeps its width; the button beside it takes the rest of the row.

Theming

Surface, edge and text follow the theme.

Both themes

1
1

Accessibility

The buttons are named; the value is an output with aria-live="polite", so “2” is announced after “One more”. A disabled button says the limit was reached.
