Launch confirmation

The launch confirmation takes a form’s place once it is sent. It says that the details arrived and what happens next, and it takes focus so that nobody is left on a form that is gone.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="status"Marketing siteL2-216L2-217

Overview

Use it when the API answers 202 to one of the two launch forms. It stands exactly where the form stood, in the same tone, and stays until the page is loaded again.

  • It is not a toast and not an alert: it does not go away, and the reader does not have to dismiss it.
  • It has no action. There is nothing to do next but wait for the e-mail.
  • It says the same thing whether or not the address had signed up before, so nobody can learn from it whether an address is on the list (L2-217 AC5).

Anatomy

  1. Icon.mkt-confirmation__icon: a 40px green circle with a paper check mark. Decoration; the check is aria-hidden="true".
  2. Title.mkt-confirmation__title: an <h3> in Archivo bold at the wide width, --font-size-2xl. It says what happened.
  3. Text.mkt-confirmation__text: one or two sentences, 17px, saying what happens next. It names the address the reader gave; a long address wraps.
  4. Panel.mkt-confirmation: the same panel as the form (--radius-lg, the hairline ring, the same padding), with role="status" and tabindex="-1".

Two tones, matching the form it replaces.

Sizes

One size. The panel fills the form’s column and is as tall as its content; its padding steps at 640px as the form’s does.

States

One state: shown. It has no hover, active, busy or disabled state, and no close button. The examples above are that state in both tones.

  • On arrival it replaces the form in the same place and takes focus. A screen reader reads the title and the text; a sighted keyboard user sees the focus ring around the panel.
  • After that it is plain text. Tab moves on to the next link on the page; the panel is not in the tab order.
  • The other form on the page is untouched and can still be sent.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 640px the padding is --space-5; from 640px it is --space-8.
  • From 1024px it stands beside the section’s heading, in the form’s column. It is shorter than the form was, and stays at the top of the column (align-content: start).
  • Icon, title and text stack at every width, aligned to the start.
  • An e-mail address longer than the panel breaks across lines (overflow-wrap: anywhere) and never widens the page.

Theming

The marketing site is light only: marketing.css defines no dark values and the page sets data-theme="light" on <body class="mkt">. Every example on this page stands in an element with class="mkt" and data-theme="light", so it looks the same whichever theme these docs are in.

The icon is the page’s heading accent, --jb-mkt-accent (--color-success-solid), with the check in --jb-mkt-paper. The text is the text colour at 70%.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="status": a polite live region, since it reports that something the reader did has succeeded. It also has tabindex="-1" so that focus can be placed on it by script; without that, focus would fall back to the top of the page when the form is removed (2.4.3).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves from the confirmation to the next link or field on the page. The confirmation itself is skipped when tabbing back.

Focus

Focus moves to the panel as soon as it is shown. The shared 2px ring shows around it. It is not scrolled out from under the reader: the panel is where the form was.

Labelling

The title is an <h3> under the section’s <h2>, so the heading order of the page holds. The check mark is hidden from assistive technology.

Colour is not the carrier

The green circle and the check say “done” to the eye; the title says it in words: “You’re on the list.”, “Thank you. We have your details.”

Contrast

The text is the text colour at 70%: 7.3:1 on the surface panel and 7.0:1 on the paper panel, against a 4.5:1 minimum.

Motion

None. The panel appears; it does not slide or fade.

Content

The copy is fixed by L2-216 AC5 and L2-217 AC4. {email} is the address the reader typed.

AfterTitleText
Launch notificationYou’re on the list.We’ll e-mail {email} the day Jubilee launches, and not before.
Business pre-registrationThank you. We have your details.The Jubilee team will be in touch at {email} before launch. Your business is not listed or verified yet: every business is verified before it appears.
  • The title says what happened; the text says what happens next and what has not happened. An owner must not leave thinking the business is listed.
  • No date and no number of days. “The day Jubilee launches” and “before launch” are all Jubilee can promise.
  • Nothing here says Jubilee is open, and nothing promises customers or growth. Jubilee never promises prosperity (docs/scripture.md, “What Jubilee never says”).
  • No “Welcome” and no “You’re in”: sending a form creates no account.

Do and don't

  • Do move focus to it and keep it in the form’s place.
  • Don’t show it as a toast, and don’t add a close button, a “Send another” or a link to the app.
