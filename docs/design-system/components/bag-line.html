Bag line

One line of the bag: the shirt small, its name, fit and size, the line’s amount, the quantity and “Remove”, and what is wrong with it.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newbag-lineL2-266L2-267

Overview

  • In the bag dialog, one per fit and size, in a List.

Anatomy

  1. ShirtThe front of the tee, 56 px, hidden from assistive technology.
  2. Name and meta“Purpose Tee · Black”, “Men’s L · $25 each”.
  3. AmountUnit price × quantity.
  4. ControlsA small Quantity stepper and “Remove”.
  5. ProblemWhat stops checkout, with the alert icon: “Only 1 left in Men’s 3XL. Lower the quantity to check out.”

Sizes

One size.

States

Default; with a problem. “Remove” has hover and focus.

Responsive behaviour

Three columns at every width; the controls wrap under the name.

Theming

Follows the theme; the shirt does not.

Accessibility

The stepper is named for its line (“Quantity of Men’s L”) and “Remove” too (“Remove Men’s L”). The problem is a status, in words with an icon.
