Stepper

Two numbered components share this page. The stepper shows where a person is in a multi-step task; the steps list explains a process in three numbered beats, as on the Welcome page.

Overview

  • Stepper (ol.stepper): a horizontal progress indicator for a task split across screens. Steps are upcoming, current (aria-current="step"), complete (data-complete) or in error (data-error). It is in the kit but no mock uses it: List your business is one long form today. The example below shows how it would read if that form is split into four steps.
  • Steps (ol.steps): a static, numbered explainer with gold numerals, a heading and a sentence per step. Used once, in "How Jubilee works" on pages/welcome. Not interactive and not progress.

A stepper is not navigation between sections; use Tabs for that. A list of past events is a Timeline.

Anatomy

  1. Complete markerli[data-complete]::before: "✓" on --color-success-solid, --color-fg-inverse mark.
  2. Current markerli[aria-current="step"]::before: the number on --color-accent with --color-accent-border; label semibold and --color-fg-default.
  3. Upcoming markerCounter in a --avatar-size-sm circle with a --border-width-thick --color-border-strong ring.
  4. Label<span> in --text-label, muted. Below 768px only the current label shows.

Sizes

The stepper has one size (--avatar-size-sm markers). The steps list uses the larger --avatar-size-md numeral with --text-h4 because it is a page section, not a control.

States

Stepper items are status, not controls: they have no hover, focus, active or disabled states. If completed steps become links back to their screen, they take the Link states. The states are:

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px the stepper hides every label except the current one (visually hidden, still read), so four markers and one label fit 360px. Pair it with a "Step 2 of 4" caption.
  • The steps list is one column below 768px and three columns (.steps grid) from 768px.

Theming

Upcoming rings and labels flip with the theme; the current marker stays gold with an ink numeral; complete uses --color-success-solid with --color-fg-inverse, which flips to a light green with a dark mark in dark. The steps numerals stay gold with ink text.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An ordered list (<ol>) with an aria-label; the current item has aria-current="step". No APG widget pattern applies; status that is only conveyed by the marker is repeated as visually hidden text (", complete", ", needs attention"). For a split form, each screen also follows the APG landmark and form guidance, and the step heading says "Step 2 of 4: What you offer".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabNot a stop: the stepper is not interactive. Focus goes to the form.
—If completed steps are links, Tab reaches them and Enter follows.

Focus

None on the stepper. When a step loads, focus moves to its heading.

Labelling

The list is labelled ("Listing progress"); counters are generated by CSS, so the position is given by the list itself ("list, 4 items") and by the step heading.

Motion

None. Moving between steps is a page change.

Touch

Not a touch target. Completed steps that become links need the full item as the target, at least 44px tall.

Content

  • Stepper labels are two or three words naming the screen: Business details, What you offer, Photos, Review.
  • Three to five steps; more means the task should be simplified.
  • Steps headings are short promises ("Ask, book or order"), each with one sentence underneath.
