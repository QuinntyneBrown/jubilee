Link

Links move a member to another page or place. They are underlined text in Jubilee's link blue; anything that changes data is a button instead.

Overview

Use a link when activating it only navigates: “Forgot password?”, “View request”, “Back to browse”, a category in a business kicker. Use a Button when it changes something, and the .btn--link button when an action must look like a link (“Retry”). Navigation that should look like a button is <a class="btn">. The link under the Log in and Sign up forms has its own look, .auth-link; see Auth layout.

  • Inline sits in running text and inherits the type size; it is always underlined so it is not identified by colour alone (1.4.1).
  • Back link (.back-link) opens detail pages: muted, with a left arrow, 44px tall.
  • Standalone (.link--standalone) ends a card or section (“View request”, “See all”) with a small chevron.
  • Muted (.link--muted) is for low-priority links in captions and the footer; still underlined.
  • External links to a business's own site add the external icon and tell screen readers it opens a new tab.

Anatomy

  1. LabelThe words that describe the destination; the accessible name. Colour --color-fg-link.
  2. Underline1px (--border-width-hairline) at a 0.15em offset; thickens to --border-width-thick on hover.

States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state. Jubilee does not style :visited: members revisit the same businesses and the colour change carried no meaning. Active keeps the hover look. Links are never disabled; when the destination is not available, show plain text.

Responsive behaviour

  • Inline links wrap with their sentence; long URLs break with overflow-wrap: anywhere in alerts and messages.
  • Back links stay at the top-left of the content column at every width; on phones they are the only way back besides the tab bar, so they never hide.
  • Standalone links in a section head move under the heading when the row wraps (.section-head).

Theming

Links read --color-fg-link and --color-fg-link-hover. Dark mode lightens both to stay above 4.5:1 on the dark surfaces. Muted and back links use --color-fg-muted and turn --color-fg-default on hover. The footer sets color: inherit so its links match its muted text.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <a href>. Never a span with a click handler, and never an <a> without href. See the APG link pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the link.
EnterFollows it. Space scrolls the page, as browsers do.

Focus

The shared 2px ring with a 2px offset and --radius-sm corners; gold on the brand band.

Labelling

The link text names the destination out of context (“View request”, “Back to browse”), never “click here” or “more”. Icons are aria-hidden; “opens in a new tab” is visually hidden text inside the link.

Motion

No transition on inline links; the hover thickening is instant. Back links transition colour over --duration-fast, zeroed under reduced motion.

Touch

Back links are 44px tall (--target-comfortable) and standalone links 24px (--target-min). Inline links are exempt from 2.5.8 as part of a sentence, but two links never sit closer than a word apart.

Content

  • Describe the destination: “View request”, “Statement of faith”. Match the page title it opens where possible.
  • Back links say where they go: “Back to browse”, “Back to requests”, not just “Back”.
  • Link the meaningful words of a sentence, not the whole sentence and not “here”.
  • Show a business website without the protocol (“luzandre.com”).
