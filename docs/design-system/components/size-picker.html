Size picker

The size of a piece of merchandise: the legend with what is missing, the “Size guide” toggle, the sizes with their stock, the line under them, and the guide.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newsize-pickerL2-264L2-265
  • Overview
  • Anatomy

Overview

  • Under the fit. The sizes are those of the chosen fit; a youth size shows its ages.
  • A size with no stock is disabled, struck through and named “sold out”.

Anatomy

  1. Legend“Size”, and “Pick a size to continue” with the alert icon when the buyer added with no size.
  2. Guide toggleA button with aria-expanded that shows the guide.
  3. SizesChips around native radios, 56 px tall, 3 to a row on a phone and 6 from 640 px.
  4. Line“Only 2 left in Women’s XL.” at 3 or fewer, otherwise the chest and length.
  5. GuideA table of chest and length for the fit, in inches.

Sizes

One size.

States

Size: default, hover (ink edge), chosen (filled ink, --color-bg-inverse), sold out (disabled, struck through, --color-fg-disabled, “sold out” in its name), focus. Guide toggle: closed, open (the chevron turned).

Responsive behaviour

3 columns under 640 px, 6 from it. The guide scrolls sideways inside its box under 320 px rather than the page.

Theming

Chosen is ink on cream in light and cream on ink in dark (the inverse pair).

Both themes

Size

Men’s L: 22″ chest, 30″ long.

Size

Men’s L: 22″ chest, 30″ long.

Accessibility

Native radios: the arrow keys move between sizes and skip the sold-out ones. The fieldset is described by the line under the sizes, which is announced as it changes. The nudge is an alert inside the legend. Sold out is said by the word, the line through and the disabled state, not by grey alone.

Content

  • “Only {n} left in {fit} {size}.”, “{fit} {size}: {chest}″ chest, {length}″ long.”
  • “Pick a size to continue”
  • The guide’s caption: “{fit}, garment laid flat, in inches.” and the fit’s note.
