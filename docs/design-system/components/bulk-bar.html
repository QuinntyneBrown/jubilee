Bulk bar

The bar that floats at the foot of an admin table while rows are selected: the count, "Set status", Export, Delete and Clear, and the confirmation that lists what will be deleted (L2-278, L2-281).

Overview

When rows of a Records table are selected, .bulk-bar floats at the foot of the screen with how many are selected and what can be done to all of them: "Set status", Export, Delete and Clear (L2-278). It is an ink pill so it reads as a different layer from the table. Delete opens a confirmation that lists the records to delete (.doomed).

Variants

Delete confirmation

Delete opens an alert dialog that names the records (.doomed: name and ID per line), warns when other records point to them, and asks for delete to be typed when there is more than one. Deleting one record from its row menu asks without the typing.

Delete 2 listing reports?

This removes them for everyone on Jubilee. The deletion is kept in Audit records, but the data can’t be brought back after 30 days.

Sizes

One size. Its buttons are --control-height-sm tall; the clear button is round.

States

The bar shows while at least one row is selected and goes when the selection clears. Its buttons have default, hover (a 12% tint of the text) and focus; "Set status" is aria-expanded="true" while its menu is open.

After a delete the selection clears, the bar goes and a Toast says "2 listing reports deleted" with Undo.

Responsive behaviour

  • The bar centres at the foot of the viewport with --space-4 on each side, at every width.
  • Below 640px Export hides; the count, "Set status", Delete and Clear stay.
  • The table screen (admin-page--wide) keeps --space-24 free below its last row so the bar never covers it.

Theming

The bar is the inverse ink in both themes (--color-bg-inverse, --color-fg-inverse); Delete is --color-danger-icon-inverse, checked as text.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A region named for its table ("Selected listing reports"), after the table in reading order. "Set status" is a menu button. The confirmation is an alertdialog with its title and description, focus on the typing field.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFrom the table to the bar, through its buttons.
Enter, Space, ↓ on Set statusOpens the menu with its first status focused; Esc closes it.
Esc in the dialogKeeps the records; focus returns to Delete.

Labelling

  • The count is text, so the region says "2 selected".
  • Clear is named "Clear selection".
  • The dialog's confirm button says what it does: "Delete 2 listing reports".

Motion

None of its own; the menu and dialog take theirs from Menu and Dialog.

Content

  • "{n} selected"; never "items".
  • "Set status" lists the statuses of the table, as words.
  • The confirmation names the kind and the number ("Delete 2 listing reports?"), says what is kept ("kept in Audit records") and what is lost ("can’t be brought back after 30 days").
  • The way out is "Keep them".
