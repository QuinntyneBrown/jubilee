Rental facts

The facts of a space on its page: the key facts grid, the rental rows (Available, Minimum stay, Neighbourhood, Rent), what's included and the house rules.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newL2-196

Overview

Four parts, each left out when the space has nothing for it (L2-196 AC3): .space-facts (up to four key facts), the Description list as .dl--rows, .included-list and .house-rules. Use them only on the space page; elsewhere a space shows its facts through the Space card.

Anatomy

  1. Key facts.space-facts with .space-facts__item: a ruled grid, two across on a phone and four from 640px.
  2. Rental rows.dl.dl--rows: each pair in a <div>, term left in muted text, value right, ruled below; two columns from 640px.

Variants

  • Wi-Fi
  • Furnished
  • Parking
  • Private bath
What’s included: .included-list with a check icon per .included-list__item.
HTML
<ul class="included-list"><li class="included-list__item"><svg …/>Wi-Fi</li><li class="included-list__item"><svg …/>Furnished</li><li class="included-list__item"><svg …/>Parking</li><li class="included-list__item"><svg …/>Private bath</li></ul>

House rules

  • Check-in after 3 pm
  • No parties
  • No smoking
.house-rules: one rule a line, each with a rule on its left. Not the marketing rule-list, which has headings.
HTML
<ul class="house-rules"><li class="house-rules__item">Check-in after 3 pm</li><li class="house-rules__item">No parties</li><li class="house-rules__item">No smoking</li></ul>

Sizes

One size. The parts follow the body type scale.

States

Static content with no interactive state; a part with no data is left out rather than shown empty.

Responsive behaviour

  • Key facts: two columns, four from 640px.
  • Rental rows: one column, two from 640px; values align right.
  • Included and rules sit side by side from 640px (form-grid--2) and stack below.
  • Queen bed
  • Private bath
  • Sleeps 2
  • Self check-in

Theming

The facts grid draws its rules from --color-border-default showing through a hairline gap on --color-bg-canvas cells. Checks use --color-success-icon; rules use --color-border-strong.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Lists are lists (<ul>) and the rows are a native <dl>; <div> wrappers around each pair are valid HTML and keep the term–value relationship. The check icons are decorative (aria-hidden); the item text carries the meaning.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabNothing here is focusable.

Focus

None.

Labelling

Each part sits under its heading: the space ("Guest suite"), "What’s included", "House rules".

Content

  • Key facts: short noun phrases, up to four, 60 characters at most: "1 bedroom", "Separate entrance".
  • Rent row: "$120 per night", "$1,850 per month".
  • Included items come only from the fixed set of L2-190 AC3.
  • House rules: one per line, up to eight.
