Launch badge

The launch badge is the label “Launching soon” above the marketing page’s heading. It is the first thing the page says: Jubilee is not open yet.

Overview

Use it once, in the opening of the marketing page, above the heading (L2-215 AC1). It is text, not a control: it is not a link and not a button.

  • Its holder, .mkt-hero__eyebrow, is a flex row with --space-5 below it, so the pill is as wide as its words and not as wide as the column.
  • The “Launching soon” in the top bar is a different thing: a Marketing button in its pill variant that moves the page to the launch notification form.
  • It is not the app’s Badge. That one carries a status or a count inside the app; this one belongs to the marketing site and has its own block, mkt-badge.
  • In Angular it is jb-launch-badge, an attribute on a <p> placed in the eyebrow slot of the heading.

Anatomy

  1. Dot.mkt-badge__dot: an 8px gold circle with a thin ink ring. Decoration, hidden from assistive technology with aria-hidden="true".
  2. LabelThe words “Launching soon”, 13px semibold, in the text colour. They carry the state.
  3. Pill.mkt-badge: a quarter-strength gold tint on the paper, --radius-full, with a hairline ring drawn as a shadow.

Variants

One badge. It has no tone, no icon and no link.

Sizes

One size: 13px text on a 1.5 line with --space-1 above and below, about 28px tall. It is not a target, so the 44px rule does not apply to it.

States

The badge has one state. It is not interactive, so it has no hover, focus, active or disabled state, and it does not animate: the dot does not pulse.

Launching soon

Toronto’s Christian businesses, in one place.

In the opening, above the heading.
HTML
<div class="mkt-hero__eyebrow"><p class="mkt-badge"><span class="mkt-badge__dot" aria-hidden="true"></span>Launching soon</p></div><h1 class="mkt-hero__title">Toronto’s Christian businesses, <span class="mkt-accent">in one place.</span></h1>

When Jubilee opens the badge is removed with the rest of the launching-soon state (ADR 0044). It is never restyled to say something else.

Responsive behaviour

  • The same size at every width, from 360px up. The label is two words and never wraps.
  • It stays at the start of the text column: left on a phone and on the desktop, where the opening’s text stands beside the glimpse of the app.
  • The space under it is --space-5 at every width.

Theming

The marketing site is light only: marketing.css defines no dark values and the page sets data-theme="light" on <body class="mkt">. Every example on this page stands in an element with class="mkt" and data-theme="light", so it looks the same whichever theme these docs are in.

The fill is --color-accent mixed to 25% over the paper, and the ring is the text colour at 10%. Both are made with color-mix() from tokens; no colour is written as a value.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A paragraph. It comes before the <h1> in the source, so a screen reader reads “Launching soon” and then the heading, as a sighted reader does. It has no role, no live region and no aria-label.

Keyboard

None. It is not in the tab order and takes no focus.

Colour is not the carrier

The state is the words. The gold dot and the tint add nothing a reader needs, which is why the dot is aria-hidden. In forced colours the tint and the rings are dropped and the words remain.

Contrast

The label is the text colour on a tint that lies between the paper and full gold, so its ratio lies between the two pairs below; it works out at 15.4:1 against the 4.5:1 minimum.

Label on the paper
Label on full gold

Zoom

Sizes are in rem. At 200% zoom and at 320px wide the pill still fits on one line.

Content

  • The label is “Launching soon”, exactly. Sentence case, no full stop.
  • No date, no countdown and no “Coming soon”, “Beta” or “New”. Jubilee does not name a day it cannot keep.
  • Never a label that implies Jubilee is open: no “Now open”, “Live” or “Open for business” (L2-215 AC5).
  • Never a promise. Jubilee does not say that listing or joining will make anyone prosper (docs/scripture.md, “What Jubilee never says”); the badge states a fact about Jubilee and nothing about the reader.

Do and don't

Tokens

PartTokenValue (light)
Fill (25% over the paper)--color-accent
Dot--color-accent
Label, and the rings at 10% and 25%--color-fg-default
Radius--radius-full
Padding / gap--space-1 / --space-3 / --space-2 / /
Label weight--font-weight-semibold
Space below (the holder)--space-5

The 13px label, the 8px dot and the 10px left padding are written in rem in marketing.css; they follow the Magic Patterns marketing reference’s scale and have no token.

<div class="mkt-hero__eyebrow"><p class="mkt-badge"><span class="mkt-badge__dot" aria-hidden="true"></span>Launching soon</p></div>

Class / attributeEffect
