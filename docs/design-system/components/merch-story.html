Merchandise story

Why the print says what it says: a heading with its accent word, and the passage it rests on.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newmerch-storyL2-263

Overview

  • At the foot of the Purpose Tee page, after the purchase column, divided from it by a rule.
  • The verse is quoted word for word in the WEB with its reference; the sentence about what a sale funds is its own paragraph, nowhere near the price.

Anatomy

  1. Titleh2, Archivo bold at 110%, 30 px and 36 px from 640 px, one accent word.
  2. BodyParagraphs at --text-body-lg, relaxed.

Variants

Why “purpose” is the gold word.

“But you shall remember Yahweh your God, for it is he who gives you power to get wealth, that he may establish his covenant which he swore to your fathers, as it is today.” (Deuteronomy 8:18, WEB) Prospering isn’t the goal on its own. The ability is a gift, and it is given for a purpose.

Every shirt sold helps keep Jubilee free for the Christian business people of Toronto, and gives you an easy way to tell someone what it’s for.

Sizes

One size.

States

None.

Responsive behaviour

Stacked under 1024 px; from it the title takes 1 part and the body 1.4.

Theming

The accent word follows --color-fg-accent.

Content

  • Quote Scripture word for word, with its reference and translation (docs/scripture.md).
  • Never place the verse beside a price or a call to pay, and promise nothing about prospering.
