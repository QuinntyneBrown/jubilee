Spinner

A spinner says "working on it" for a wait of a second or a few, when there is nothing to measure: confirming an e-mail link, sending a request, signing out. It is always beside words that say what is happening.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableDecorative, aria-hiddenAlso inside every busy button

Overview

Use a spinner for a short, unmeasurable wait triggered by the person. A Button with aria-busy="true" draws its own spinner, which covers most cases. Use a Skeleton when a page or list is loading, and a Progress bar when you can count.

  • Standalone with a heading, on an interstitial screen (Confirming your e-mail…).
  • Inline beside a short status sentence.
  • In a button: drawn by .btn[aria-busy], not placed by hand.

Anatomy

  1. ArcTop border in --color-accent-border (gold-600 light, gold-400 dark), at least 3:1 on cream and white.
  2. Track--border-width-thick ring in --color-border-default; decorative.

Variants

Sizes

Small 16px (--icon-size-sm) beside caption text; default 20px (--spinner-size) beside body text and inside busy buttons; large 40px (--avatar-size-md) on interstitial screens.

States

A spinner has one state, spinning. Hover, focus, active and disabled do not apply; it disappears when the wait ends and the result replaces it.

Signing out…
Signing out…
  • Show it after about 300ms; a wait shorter than that should flash nothing.
  • After ten seconds, add a sentence ("Still working. You can leave this page; we’ll e-mail you.") or offer Cancel.

Responsive behaviour

  • Sizes are fixed tokens and do not change with the viewport. Interstitial screens centre the large spinner in the auth card at every width.

Theming

The arc reads --color-accent-border, which is gold-600 on light surfaces and gold-400 on dark ones, so it keeps 3:1 in both themes. The track uses --color-border-default (ink 10% in light). A busy button draws its 20px spinner in its own label colour instead.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The spinner is decorative (aria-hidden="true"). Its meaning comes from text in a role="status" region, or from aria-busy="true" on the button. There is no APG spinner pattern; announce with a status message per WCAG 4.1.3 and the APG alert pattern for failures.

Keyboard

KeyAction
NoneNot focusable. While a button is busy it ignores activation but keeps focus.

Focus

Focus stays where it was. When the wait ends, focus moves only if the view changed (to the new heading).

Labelling

The text beside the spinner says what is happening: "Confirming your e-mail…". Never "Loading…" alone when you know what is loading.

Motion

Rotates once per --duration-spin. It keeps turning under reduced motion because it is status, not decoration; the rotation is steady and small (WCAG 2.3.3 allows it).

Touch

Not interactive.

Content

  • Present progressive with an ellipsis: "Confirming your e-mail…", "Signing out…", "Sending your request to WaterWorks…".
  • Name the business or thing when you can.
