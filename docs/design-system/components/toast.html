Toast

A toast is a short, temporary message about something that just happened: a save, a sent request, a new message, a failure. It rises from the bottom on an ink card, waits eight seconds (forever if it is an error) and never steals focus.

Overview

Use a toast for the outcome of an action the person just took, or for news that arrives while they are elsewhere in the app, when the result does not need its own place on the page. Use an Alert and banner when the message belongs to the content (a form error summary, a request's status); use an banner when the condition lasts (offline, e-mail not confirmed); use a Dialog when the person must decide before going on.

  • Info tells about something that happened without the person: "Directory updated", "Trevor is away until Thursday".
  • Success confirms the person's own action: "Saved Legacy Financial", "Request cancelled". It usually carries Undo.
  • Warning says an action is going ahead with a catch: "Still sending your request", "Photo is large".
  • Danger says an action failed and nothing changed: "Couldn’t save Legacy Financial". It never auto-dismisses and is announced assertively.
  • With action adds one text button that does the obvious next thing: Open conversation, Add to calendar, Try again.

Anatomy

  1. Tone icon20px (--icon-size-lg), coloured with the --color-*-icon-inverse token for the tone so it holds 3:1 on the inverse card.
  2. Title--text-label; says what happened in five words or fewer.
  3. Text (optional)--text-body-sm at full opacity; one sentence of consequence or detail.
  4. Action (optional).toast__action: one underlined text button, inheriting the toast colour.
  5. Dismiss.close: a 24px icon button labelled "Dismiss".
  6. Timer bar.toast__progress: decorative countdown at the bottom edge, aria-hidden. Absent on danger toasts, which wait for the person.

Variants

Tone changes only the icon and its colour; the card is ink in light and cream in dark for every tone, with a 12px radius (--radius-md) and --shadow-3, so a toast always reads as a passing system message, not as page content.

Sizes

Toasts come in one size: full width minus 16px margins on phones, 24rem wide in the bottom-right corner from 640px up.

States

The toast itself is not interactive; its action and dismiss button are. Hovering or focusing anywhere in the toast pauses the timer. States are forced here with data-state.

  • Entering: slide-up over --duration-base (200ms) with --ease-enter.
  • Waiting: 8 seconds for info, success and warning, shown by the timer bar. Paused on :hover and :focus-within; restarts at the full 8 s when the pointer or focus leaves.
  • Leaving: data-state="leaving" plays fade-out over --duration-base, then the node is removed. Focus inside a leaving toast moves to the element that opened it, or to <main>.
  • Error: danger toasts stay until dismissed or until the retry succeeds, when they are replaced by a success toast.
  • Disabled does not exist: a toast never shows a control the person cannot use.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm) the region spans the viewport with --space-4 on each side and sits above the phone tab bar.
  • From 640px it is 24rem wide, --space-6 from the bottom-right corner, so it never covers the primary action of a detail page.
  • .toast-region--top moves it to the top, for the rare page whose bottom edge holds a sticky composer (the conversation view).
  • Text wraps; long names break with overflow-wrap: anywhere. Nothing truncates.

Theming

The toast reads three component tokens: --toast-bg (--color-bg-inverse), --toast-fg (--color-fg-inverse) and --toast-icon, which each tone sets to its --color-*-icon-inverse token. The inverse surface flips with the theme: ink-950 in light, ink-50 cream in dark.

.toast--success { --toast-icon: var(--color-success-icon-inverse); }

The inverse icon tokens exist because the surface icon tokens (--color-success-icon and friends) are tuned for light backgrounds and dropped to about 1.8:1 on the cream dark-theme toast.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The region is a live region: <div class="toast-region" role="status" aria-live="polite">, present in the page from load and empty until a toast is inserted. Danger toasts are announced through a second, assertive region (role="alert") rather than a role="alert" nested inside the polite one. See the APG alert pattern; WCAG 4.1.3 Status messages.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the action, then Dismiss, in visual order, after the page content that precedes the region. Toasts never take focus on arrival.
Enter / SpaceActivates the focused action or Dismiss.
EscDismisses the toast that contains focus.

Focus

Toasts never move focus. Inside the inverse card the ring uses currentColor (cream on ink, ink on cream), so it keeps the same 4.5:1 as the text.

Labelling

The title and text are the announcement, so they read as a complete sentence without the icon. The dismiss button is labelled "Dismiss"; the action is labelled by its text and must make sense out of context ("Open conversation", not "View").

Motion

Enter and leave use --duration-base, which reduced motion collapses to 0.01ms, so toasts appear and disappear without sliding. The timer bar is decorative and stops with the rest. WCAG 2.2.1: the 8 s timer pauses on hover and focus, and errors never time out.

Touch

Dismiss and the action are at least 24px (--target-min) and 8px apart. On phones the toast sits above the tab bar so it never covers a tab.

Content

  • Title in past tense for outcomes ("Request cancelled", "Conversation muted"); present tense for ongoing conditions ("Still sending your request").
  • Name the thing: "Saved Legacy Financial", not "Saved". Errors say what did not happen and what still holds: "Couldn’t cancel the request. It is still open."
  • Text is one sentence. No "Success!" or "Oops". No exclamation marks except in quoted messages.
  • Action labels are a verb and an object: "Open conversation", "Add to calendar", "Undo".
