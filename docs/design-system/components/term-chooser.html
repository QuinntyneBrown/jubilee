Term chooser

"How are you renting it?": the four terms of a space as radio cards on the space form.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: new useBuilt on Choice card (Radio group)

Overview

Shown first among the space fields of List your business and Edit listing. Long term is chosen by default. Changing the term keeps every value typed; only labels, choices and the household field change (L2-199 AC5).

The block is Choice card (Radio group); this page records how rooms and rentals uses it. Everything not said here follows that page.

As Choice card (Radio group).

Sizes

One size, the default of Choice card (Radio group).

States

The states of Choice card (Radio group): default, hover, focus, active, pressed or checked, disabled.

Responsive behaviour

One column on a phone, two from 640px.

Theming

Inherits Choice card (Radio group)'s tokens in both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native radio buttons in a <fieldset> with the legend "How are you renting it?" (APG radio group). The card is the label, so the whole card is the target.

KeyAction
TabMoves into the group, to the checked term.
↑ ↓ ← →Moves and checks the next term.

Focus

The ring is drawn around the card (:has(input:focus-visible)).

Labelling

Title and text are inside the label, so both are announced.

Content

  • Labels and lines exactly as L2-197 AC1: "Short stays", "Long term", "Rooms", "Roommates".
  • No icons: the four cards are told apart by their words.
