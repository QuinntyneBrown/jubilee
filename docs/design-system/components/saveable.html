Saveable card

A business, product or space card with its heart: the toggle that saves the item to the member's private saved list (L2-041).

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newWraps Business card and Space card

Overview

The block saveable wraps a business card, a product card or a space card on Browse, a category, search, a shop and the cards of a listing or product page. The card stays a link; the heart is a button beside it, never inside it, because a button in a link is one control to assistive technology (ADR 0054).

Directory rows and search rows have no heart; the page they open has one. The welcome page, the saved page and the owner's previews have none either.

Variants

One heart for every card. It sits at the top right of the picture, clear of the media tag and the space badge at the top left.

Sizes

One size: the heart is 44px, the default control height, on the phone and the desktop.

Responsive behaviour

The same at every width; on the narrow product cards of the shops' scroller (152px) the heart still leaves the picture's left half to the tag.

Theming

The heart's surface, border and icon are read by role, so it follows both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A toggle button (APG button, toggle) with aria-pressed. Saved is said three ways: the name, aria-pressed and the filled shape, so colour is never the only carrier.

KeyAction
TabMoves from the card's link to its heart, then to the next card.
Enter / SpaceSaves or removes the item; focus stays on the heart.

Focus

Stays on the heart. A visitor's press opens "Join to save {name}", which returns focus to the heart when it closes.

Labelling

Each heart names its item, so a list of hearts can be told apart.

Contrast

Motion

Colour transition and a small press; none with reduced motion.

Touch

The heart is a 44px target.

Content

  • Unsaved: "Save {name}".
  • Saved: "Saved. Remove {name} from saved".
  • The toasts are those of Toast: "Saved {name}. Find it any time under Saved." with "Undo".
