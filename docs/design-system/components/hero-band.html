Hero band

The hero is the ink band at the top of browse and welcome that greets the member, says what Jubilee is and gets them to a category in one tap. The CTA band is its small sibling inside a page. The detail head is the plain-surface heading block of a business or product page.

Overview

Use the hero once per page, only on browse (signed in or out) and welcome. Use the band for a single call to action inside a page ("New here? Join free" for signed-out visitors). Use the detail head at the top of every business, shop and product page. Everywhere else a page starts with a plain page header (see Top bar and Navigation and page structure).

  • Hero (.hero): full-bleed --color-bg-brand; greeting line, headline (the page's h1 in product; an h2 here because this page has its own), a count line, a phone-only search and a .chip-row of categories. The welcome variant swaps the search for Join free and Browse businesses.
  • Band (.band): a rounded ink block with a heading, one line of copy and one primary button.
  • Detail head (.detail-head): a kicker (verified check + category link), the name, the owner, the pitch, credential tags, the action row (primary + icon buttons) and the perk.

On the ink surfaces chips, ghost buttons, inputs and the focus ring re-map to on-brand tokens; gold stays gold.

Anatomy

  1. Greeting.hero__greeting: "Morning, Joanna" signed in; "Toronto’s Christian marketplace" signed out. --text-body-sm, on-brand muted.
  2. Headlineh1 (h2 in these docs), max 18ch, 5xl from 1024px.
  3. Sub.hero__sub: the live count of verified businesses.
  4. Search (phone only).search in a role="search" form, 48px pill input; hidden from 768px where the top bar has search.
  5. Category chips.chip-row of links; the current one is gold with aria-current="page". Scrolls sideways on phones, wraps from 768px.

Sizes

No size scale. The hero padding grows from --space-6/--space-8 to --space-10 at 1024px; the band and detail head have one size.

States

The surfaces are static; their controls carry the states. On the ink band chips use the on-brand subtle fill, hover deepens it, and the current chip turns gold. Real :focus-visible on the band uses --color-focus-ring-on-brand (gold in light, so it reads on ink).

Responsive behaviour

  • Hero: full bleed at every width; the content sits in .container with --layout-margin (16/32/48px). Padding is 24/32px on phones and 40/40px from 1024px, where the headline grows to --font-size-5xl.
  • The hero search is phone and small-tablet only (hidden from 768px); from 1024px the top bar shows its search, and between 768px and 1023px the top bar's search icon opens it.
  • The chip row scrolls sideways under 768px (no scrollbar, chips never wrap their labels) and wraps from 768px.
  • Band: wraps its copy above the button when narrow; the button never stretches.
  • Detail head: below 640px the primary action takes the full row and the icon buttons drop to the next row.

Theming

The hero and band use the brand group: --color-bg-brand, --color-fg-on-brand, --color-fg-on-brand-muted, --color-bg-on-brand-subtle, --color-border-on-brand and --color-focus-ring-on-brand. Ink stays ink in dark mode, so the band reads as the same brand moment in both themes.

/* Chips and ghost buttons re-map on the band */
.hero .chip, .band .chip { --chip-bg: var(--color-bg-on-brand-subtle); --chip-border: var(--color-border-on-brand); --chip-fg: var(--color-fg-on-brand); }
.hero .btn--ghost { --btn-fg: var(--color-fg-on-brand); --btn-border: var(--color-border-on-brand); --btn-bg-hover: var(--color-bg-on-brand-subtle); }

The detail head sits on the canvas and uses ordinary tokens; its kicker check is --color-success-icon.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The hero is a <section> labelled by its heading, which is the page's h1. The search is a <form role="search"> landmark (see APG landmark regions). Category chips are links in a list with aria-current="page" on the current one (APG link pattern).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSearch field, then each category chip in order, then the page. The chip row scrolls the focused chip into view.
EnterIn search: submits to search results. On a chip or a CTA: follows it.
Esc in searchClears the field (native type="search").

Focus

Inside .hero and .band the focus ring switches to --color-focus-ring-on-brand, which is above 3:1 on ink and on the on-brand subtle chip fill. The chip row pads by --space-1 so rings are not clipped.

Labelling

The greeting is plain text, not a heading. The search has a visually hidden label "Search businesses and products"; the placeholder gives examples. Detail-head icon buttons name their object: "Message Legacy Financial", "Save Legacy Financial" (aria-pressed), "Share Legacy Financial".

Motion

None on the surfaces. Chip and button transitions use --duration-fast, which reduced motion zeroes.

Touch

Chips on the band are 44px tall (--control-height-md) with 8px gaps; the hero search is 48px; the welcome CTAs are 48px; detail-head actions are 48px.

Content

  • Greeting: time of day and first name ("Morning, Joanna", "Evening, Joanna"); signed out, the tagline "Toronto’s Christian marketplace".
  • Headline: one sentence that says what Jubilee is for: "Do business with Toronto’s Christian community." Do not change it per campaign.
  • Sub: a live, honest count: "47 verified businesses — shops, advisors, trades and more." Never round up.
  • Band: a two-word question or statement as the heading ("New here?"), one line of benefit, one verb-phrase button ("Join free").
  • Detail head kicker: "Verified Christian business · Category"; the name exactly as verified; the pitch in the owner's words.
