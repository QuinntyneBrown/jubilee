RSVP button

The one action of an event: a toggle between gold “RSVP” and green “Going”, which a host sees as “You’re hosting”, a full event as “Full” and an event under way as “Happening now”. Large in the feature card and on the event page, default in a row.

Overview

  • A toggle button with aria-pressed: pressing “RSVP” makes it “Going” at once (optimistic), pressing “Going” takes the RSVP back (L2-304 AC2, AC3).
  • A visitor gets the same gold “RSVP” as a link to the sign-in prompt “Join to RSVP to {title}” (L2-304 AC4).
  • It is not a Button variant: its states (going, full, hosting, happening now) belong to an event, and it keeps a pill shape and a 44 px height at every size.

Anatomy

  1. IconCalendar-plus for RSVP, a check for Going, 16 px, decorative. Full, You’re hosting and Happening now have none.
  2. Label“RSVP”, “Going”, “Full”, “You’re hosting” or “Happening now”, with the title in a visually hidden span, so the accessible name is “RSVP to {title}”.

Responsive behaviour

The label never wraps. In a row it sits in its own column from 576 px and under the text below 576 px (Event row). The target is at least 44 px at every width.

Theming

RSVP is gold with ink text in both themes; Going is --color-success-solid with --color-fg-inverse (white on green in light, ink on light green in dark).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <button> with aria-pressed (APG toggle button). The label does not change its meaning when pressed: “RSVP” and “Going” both name the RSVP, and aria-pressed says whether it is made. A polite live region says “You’re going to {title}.” (L2-304 AC2).

Labelling

Every instance names its event: “RSVP to {title}”, “Going to {title}”, “{title} is full” (aria-label on the disabled button), “You’re hosting: {title}”.

Keyboard and touch

Enter or Space toggles. 44 px tall at the default size, 48 px large.

Content

  • “RSVP”, “Going”, “Full”, “You’re hosting”, “Happening now”: nothing else.
  • The count beside it is “{price} · {n} going”, or “{price} · Full” when it is full (L2-304 AC5).
  • Never say going brings a blessing or a reward (L2-312 AC6).
