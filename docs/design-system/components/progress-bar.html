Progress bar

The progress bar shows how far a task has got when the task takes long enough to notice: a photo uploading, a listing being set up. It is a pill-shaped 8px track with a gold fill, and it never stands without a label and a number.

Overview

Use a progress bar when the task has a known size and takes more than about two seconds: uploading a product photo, shrinking an attachment, completing the four parts of a business listing. Use a Spinner for a short wait with no measurable progress, and a Skeleton while a page's content loads. A Stepper shows named steps; a progress bar shows quantity.

  • Determinate: --value sets the fill; aria-valuenow says the same number.
  • Indeterminate (.progress--indeterminate): a 40% segment sweeps across while the size is unknown, such as while a file is being checked.
  • Success and danger recolour the fill at the end: complete, or stopped with an error.

Anatomy

  1. LabelNames the task; it is the bar's accessible name through aria-labelledby.
  2. Value text.text-caption: the number, in units the person understands.
  3. Fill.progress__bar, --color-accent, width from --value, animated over --duration-slow.
  4. Track.progress: --indicator-size high, --radius-full, --color-bg-subtle-hover.

Sizes

One height, <code>--indicator-size</code> (8px). It takes the width of its container.

States

The bar is not interactive, so hover, focus, active and disabled do not apply. Its states are its values.

  • On failure, keep the bar at the value it reached, turn it danger and put the reason and Try again in an Alert and banner or field error beside it.
  • On success, hold the full bar for about a second, then replace it with the result (the uploaded photo, a success alert).

Responsive behaviour

  • The bar stretches to its container at every width. At 360px the value text drops under the label if they do not fit on one row (the label row is a wrapping cluster).

Theming

Fill --color-accent (gold in both themes), track --color-bg-subtle-hover, success --color-success-solid, danger --color-danger-solid. Gold on the light track is low contrast by design: the text value carries the information, so the bar is supplementary (WCAG 1.4.11 does not require 3:1 for a redundant graphic).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" with aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax and aria-valuenow; omit aria-valuenow when indeterminate. Name it with aria-labelledby. See the APG meter pattern (the closest pattern) and the ARIA progressbar role.

Keyboard

KeyAction
NoneNot focusable. Any Cancel or Try again button sits next to it in the tab order.

Focus

No focus of its own.

Labelling

Label names the task ("Uploading walnut-bench.jpg"); add aria-valuetext when the number alone is unclear ("2.2 of 4.8 MB"). Announce only the start, the end and failures through a nearby status region; do not announce every percent.

Motion

Width transitions over --duration-slow; the indeterminate sweep loops over --duration-loop. Reduced motion zeroes the transition and replaces the sweep with a full, 60% opacity bar.

Touch

Not interactive.

Content

  • Label with the thing being done: "Uploading walnut-bench.jpg", not "Progress".
  • Value in human units: "2.2 of 4.8 MB", "3 of 4 parts done". Percent only when there is no better unit.
  • Failure text says what to do: "Upload stopped. Check your connection and try again."
