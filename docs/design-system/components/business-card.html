Business card

A business card is the whole tile a member taps to open a business or a product: photo, name, one-line pitch and the facts that decide the tap (a perk, the price, Verified). It is one link, laid out in a card grid, a feature grid or a scroller.

Overview

Use a business card wherever Jubilee shows businesses or products as a browsable set with pictures: Featured this week and From the shops on browse, category and search results, a shop's catalogue and the Saved page. For a dense, text-only list of every business use a Directory row; for a framed box of mixed content use a Card.

  • Business card (.biz-card): 4:3 media tile, name (h3), tagline clamped to two lines, a meta line with a perk and the Verified mark.
  • Product card (.biz-card.product-card): square media on a white tile, a 15px name, the seller in the tagline slot and a .price in CAD, set in the display font.
  • Saved card (.saved-card wrapper): either card with a small remove button pinned to the top-right corner, outside the link.
  • Layouts: .card-grid (2, 3 then 4 columns), .feature-grid (the first card spans the row below 1024px and a 2×2 block from there) and .scroller (one row that scrolls sideways with snap on phones and becomes a grid from 640px).

The card has no box: no border, no background, no padding. The media tile carries the edge, so cards sit on the cream canvas like a photo feed, not like a list of panels.

Anatomy

  1. Media tag (optional).media__tag: the category in one word (Financial, Shop, Home). Cream pill (--color-bg-canvas), 12px semibold, no shadow.
  2. Media.media 4:3 (square on product cards). A tonal stand-in in the mocks; a photo with alt text in product. See Media tile.
  3. Remove button (saved only).btn.btn--icon.btn--sm, a sibling of the link, never inside it.
  4. Name.biz-card__name, h3, --text-h4 at 16px, 18px from 640px (--text-body-md, 15px, on product cards). Clamped to two lines. Not underlined on hover.
  5. Tagline.biz-card__tagline, --text-body-sm (14px) in --color-fg-supporting, clamped to two lines. On product cards: the seller, --text-meta (13px) muted, on one line.
  6. Meta.biz-card__meta, --text-meta (13px): .perk (--color-fg-link green, semibold) and .verified (14px check icon + muted label). On product cards: .price, display font, 18px bold.

Variants

Pick the variant by what is listed; pick the layout by how many and how important.

Sizes

Cards have no size scale. Their width comes from the grid column or the scroller item width; the product card is the only smaller form.

States

The card is a link, so its states are the link's. Hover zooms the media image to --zoom-scale (1.03) over --duration-slow (300ms) inside the tile; the name is not underlined and nothing lifts. data-state="hover" forces the zoom. Focus draws the shared ring around the whole card, following its --radius-lg.

  • Active has no look of its own: the tap navigates at once. The mocks never style it; do not add a press effect that delays the page change.
  • Loading keeps the grid and swaps each card for a skeleton of the same shape (media, two text lines, a short third), with aria-hidden on the list and one role="status" line ("Loading the directory…") for screen readers. See Skeleton.
  • Removing a saved card sets aria-busy on its button; on success the card leaves the grid and a Toast offers Undo. An empty Saved page is an Empty state, not an empty grid.
  • Long names wrap (overflow-wrap: anywhere) onto a second line and clamp there, as the tagline does. Never cut a name to one line: "Adrianne Sequeira, DMin., RP, RMFT, RN" needs both.

Responsive behaviour

  • Card grid: 2 columns below 640px, 3 to 1023px, 4 from 1024px. Cards never go to 1 column; at 360px each card is about 152px wide and the name wraps.
  • Feature grid: the first card spans the full row below 1024px (the rest sit 2 across from 640px), then takes 2 columns × 2 rows of 4 with a stretched media tile.
  • Scroller: items are 44% of the row below 640px, where it scrolls sideways and bleeds to the viewport edge by --layout-margin; --space-1 of padding above keeps focus rings from being clipped. From 640px it is a 3-column grid, 5 columns from 1024px, and does not scroll.
  • The saved remove button stays 36px in the corner at every width; it is the only control on the card.

Theming

Cards set no colour of their own: the name and meta inherit --color-fg-default, the tagline is --color-fg-supporting (--color-fg-muted on product cards), the perk is --color-fg-link and the verified check is --color-success-icon. Green perk text on the cream page is 4.07:1 in light, below 4.5:1: this follows the design reference, is recorded in tokens/contrast-pairs.json, and prefers-contrast: more restores 4.5:1. The media tile and its tag carry the surfaces. Switching theme changes those semantic tokens and nothing on .biz-card.

The saved remove button re-maps the button's component tokens (--btn-bg: --color-bg-surface-glass, --btn-border: transparent) and adds --shadow-1 so it reads on any media tone, including ink.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each card is one native <a href> containing the heading, so the whole tile is one target and one tab stop. Grids and scrollers are <ul> lists. See the APG link pattern. The saved remove button is a sibling of the link (button pattern); interactive content is never nested inside the card link.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves card to card in DOM order (row by row). On a saved card, the remove button follows its card.
EnterOpens the business or product.
SpaceActivates the remove button. On a card link it scrolls the page, as links do.
Shift+scroll, trackpad, swipeScrolls a scroller sideways. Keyboard users do not need it: focusing a card scrolls it into view.

Focus

The shared ring (2px --color-focus-ring, 2px offset) wraps the whole card and follows --radius-lg. Scrollers and grids pad by --space-1 so the ring is never clipped by overflow.

Labelling

The link's name is its text: tag, name, tagline and meta, in that order ("Financial Legacy Financial Build a Legacy… Free first meeting Verified"). Keep taglines to one sentence so the name stays short. The media icon is aria-hidden; a real photo gets alt="" inside the card because the name already says what it is. Remove buttons name the item: "Remove Legacy Financial from saved".

Motion

On hover the media image scales to --zoom-scale over --duration-slow with --ease-standard; reduced motion sets the scale to 1. Scroll snapping is proximity, never mandatory, so it does not fight the person. Skeleton shimmer uses --duration-loop, which reduced motion zeroes.

Touch

The whole card is the target (at least 152 × 200px at 360px). The saved remove button is 36px with its own corner, well above the 24px minimum, and does not overlap the link's name or text.

Content

  • Name: the business's legal or trading name as verified, exactly as the owner wrote it, credentials included. Never shortened.
  • Tagline: the business's own one-line pitch in their words ("Treating Every Customer As Family"). Two lines maximum; anything longer is cut by the clamp, so put the point first.
  • Perk: one concrete reason to tap, in sentence case, 2–4 words: "Free first meeting", "Emergency calls", "Ships across Canada". No exclamation marks, no prices.
  • Media tag: the category in one word (Financial, Shop, Home, Health). Leave it off product cards.
  • Price: CAD with no currency code ("$34"); add "ea" for per-item minimums ("$22 ea"). Never "From $" unless the product page explains the range.
