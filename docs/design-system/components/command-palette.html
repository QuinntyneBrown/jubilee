Overview

⌘K (Ctrl+K), the jump in the Admin shell, opens .palette: one field that finds a table by its name or group and up to six records by name, e-mail or ID across every table the staff member may read (L2-273). It is a modal dialog holding a combobox and its listbox; choosing an option opens it.

Sizes

One size: up to 36rem wide, 12vh from the top of the viewport; the list scrolls past 22.5rem.

Focus stays in the field; the active option is the one aria-activedescendant names, shown by its background and the Enter mark, not by a focus ring.

Responsive behaviour

  • The panel is the viewport's width less --space-4 each side up to 36rem.
  • Below 768px the round search button of the bar opens it; the shortcut works at every width with a keyboard.
  • A record's meta truncates first (at most 45% of the row), then its name.

Theming

The panel is --color-bg-surface-raised with --shadow-4 over --color-bg-backdrop; the active option is --color-bg-canvas.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A modal dialog named "Jump to" holding the combobox pattern (list autocomplete): the field is role="combobox" with aria-controls and aria-activedescendant; the list is a listbox of option links. The kind mark is aria-hidden; the meta says the kind in words.

Keyboard

KeyAction
⌘+K / Ctrl+KOpens the palette with the field focused.
↓ ↑Moves the active option.
EnterOpens the active table or record.
EscCloses; focus returns to what opened it.

Labelling

  • The field is named "Jump to a table or record"; its placeholder shows what may be typed and is not its label.
  • The number of results is announced through the listbox; "Nothing matches “{text}”." is in the list.

Motion

The panel scales in with scale-in in --duration-fast, none under prefers-reduced-motion.

Content

  • The placeholder gives one example of each thing it finds: a table, an e-mail and an ID.
  • A table's meta is its group; a record's is "{kind} · {ID}".
  • No match: "Nothing matches “{text}”." with the typed text kept.
  • Records the role may not read never appear (L2-284).
