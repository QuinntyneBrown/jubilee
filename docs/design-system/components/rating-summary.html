Rating summary

The head of a listing’s reviews: the average large, its stars and the count; one bar for each of 5 to 1 stars, each a toggle that filters the list.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newrating-summaryL2-226L2-229L2-230

Overview

  • A bar’s length is its share of the largest count, not of the total.
  • Pressing a bar filters the list to that rating (aria-pressed="true"); pressing it again, or “Clear”, shows all (L2-230).
  • It carries no “Verified” note: Jubilee shows no “Verified” mark anywhere (ADR 0057).

Anatomy

  1. Average.rating-summary__average: Archivo bold, wide, --font-size-5xl.
  2. Stars and countA Stars named “Rated 4.6 out of 5” and .rating-summary__count.
  3. Barbutton.rating-summary__bar[aria-pressed]: the digit, the track and fill, the count.

Sizes

One size; its width is the column it sits in (260px or 300px from 768px, full width below).

States

BarDefaultHoverFocusPressed
4 stars

Pressed is a subtle ground and a semibold digit and count: never colour alone, and aria-pressed says it. A bar with no reviews can still be pressed; the list then says “No {s}-star reviews written up yet.”

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 768px the summary sits above the list, full width.
  • From 768px it is a 260px column (300px from 1024px) that stays in view while the list scrolls (position: sticky, L2-229 AC10).

Theming

Fill --color-accent; track --color-bg-subtle-hover; pressed and hover ground --color-bg-subtle; counts and note --color-fg-muted; the note’s icon --color-success-icon. In forced colours the fill is CanvasText and a pressed bar gets a Highlight outline.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Five toggle buttons (APG button, aria-pressed), each named “{s} stars, {n} reviews”; the digit, track and count are hidden so the name is read once. When the filter changes a polite status says “Showing {n} reviews” (L2-230 AC6).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves bar to bar, 5 to 1.
Enter / SpacePresses or releases the bar.

Touch

Each bar is 44px high across the column’s width.

Contrast

Count and note
Pressed ground
Count and note

Content

  • Average to one decimal; count “{n} reviews”.
  • Stars named “Rated {average} out of 5”.
  • Bars named “{s} stars, {n} reviews” (“1 star”, “1 review”).
