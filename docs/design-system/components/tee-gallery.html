Tee gallery

The shirt on its stage, with the Front and Back thumbnails under it. It draws the Purpose Tee from its print, as it is sold: black, cream ink, “purpose.” in gold.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: newtee-galleryL2-263L2-264

Overview

  • At the head of the Purpose Tee page, left of the purchase column from 1024 px and above it on a phone; sticky beside it on a desktop.
  • The shirt is an inline SVG (.tee, 600 × 700), so the print uses the bundled Archivo at its 110% width. The same drawing is docs/assets/purpose-tee-front.svg and -back.svg.

Anatomy

  1. Stage.tee-gallery__stage, --color-bg-subtle, --radius-xl.
  2. Shirt.tee with role="img" and a name that says what is printed.
  3. ThumbnailsTwo toggle buttons in a group “Show the tee from”, aria-pressed; the chosen one has a 2 px ring.

Sizes

One size: the stage fills its column; the shirt is at most 460 px wide, and 60% of a thumbnail.

States

Thumbnail: default, hover (a hairline ring of --color-border-strong), pressed (a 2 px ring of --color-fg-default), focus (the focus ring). The shirt fades in over --duration-base when the view changes, and does not move under reduced motion.

Responsive behaviour

The thumbnails fill the column on a phone and stop at 24rem from 640 px. The stage’s padding grows from 40 × 24 px to 48 px.

Theming

The stage and the thumbnails follow the theme; the shirt does not. The garment tokens are the same in both themes, because the shirt is black whatever the page is.

Both themes

Accessibility

The stage’s shirt is named (“The Purpose Tee from the front: “Prosper with purpose.” across the chest, “purpose.” in gold”); the thumbnails’ shirts are hidden, and each thumbnail is named by its word and says it is pressed. Colour is not the only carrier: the ring and aria-pressed both say which view is shown.

  • The shirt’s name says what is printed, not what it looks like (“black shirt”).
