Moment check

A moment check asks one question right after something that matters: how easy was it? It sits under a confirmation, takes one tap to answer and never gets in the way.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableScore scale + follow-upL2-211

Overview

Use it on the confirmation of a request that was just sent (a meeting, a quote, an order) and of a listing that was just submitted. Nowhere else: the Feedback launcher covers every other moment.

  • The score is stored the moment it is chosen. Choosing another score replaces it.
  • A score of 4 or 5 shows “Thanks, that helps us.” A score of 3 or less shows a field, “What got in the way?”, and “Send”; sending shows the thanks.
  • If the answer cannot be stored nothing is said. The question is a courtesy and never an error on a success screen. Ignoring it stores nothing.

Anatomy

  1. Question.moment-check__question, --text-label semibold, under a hairline that sets the check apart from the confirmation.
  2. ScaleA Score scale, .score-scale--wide, whose hidden legend repeats the question and the ends.
  3. Ends.moment-check__ends: “Very hard” and “Very easy”, --text-caption, muted.
  4. Follow-up fieldIn .moment-check__follow-up: a Textarea in a Form field, optional, 1,000 characters.
  5. SendA default Button in .moment-check__actions.

Responsive behaviour

  • The scale and the follow-up fill the container up to 24rem; at 360px each of the five options is about 60px wide.
  • In a dialog the check scrolls with the body; the dialog’s actions stay in reach.

Theming

The check has no surface of its own: it takes the page or the dialog behind it, and a --color-border-default hairline above. The thanks line is --color-success-fg with a check icon, so the colour is not the only carrier.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A plain container. The scale is a <fieldset> of native radios whose hidden legend is the question and the meaning of the ends (“… 1 is very hard, 5 is very easy.”), so it is one group of five choices. The visible ends are hidden from assistive technology.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabInto the scale, then the field and “Send” when they show.
← / →Choose a score.
Enter / Space on “Send”Sends the note.

Focus

The shared ring on the option, the field and the button. Choosing a score does not move focus; the follow-up appears after the scale in the order.

Labelling

The field has a visible label, “What got in the way? (optional)”. The thanks line is a live region (role="status"), so it is announced when it replaces the field.

Motion

The follow-up fades in over --duration-base; under reduced motion it appears at once.

Touch

Each option is at least 44px tall; “Send” is a 44px button.

Content

  • The question names the thing just done: “How easy was it to send your request?”, “How easy was it to list your business?”
  • The ends are “Very hard” and “Very easy”. The field is “What got in the way?”. The thanks is “Thanks, that helps us.”
  • Never an error, a required mark or a reminder. One question per confirmation.
