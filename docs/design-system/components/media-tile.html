Media tile

A media tile is the frame a business or product photo sits in. In the mocks it is a tonal block with a line icon, standing in for photography; in product the same frame holds the real photo, with alt text where the picture adds meaning.

Overview

Every picture in Jubilee goes in a media tile: cards in a Business card grid, the large photo on a business or product page, order thumbnails in lists. It fixes the shape (so the layout never jumps while photos load), rounds the corners and gives a tone to show when there is no photo yet.

  • Tones (neutral, gold, green, blue, ink) are placeholders and fallbacks only. The mocks pair them loosely with categories (gold for shops, green for financial and health, ink for trades); product uses the business's own photo.
  • Shapes: 4:3 by default, .media--square for products, .media--thumb (48px square) beside rows and in order summaries.
  • Tag (.media__tag): an optional pill in the top-left corner. It names the category, or says “Sold out” on a sold-out product, on its page and on its card. The words carry the state; the tile does not change colour.

The tile is never interactive on its own. When it is part of a link (a card), the link carries the name and the states.

Anatomy

  1. Tag (optional).media__tag: surface pill, --text-caption semibold, --shadow-1, inset --space-3.
  2. Icon (stand-in only)28% of the width, max 5rem, stroke 1.25, coloured by the tone. Replaced by the photo in product.
  3. Frameaspect-ratio: 4 / 3, --radius-lg, hairline --color-border-default edge, overflow: hidden.

With a photo

In product the tile wraps an <img> that fills it with object-fit: cover. Inside a card link the image is decorative (alt="") because the card name follows; on a business or product page the large photo gets real alt text ("Charcoal tee with a gold FAITH print, folded").

<div class="media media--square">
  <img src="/img/faith-tee.jpg" alt="Charcoal tee with a gold FAITH print, folded" loading="lazy" width="800" height="800">
</div>

  • In a hovered card the tile lifts to --shadow-2; this is set by .biz-card:hover .media.
  • Loading uses .skeleton--media (or .skeleton--square) of the same ratio, so nothing shifts when the photo arrives.
  • Photo failed falls back to the business's tone and icon without the tag; never a broken-image icon.

Responsive behaviour

  • The tile is fluid: 100% of its column at every width, the ratio fixed by aspect-ratio.
  • In the feature grid's first card from 1024px the ratio is released (aspect-ratio: auto) and the tile stretches to at least 20rem tall.
  • Thumbs stay 48px at every width. The tag never scales.
  • Serve photos with srcset at 1×/2× for each column width; the tile never shows a photo larger than 640px wide.

Theming

Tones are semantic tokens, so both themes are automatic: the subtle backgrounds darken in dark mode and the icon colours lighten. Ink uses --color-bg-brand with --color-border-on-brand, so it stays ink in both themes, like the hero.

The tag is a surface pill in both themes; its --shadow-1 keeps it apart from light photos.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Presentational. The tile is a <div>; the stand-in icon is aria-hidden. There is no APG pattern for images; follow the WAI images decision tree. Inside a link (a card) the photo is decorative (alt=""); on a detail page it is informative and needs alt text.

Keyboard

KeyAction
NoneThe tile is not focusable. Keyboard users reach the card or page it belongs to.

Focus

None of its own. When a card is focused, the ring is drawn around the card, not the tile.

Labelling

The tag is real text and is read as part of the card's name ("Financial Legacy Financial…"); keep it one word. Alt text describes the photo, not the business ("Trevor Chew at his desk with a client", not "Legacy Financial"). Thumbs beside a row are always alt="".

Motion

None on the tile. The hover lift in cards is instant. Photo loading never fades in longer than --duration-fast, which reduced motion zeroes.

Touch

Not a target. The card or page action around it provides the target.

Content

  • Photos show the work, the product or the person, in good light. No logos on a white field; logos go in the avatar.
  • Product photos are square and centred on the product; business photos are 4:3.
  • Tags are a single category word in sentence case: Financial, Shop, Home, Health, Legal.
  • Never put text, prices or badges inside the photo; use the card's meta line.
