Star scale

A star scale asks for a rating from 1 to 5. It is five native radios in one group; the chosen star and those before it are filled, and a word beside the stars says what the choice means.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableradiogroup of native radiosL2-210L2-212

Overview

Use it for the one overall rating of Jubilee: in the feedback panel and at the top of the feedback page. For a score of a part, or a question between two words, use the Score scale.

  • Each star is named “{n} of 5: {word}”, with the words Frustrating, Not great, Okay, Good and Love it.
  • The word of the chosen star shows beside the stars; before any is chosen it reads “Tap to rate”.
  • The state is the radio’s :checked, never a class: the arrow keys and assistive technology work without script.

Anatomy

  1. LegendThe question. A <legend> in the label style, referenced by aria-labelledby.
  2. Filled star.star-scale__star at or before the checked radio: --color-accent with a --color-accent-border edge.
  3. Empty starA --color-border-strong outline. Each star is a 44px label around a hidden radio.
  4. Word.star-scale__word: the chosen star’s word, semibold; muted “Tap to rate” before a choice.

Responsive behaviour

  • The stars never shrink: five 44px targets are 220px. At 360px the word stays on the row; if it cannot, it wraps under the stars.
  • The row is pulled 6px toward the start so the first star, not its target, lines up with the legend.

Theming

A filled star is --color-accent with a --color-accent-border edge: gold alone is 1.6:1 on cream, so the edge carries the 3:1. An empty star is a --color-border-strong outline. Both hold in dark, where the accent edge is the accent itself.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <fieldset role="radiogroup"> named by its legend, holding five <input type="radio"> with one name. See the APG radio group pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves into the group: to the checked star, or the first when none is.
← ↑ / → ↓Moves to the star before or after and chooses it.
SpaceChooses the focused star.

Focus

A 2px ring drawn inside the focused star’s 44px target, so neighbouring rings do not overlap.

Labelling

Each radio’s name is its visually hidden text, “4 of 5: Good”. The visible word repeats the choice and is hidden from assistive technology. When required, the group carries aria-required; when invalid, aria-invalid="true" and aria-describedby the error.

Motion

Fill and outline change over --duration-fast; a press scales the star to 90%. Under reduced motion nothing moves.

Touch

Every star is a 44 × 44px label; the radio fills it.

Content

  • The words are fixed: Frustrating, Not great, Okay, Good, Love it.
  • The legend is a question (“How’s Jubilee working for you so far?”).
  • The error says what to do: “Choose a rating from 1 to 5.”
  • A note that follows the stars changes its placeholder with the rating: “What would make it better? (optional)” below four, “What’s working well? (optional)” from four.
