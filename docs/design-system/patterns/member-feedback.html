Pattern

Member feedback

Member feedback is how a member tells Jubilee how it is going: a launcher on every screen that opens a short panel, one question after a moment that matters, and a page for the full story with what became of it.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableL1-029L2-210 to L2-213ADR 0043

Overview

Three places, one vocabulary. A member can rate Jubilee, report a bug or send kudos; the same three tabs, with the same icons, lead both the panel and the page.

  • The launcher and the panel: a Feedback launcher on every member screen opens a Dialog in the corner placement, with segmented Tabs.
  • The check-in: a Moment check under the confirmation of a sent request or a submitted listing.
  • The page: the full forms, with the Star scale, Score scale rows, the File list, and Feedback history beside them.

When to use

  • Use the panel for a quick word from anywhere; it never leaves the screen.
  • Use the check-in only on the four confirmations: a meeting, quote or order request sent, and a listing submitted.
  • Use the page when the member has more to say (“Full form” in the panel), and to see what they sent.
  • Do not add another feedback entry to a page, and do not use these for reporting a listing or a conversation: those are reports, with their own dialogs.

States

  • Nothing to send: the panel’s send button is disabled until a star is chosen, a bug is described, or a chip is pressed or a note typed. The page validates on submit instead and shows each error under its field.
  • Sending: the button is busy and the fields cannot be changed.
  • Failed: an Alert, “We couldn’t send that”, with the reason. Everything chosen and typed is kept.
  • Sent: the panel thanks the member by first name and offers “Done”; the page replaces the form with a confirmation and “Back to Jubilee”, and the new item is at the top of “Your feedback”.
  • Your feedback: loading (skeleton rows), empty, failed to load (the form still works).

Responsive behaviour

  • The panel is a bottom sheet under 640px and a 24rem card in the bottom right corner from 640px, over the scrim at every width.
  • The launcher is a round button above the tab bar under 768px and a pill from 768px.
  • On the page, “Your feedback” follows the form under 1024px and sits beside it from 1024px. Score rows stack their scale under the name below 640px.

Accessibility

  • The panel is a modal dialog: focus is held inside, Escape, the close button and the scrim close it, and focus returns to the launcher.
  • The tabs are a tab list: only the selected tab is in the tab order and the arrow keys move between them.
  • Stars and scores are native radios in a group. Chips are toggle buttons with aria-pressed.
  • Consent is never ticked for the member.
  • On the page a failed submit moves focus to the first field with an error; in the panel there is nothing to mark invalid.
  • Every target is at least 44px on a phone; nothing moves under reduced motion.

Content

  • Panel: “Tell us how it’s going”; tabs “Rate”, “Report a bug”, “Kudos”; buttons “Send rating”, “Send report”, “Send kudos”.
  • Page: “Help us make Jubilee better”; buttons “Send feedback”, “Send bug report”, “Send kudos”.
  • A bug report says what will be attached before it is sent: the screen, the browser and device, and the app’s version.
  • Promise only what happens: “Logged as FB-7K2M9Q. Follow it under Your feedback.” Never a date for a fix, never that kudos will appear on a page.
