Pattern

Data tables and lists

Jubilee shows a collection in the shape of the decision people make with it: browse by picture, act on a request, scan a queue. The shape decides the component; the states are shared.

Overview

Four collection shapes cover every list in the mocks. Card grids (.card-grid of .biz-card/.product-card) for browsing; rows (.rows/.row, see List) for requests where each item carries a status and actions; lists for the inbox (Message thread) and notifications (Notification item); and the Table for owners and the verification team comparing several attributes. Directory rows list the directory by category.

  • Status is a badge with a word: Confirmed, Awaiting reply, Declined, Completed, Sold out, Listed. Colour backs it up, never replaces it.
  • Actions name their row: "Confirm request from Samuel Park", "Edit Gold cross stoneware mug".
  • Counts are visible: in tabs ("New 3"), in the subtitle ("3 new · reply within a day") and in the pagination range.

Responsive behaviour

  • Card grids are two columns on phones and up to four from 1024px (.card-grid, gap --space-6/--space-4).
  • Tables scroll horizontally inside .table-wrap; the first column holds the name so it is visible first. Keep tables to five columns; the verification queue’s six is the maximum.
  • Rows stack below 640px (.row--stack): actions move under the body and share the width.
  • The inbox is a list and a conversation side by side from 768px; on phones the list is the page and a conversation opens on its own with a back link.
  • Filter tabs scroll horizontally; their counts never wrap under the label.

Accessibility

Pattern and roles

Tables are native <table> with <caption> and scope="col"; sortable headers contain a button and carry aria-sort (APG table pattern). Rows and lists are <ul>/<li> so screen readers announce the count. Only use role="grid" if cells become editable; none are today.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through links and buttons in each row, row by row. Rows themselves are not focusable.
Enter on a sort headerToggles ascending/descending; the new order is announced through aria-sort.
Enter on a list itemOpens it (the whole notification or thread is one link).

Focus

After a row is removed (delete, decline), focus moves to the next row’s first control, or to the list heading if it was the last. After paging, focus moves to the first new item or the results heading.

Labelling and announcements

Icon actions name the row ("Delete Faith heavyweight tee, charcoal"); the actions column header is visually hidden text "Actions". Unread items prepend visually hidden "Unread.". Count badges in tabs are read with the tab name ("New 3").

Motion

Row hover and the appearance of row actions transition over --duration-fast; skeleton shimmer stops under reduced motion.

Touch

Row actions are small buttons (32px) spaced 8px apart; on phones stacked row actions grow to the full row width. Whole-item links (threads, notifications) are at least 64px tall.

Content

  • Row titles are the thing requested, as the member wrote it or as the catalogue names it: "Kitchen drain is slow and gurgling", "Custom church team tee × 24".
  • Metadata order: kind · counterpart · time. "Order · Samuel Park · 8:15 a.m." Then the second line of detail.
  • Status words are past participles or short phrases, never codes: Confirmed, Declined, Cancelled, Awaiting reply, Ready to review.
  • Column headers are nouns in sentence case. Numbers right-aligned; prices with $ and no decimals unless cents exist.
