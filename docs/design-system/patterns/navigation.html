Pattern

Navigation and page structure

Every Jubilee page answers where am I, what can I do here and how do I get back, with the same few parts in the same places.

Overview

The frame is the Top bar (ink, .topbar--ink) on every signed-in page, the Tab bar below 768px, a Skip link before both, and the Footer. Inside the content, a page has at most one way up (Breadcrumb or back link), one page header (h1, subtitle, page actions) and, when it has sections, Tabs. Long lists end in Pagination.

  • Primary destinations are few and fixed: Browse, Messages, List your business / My business, the bell and the account menu. On phones: Browse, List, Messages, You.
  • The current place is always marked with aria-current="page": a gold underline in the top bar, a gold pill in the tab bar, a gold underline on tabs.
  • Buyer and seller share one frame. Joanna sees "List your business"; once Naomi owns a listing the same slot reads "My business". The Jubilee team works in the admin application, whose bar (.admin-bar) carries Listings, Reports and Featured; the app has no admin link.
  • Auth pages have no frame: just the wordmark linking to Welcome, the card, and a foot link. Log in and Sign up add an ink brand panel beside the form from 1024px; see Auth layout.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 768px (--layout-breakpoint-md) the top-bar links and the List your business button hide (.only-desktop) and the fixed tab bar appears; the body gets bottom padding of --layout-tabbar-height so content and toasts clear it.
  • Below 1024px the top-bar search collapses to an icon button (.only-narrow) that opens the search page.
  • The account menu is an anchored menu from 640px and a bottom sheet below it.
  • Tabs scroll horizontally when they do not fit (overflow-x: auto, no scrollbar); the current tab scrolls into view. Breadcrumbs wrap rather than truncate.
  • Page-header actions wrap below the title; on a detail page the primary takes the full row.

Accessibility

Pattern and roles

Landmarks: <header> (top bar), <nav aria-label="Primary">, <nav aria-label="Primary, phone">, <main id="main">, <footer>, each nav uniquely labelled. Breadcrumbs follow the APG breadcrumb pattern; in-page filters follow the APG tabs pattern; page-to-page section tabs are links, not tabs.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab (first press)Reveals the skip link; Enter jumps to #main.
TabTop bar, then content, then footer. The phone tab bar comes last in the DOM even though it is fixed on screen.
← → in a tablistMove between filter tabs; Home/End jump to first/last. Link tabs use Tab like any link.
EnterFollows a link or activates a tab.

Focus

Focus is never trapped by navigation. After a route change focus moves to the new page’s h1 (or #main) and the document title updates ("Your requests · Jubilee").

Labelling and announcements

Current location uses aria-current="page", not colour alone. Icon-only top-bar buttons name their destination and count ("Notifications, 3 unread"); count badges carry the count in words. Tab bar labels are visible text under each icon.

Motion

Tab underlines and hover colours change over --duration-fast; nothing slides. Reduced motion zeroes the transitions.

Touch

Top-bar links are the full bar height (--layout-topbar-height); tab bar items are --layout-tabbar-height tall and a quarter of the width; tabs and back links are 44px (--target-comfortable).

Content

  • Navigation labels are short nouns: Browse, Messages, Saved, You. The one verb is "List your business", because it is an invitation.
  • Page titles name the thing, not the action: "Your requests", "Luzandre.com", "Kitchen drain is slow and gurgling".
  • Subtitles carry the page’s status in a line: "3 open · 1 awaiting reply", "Live since June 2026". Separate facts with a middle dot.
  • Back links name the destination: "Back to browse", never just "Back".

Do and don't

Sources

Extracted from these mocks:

  • pages/browse/default (ink top bar, tab bar)
  • pages/product/default (breadcrumb)
  • pages/business/default (back link)
  • pages/incoming-requests/default (section tabs and filter tabs on one page)
  • pages/category/long (pagination)
  • dialogs/user-menu/default (account menu as a sheet)

Drift found: 10 of 11 role="tablist" filter bars have no role="tabpanel" or aria-controls; the system requires them. The tab indicator radius was a raw 2px, now --radius-xs. Selected chips and tabs were restyled by a later brand override; that look is now the base rule. The pagination range text and sort select carry inline layout styles (style="width: auto", margin-top), which stay as token-only layout tweaks. The .sidebar component exists in the kit but no mock uses it.
