Pattern

Forms

Every Jubilee form follows one recipe: a visible label for each control, help before the input, an error under it, a summary at the top that links to each error, and one primary action that names what happens next.

Overview

Forms appear on every side of Jubilee: joining and signing in, the List your business application, the account and notification settings, and inside dialogs that send a request (Book a meeting, Request a quote, Request an item) or answer one (Decline request). They are built from Form field, Text field, Textarea, Select, Checkbox, Radio group and Switch, laid out with Form layout, and finished with Buttons.

  • Validate on submit, then live. Nothing turns red while a member is still typing. On submit, every problem shows at once: inline under its field and in a summary at the top that receives focus. After that, a fixed field clears its error as soon as it is valid.
  • Keep the submit button enabled. Jubilee never disables a submit to signal missing input; pressing it explains what to fix.
  • Nothing is lost. Values survive an error, a failed request and a reload. Long forms offer Save draft; the success state says what happens next, not just that it worked.
  • No payment fields. Jubilee takes no payment: request forms collect what the business needs to confirm or decline (date, time, quantity, a note), and confirmation copy says the member pays the business directly.

Responsive behaviour

  • .form-grid--2 pairs short related fields (first and last name, business name and store address, day and how) from 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm); below it they stack in reading order.
  • Auth forms sit in a 26rem card at every width; their buttons are .btn--block .btn--lg so the target is full width on phones.
  • .form-actions wraps; on phones the primary takes its own row and stays last. In a dialog the footer is sticky, so Send request is never scrolled away by the keyboard.
  • List your business shows its live card preview in a sticky side column from 1024px and after the form below that.
  • Choice cards are one column below 640px and two above. Inputs are at least 16px text so iOS does not zoom on focus.

Accessibility

Pattern and roles

Native form controls inside a <form novalidate> so Jubilee’s own messages replace the browser bubbles. Radio groups and checkbox groups are <fieldset> + <legend>; the switch is <input type="checkbox" role="switch">. The error summary follows the GOV.UK-style summary built on the APG alert pattern; radio groups follow the APG radio group pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through fields, links in help text and the actions in DOM order (which matches visual order, including in two-column grids).
Arrow keysMove between options of a radio group or choice cards; the selection follows focus.
SpaceToggles a checkbox or switch.
EnterSubmits from any single-line field. In a textarea it adds a line; the composer in Messages is the only place Enter sends.
Activating a summary linkMoves focus to that field (the link targets the control’s id).

Focus

After a failed submit, focus moves to the summary (it has tabindex="-1"); after success it moves to the outcome heading. Nothing steals focus while a member types. Fields with errors are never auto-focused one by one.

Labelling and announcements

Every control has a visible <label for>; placeholders are examples only ("name@example.com"). aria-describedby lists the help id then the error id, so both are read with the label. aria-invalid="true" marks the control; the error text starts with what to do. Personal fields carry autocomplete (given-name, email, new-password) for 1.3.5 and 3.3.7 redundant entry. Busy primaries keep their label and set aria-busy; a visually hidden status announces "Sending your request…".

Motion

Errors appear without animation. The summary does not shake or slide. The busy spinner keeps turning under reduced motion because it is status; everything else (focus ring, border colour) changes over --duration-fast, zeroed under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Inputs are 44px tall (--control-height-md). Checkbox and radio rows (.choice) are at least 24px with the whole label clickable; choice cards make the whole card the target.

Content

  • Labels are nouns in sentence case, no colon: "E-mail", "Home church address", "One line on what you do".
  • Mark fields explicitly when a form mixes required and optional fields: "(required)" or "(optional)" on every label, as Edit listing does. Sign up is the exception: it marks only "Home church (optional)" and "Home church address (optional)", and its required controls carry required alone (L2-126). A form where every field is required and the purpose is obvious (Log in, Forgot password) marks nothing.
  • Help says why or how, before the input: "Shown under your name everywhere on Jubilee. 90 characters at most." Sign up sets its hints under the input, as designed.
  • Errors say what to do, in the member’s terms, with an example when the format matters: "Enter an e-mail address like name@example.com" Never "Invalid input" or "Field required".
  • The summary title counts: "Fix 2 things to continue". Its items repeat the first sentence of each inline error.
  • The primary names the outcome: "Create account", "Submit for verification", "Send request", "Decline request". Never "Submit" or "OK".
  • See Content and tone for dates, prices and the error formula.

Drift found: the required marker is a <span class="optional"> that says "(required)" (the class name and the text disagree; the system keeps the class for compatibility and documents it as the label suffix). Join marks both required and optional fields while List your business leaves Category and Store address unmarked; the rule above resolves it. Help text sits before the input everywhere except the password field on Join, where it follows the error. The summary list carries an inline style for its indent on all 8 forms, and the two-column grid adds margin-top inline; both are layout-only token tweaks and stay until a .form-summary ul rule exists (suggested in the report).
