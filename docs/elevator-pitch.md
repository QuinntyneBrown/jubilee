# The elevator pitch

The first-contact pitch for Christian business people, sent on WhatsApp and social media. It is
framed from [`scripture.md`](scripture.md) and serves the mission in
[`specs/L0.md`](specs/L0.md). Scripture is quoted from the World English Bible.

## What the pitch conveys

Six things, in this order. Each rests on one passage.

| # | What it conveys | The reader's question | Passage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your ability to build is a gift from God.** | Why is this about me? | Deuteronomy 8:18 |
| 2 | **The gift has a purpose: to be generous and resource His Kingdom.** | What is it for? | 2 Corinthians 9:11 |
| 3 | **Fruit comes from abiding in Jesus, not from a platform.** | Why should I trust you? | John 15:5 |
| 4 | **We do this together, as the household of faith.** | What is Jubilee, and what do I do in it? | Galatians 6:10 |
| 5 | **What we build together outlasts us.** | Why does it matter? | John 15:16; Proverbs 13:22 |
| 6 | **Stand with us: list your business.** | What do I do now? | Colossians 3:17 |

The pitch does not promise that listing will make anyone prosper, and it gives no verse as the
reason to pay for anything (see "What Jubilee never says" in [`scripture.md`](scripture.md)).

## In 30 words

> Your business can leave a legacy that outlasts you. Jubilee unites Toronto's Christian business
> people to prosper with purpose and resource His Kingdom, together, in Jesus' name. Join us:
> jubilee.to.

## The document

**JUBILEE**
**Toronto's Christian marketplace.**

**Your business was meant to outlast you.**
"I chose you and appointed you, that you should go and bear fruit, and that your fruit should
remain." (John 15:16)

**God gave you the ability to build. He also gave it a purpose.**
"It is he who gives you power to get wealth, that he may establish his covenant."
(Deuteronomy 8:18)

From the women who served Jesus "from their possessions" (Luke 8:3) to Lydia, the seller of purple
whose house became a church (Acts 16:14-15), His Kingdom has always been resourced by business
people who knew whose business it really was.

**Our mission**
*Abiding in Jesus and for love of Him, Jubilee equips Christian business people in Toronto to
prosper with purpose and resource His Kingdom.*

**We can only do it together.**
Jubilee is a directory of Christian-owned businesses in Toronto. Believers find you,
message you, book you and buy from you. "As we have opportunity, let's do what is good toward all
men, and especially toward those who are of the household of the faith." (Galatians 6:10)

**What we are asking**
Stand with us. List your business free, agree to our statement of faith, and be live straight
away. Help build something that is still bearing fruit when our children's children are running
it. (Proverbs 13:22)

**Leave a legacy. Resource His Kingdom. In Jesus' name.**
Join us at jubilee.to
[Name · WhatsApp number]
