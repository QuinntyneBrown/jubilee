# Scripture: the foundation of Jubilee's content

Jubilee's mission ([`specs/L0.md`](specs/L0.md)) says why Jubilee exists. This document says where
that mission comes from. Every line of copy on the platform, the marketing site, a pitch, an
e-mail or a message is framed from the passages below.

> Abiding in Jesus and for love of Him, Jubilee equips Christian business people in Toronto to
> prosper with purpose and resource His Kingdom.

## How to use this document

- **Start from a passage, not from a benefit.** Before writing copy, find the passage here that
  the copy rests on. If none fits, the copy is probably about Jubilee and not about the mission.
- **Quote Scripture word for word, with its reference.** Do not paraphrase a verse inside
  quotation marks, join two verses into one, or cut a verse so that it says something else.
- **Read the verse in its chapter before using it.** The "In context" notes below say what each
  passage is about, so that it is not used for something it does not say.
- **Scripture is not decoration.** One passage that carries the point is better than three that
  ornament it. Most screens of the app quote no verse at all; they are still written from here.
- **Keep the cautions with the promises.** The passages under
  [What Jubilee never says](#what-jubilee-never-says) bind every line of copy.

**Translation.** The text here is the World English Bible (WEB), which is in the public domain and
may be quoted without permission or limit. It writes God's name as "Yahweh" where most
translations print "the LORD". To publish in another translation (NIV, ESV, NKJV, NLT), quote that
translation exactly, name it beside the reference, and follow its publisher's permission terms.

## The anchor: John 15

The mission begins "Abiding in Jesus", and John 15 is where that phrase comes from. It also
carries the legacy: fruit that remains.

> Remain in me, and I in you. As the branch can't bear fruit by itself unless it remains in the
> vine, so neither can you, unless you remain in me. I am the vine. You are the branches. He who
> remains in me and I in him bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing.
>
> John 15:4-5

> You didn't choose me, but I chose you and appointed you, that you should go and bear fruit, and
> that your fruit should remain; that whatever you will ask of the Father in my name, he may give
> it to you.
>
> John 15:16

**In context.** Jesus is speaking to his disciples on the night before the cross. The fruit is
the life and work that come from staying joined to him. It is not a promise about revenue.

**What it asks of Jubilee's content.** The reason comes before the product. Fruit is the result
of abiding, never a technique. "Legacy" on Jubilee means fruit that remains.

## The name: Jubilee

> You shall make the fiftieth year holy, and proclaim liberty throughout the land to all its
> inhabitants. It shall be a jubilee to you; and each of you shall return to his own property, and
> each of you shall return to his family.
>
> Leviticus 25:10

> "The Spirit of the Lord is on me, because he has anointed me to preach good news to the poor. He
> has sent me to heal the broken hearted, to proclaim release to the captives, recovering of sight
> to the blind, to deliver those who are crushed, and to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord."
>
> Luke 4:18-19

**In context.** The jubilee was the fiftieth year in Israel: debts released, land returned,
families restored. In Luke 4 Jesus reads Isaiah 61 in the synagogue at Nazareth and says it is
fulfilled in him.

**What it asks of Jubilee's content.** The name stands for liberty, restoration and return to
family. Jubilee speaks of freedom and of community, and of Jesus as the one who brings them.

## How Jubilee does business

These passages ground how the platform behaves: verification, honest listings, plain prices and
plain words.

| Passage | Text (WEB) | What it asks of the platform |
|---|---|---|
| Proverbs 11:1 | "A false balance is an abomination to Yahweh, but accurate weights are his delight." | Honest measures. Listings, prices, counts and claims are accurate, including Jubilee's own. |
| Proverbs 22:1 | "A good name is more desirable than great riches, and loving favor is better than silver and gold." | Why every business is verified: a good name is worth protecting. |
| Matthew 5:37 | "But let your 'Yes' be 'Yes' and your 'No' be 'No.' Whatever is more than these is of the evil one." | Plain speech. A business confirms or declines; Jubilee does not hedge, hype or hide terms. |
| Matthew 5:16 | "Even so, let your light shine before men; that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father who is in heaven." | Good work done in the open points to the Father, not to the business or to Jubilee. |
| John 13:35 | "By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you have love for one another." | How members and businesses treat one another is the witness. |

## What Jubilee never says

Scripture gives cautions beside its promises. They bind every line of copy.

> Charge those who are rich in this present world that they not be arrogant, nor have their hope
> set on the uncertainty of riches, but on the living God, who richly provides us with everything
> to enjoy; that they do good, that they be rich in good works, that they be ready to distribute,
> willing to share; laying up in store for themselves a good foundation against the time to come,
> that they may lay hold of eternal life.
>
> 1 Timothy 6:17-19

- **Never promise prosperity.** Jubilee does not say that listing, joining or giving will make
  anyone prosper. Riches are uncertain (1 Timothy 6:17); the promise in Matthew 6:33 is of
  provision for those who seek the Kingdom first.
- **Never credit the builder.** "My power and the might of my hand has gotten me this wealth" is
  the thing Deuteronomy 8:17 warns against. The same holds for Jubilee speaking of itself
  (Psalm 127:1).
- **Never use a verse as a sales line.** A verse is not placed beside a price, a plan or a call to
  pay as its reason.
- **Never speak against those outside.** Galatians 6:10 says "toward all men" before it says
  "especially".
- **Never measure faith by wealth.** A small business run faithfully has heard "well done"
  (Matthew 25:21) as surely as a large one.

## Where this came from

The teaching of Phil Pringle, in particular *Keys to Financial Excellence*, shaped how Jubilee
reads these passages: wealth as a gift with a purpose, generosity as its end, and the local church
as what it builds. The passages were chosen for this document; it does not reproduce that book.
