## What he hesitates over

- **Category.** Financial & insurance holds advisers and brokers. He is neither, and he wants no
  one to think he gives investment advice. The one line has to carry that.
- **The last sentence of About.** He does not want blindness to be the headline. He keeps it
  because it saves every client one awkward e-mail.
- **The photo.** He has to take Denise's word that it is in focus and that the desk is tidy. The
  alt text is the one part he can own, and 125 characters are enough.
- **Prices.** "A price is optional; “Ask” is fine." He gives two prices so that small clients are
  not afraid to write.

## What the profile cannot say

- That he is a member of a bookkeepers' association and carries liability insurance: there are no
  licence or credential fields, so it goes in About or nowhere.
- How he prefers to receive documents, as something a member sees before sending a photo of a
  shoebox of receipts.
- That he works by phone and video in a way a member can filter for: "Area you serve" is free
  text, and the category's area chips do not know "remote".
- Whether a listing's photos have real descriptions: there is nothing that tells him, as a Member,
  that an owner has written them.
