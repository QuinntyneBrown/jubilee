# Tomasz's onboarding journey

[Back to Tomasz Kowalski](README.md)

## How Tomasz finds Jubilee

At lunch on a job site in Etobicoke, the electrician on the job, a man Tomasz has prayed with
before a shift, shows him a quote request on his phone. "From Jubilee. Free. You do decks, put
yourself on." He sends Tomasz the link to his own listing (L2-038) by text before the break ends.

## Steps

| Step | What Tomasz does | What Jubilee shows | What Tomasz thinks or feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opens the link in the truck after work | The electrician's page: "Home & property", services, "Message" | It looks serious. He wonders if it is only for real companies |
| 2 | Goes to `/welcome` and finds "Own a business?" | "List your business" | Friction: he stops at the word "business" and puts the phone away |
| 3 | Asks the electrician the next day, then taps "List your business" | Asked to join; `/join`, with "Sign up with Google" first | "You get paid for the work? Then it's a business." One tap; no password |
| 4 | Accepts the agreement | The statement of faith and terms agreement | He reads the statement of faith slowly. Kasia reads the terms with him that evening |
| 5 | Opens `/list` on his break | "Free for Christian-owned businesses in Toronto." and "What do you offer?" | He picks "Services". The form is shorter than he feared |
| 6 | Types a name and one line; the signal drops | The connection banner; the form stays as he left it | Relief. On other sites he has lost everything at this point |
| 7 | Adds three services with no prices | "A price is optional; “Ask” is fine." | The best sentence on the page. He cannot price a deck he has not seen |
| 8 | Ticks the agreement and presses "Publish listing", without photos | "Kowalski Brothers Decks & Renos is live. Members can find you in Home & property now." and "How easy was it to list your business?" | He answers 4. He did not know photos were expected |
| 9 | That evening, opens his page with Kasia | His listing, live, with a tinted tile where the electrician's page has photos | Friction: his first thought is that he did something wrong. Kasia finds "Edit listing". It is only a photo |
| 10 | Adds photos from his phone | "Up to 6 photos, JPG or PNG, 5 MB each. The first is your cover." | Friction: two of his five photos are refused as too large. Three go in |
| 11 | Opens his page again | His public page, with the cedar deck as its cover | He shows Marek, then his mother on a video call |
| 12 | Three weeks later, answers a quote request for a basement wall and a door | The request under "Needs your reply"; he replies in messages and attaches a photo of similar work | The first valuable moment. He visits on Saturday and gets the job |

## Where it could go wrong

- At step 2. Without the electrician he would not have come back. Nothing on the page told him
  that a weekend trade with no company counts.
- At step 9. A bare tile where every other card has a photo reads like a mistake to someone
  reading in a second language, and nothing on the page says a photo is the fix.
- A member asks whether he is insured. He is not yet, and must say so plainly; some will go
  elsewhere, which is their right.
- A message arrives while he is on a roof with no signal and waits six hours for an answer.
