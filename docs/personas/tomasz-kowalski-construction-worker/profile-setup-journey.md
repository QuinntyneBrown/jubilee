"As it happens" is right: a quote request answered the same day is a job.

## What he hesitates over

- **Business name.** The hint says to enter it "as customers know it". Customers know him as
  Tomek. He and Marek choose a name that evening, and it is the first time the work has had one.
- **The one line.** Ninety characters in English takes him fifteen minutes. The counter helps:
  he can see he is allowed to stop.
- **Area you serve.** He lives near Roncesvalles and works across the west end. No area has that
  name, so he picks Etobicoke, where most jobs have been.
- **About.** 1,500 characters looks like an exam. He writes 370 and stops.
- **Hours.** Saturday only looks small beside other listings. It is the truth, so he leaves it.

## What the profile cannot say

- That he speaks Polish, which matters to older people in his parish who would rather explain a
  job in their own language.
- That this is weekend work by a tradesman employed full time, in a plain label and not only in
  the About text.
- That he is not yet insured for this work and is arranging it: there is no honest, standard
  place to state it, so it waits until someone asks.
- More than three photos for now, because the others are too large for the limit.
