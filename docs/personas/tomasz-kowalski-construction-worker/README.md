## Story

Tomasz was born in Tarnów and came to Toronto at nineteen to join his older brother. He started
as a labourer, learned framing on the job, and is now the one the foreman gives the stairs to. He
is good at the work and proud of it.

On Saturdays he and Marek build decks and fences and finish basements for people who hear of them
from someone else. They have no company, no website and no logo: a phone number, a truck and a
folder of photos on his phone. With a wedding to pay for and a hope of leaving the basement
apartment, he wants more Saturdays filled, and one day a crew of his own.

## What he wants from Jubilee

- More weekend jobs, from people who will treat him fairly and pay when the work is done.
- To be found without having to write much, design anything or pay for advertising.
- Something that works on his phone, on site, when the signal comes and goes.

## What worries him

- Whether he is allowed. The page says "businesses". He has weekend jobs.
- His written English. He speaks it well and writes it slowly.
- Being asked questions he cannot yet answer the way a company would, such as about insurance.
- Forms. The longer the form, the less likely he finishes it.

## How he serves the mission

Tomasz is one of the "Christian business owners in Toronto" the mission names, at the very
start. His business is two brothers and a truck, and it is run faithfully: the mission's "do well
in business" is for work of this size as much as for any other.

## In his words

"I don't have a company. I have a brother and a truck, and the deck will be straight. Is that
enough for your website?"
