# André's profile setup journey

[Back to André Baptiste](README.md)

André lists nothing. His profile is the member profile, his notification settings and, in
practice, the note he writes with each space request.

## Member profile (`/account`)

| Field | What André enters | Note |
|---|---|---|
| First name, Last name | André, Baptiste | The accent is kept. He checks |
| Phone | 416-555-0164 | "Shared with a business only when you send a request." He wants owners to call, so he adds it |
| Postal code | His Scarborough postal code | He hesitates: in 60 days it will be wrong, and he does not know what will replace it |
| Home church, Home church address | His congregation and its address | Optional, "Shown to no one." He fills them in gladly, then wishes an owner could see them: his pastor would speak for him |
| Pronouns | Left empty | |

He presses "Save profile". There is no photo, no bio and no public member page, so the form is
short. He is relieved that nothing asks for his employer.

## Notifications (`/account/notifications`)

"New messages" and "Request updates" stay on; he cannot miss an answer. "Meeting reminders" stays
on for viewings. He turns on "New businesses", hoping that it will tell him about new spaces; the
name does not say. "How often": "As it happens". A daily summary at 7:00 a.m. could cost him a
day, and he has 60.

## The note that stands in for a profile

In "Request a viewing" he chooses "Move in": the first of the month after next; "Staying":
"12 months"; and writes in "A note to Victoria":

> Good morning. We are a family of five: my wife Nadège, who is a personal support worker, and
> our children of 14, 10 and 5. Our landlord is taking back our home for his own use and we must
> move by the end of next month. I was a warehouse supervisor for nine years and I am looking for
> work since a layoff in the summer. Our rent has been paid on time for eleven years and our
> landlord will confirm it. The rent you ask is above what we can carry today. If there is any
> room to talk, we would be grateful to see the home. André Baptiste, 416-555-0164

## What he hesitates over

- **Saying he is out of work.** Leaving it out feels like a lie; putting it first feels like
  asking to be declined. He puts the eleven years of rent beside it.
- **Asking about the rent.** The page gives one price. He does not know if asking is allowed.
- **English.** He writes English well, but French is the language he would use for something this
  important, and Nadège's letter of reference from her agency is in French.
- **Writing it again.** The second request, to another host, needs the whole note retyped on a
  phone keyboard.

## What the profile cannot say

- Who his household is and what it needs: five people, three bedrooms, two schools, a budget.
- That a pastor and a landlord of eleven years will vouch for him. There is no place for a
  reference.
- That he is looking for work and what he can do: run a shift, drive a forklift, train new staff.
  There is no jobs board and no member page.
- That he is looking at all: he can only answer listings, never post a notice of his own.
