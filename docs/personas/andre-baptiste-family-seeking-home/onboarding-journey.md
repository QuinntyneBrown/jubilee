# André's onboarding journey

[Back to André Baptiste](README.md)

## How André finds Jubilee

After choir practice on a Thursday, the choir director, who knows about the notice, shares a
listing link on WhatsApp: a three-bedroom house in Scarborough she saw on Jubilee. André opens it
on the bus home, on mobile data.

## Steps

| Step | What André does | What Jubilee shows | What André thinks or feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opens the shared link | The space page: "Verified host · Scarborough", "Long term", the rent per month, "12-month lease min.", "Request a viewing", "Message host", "You won’t pay anything until the host accepts." | He can read all of it without an account. The rent is $2,950. Too high, but the first owner here has a name and a face |
| 2 | Presses "Request a viewing" | The sign-in prompt, with "Sign in" and "Join free" | Fair. He waits for the library, to save data |
| 3 | Next morning at the library, opens `/join` | "Join Jubilee", "Free for everyone in Toronto’s Christian community." | "Free" matters. He types his name, e-mail and a password, and leaves Home church for now |
| 4 | Ticks the checkbox and submits | "Check your e-mail": a link that works once, for 24 hours | Friction: his e-mail is on his phone, and the library session has 20 minutes left. He confirms on the phone and signs in again on the computer |
| 5 | Opens Browse, then "Rooms & places to stay", "All 23" | `/c/rooms-rentals`: "Rooms & rentals", "23 spaces", the filter "Any length", "Short stays", "Long term", "Rooms", "Roommates" | He presses "Long term". Seven spaces |
| 6 | Looks for a way to say three bedrooms, Scarborough, under $2,600 | A term filter only. No rent, bedroom or neighbourhood filter, no sort, no map | Friction: he opens each card. Four are one-bedroom condos downtown. He searches "3 bedroom" and "Scarborough" instead, which finds two by their key facts and neighbourhood |
| 7 | Saves both, and looks for a place to post what his family needs | Nothing: renters cannot post a notice | Friction: on other sites he could at least say "family of five looking" |
| 8 | Opens the three-bedroom upper duplex in East York, $3,400, and presses "Request a viewing" | The dialog: "Move in" (date), "Staying" ("12 months"), "A note to Victoria" | He writes the note four times. How much to say? He tells the truth (see [profile setup](profile-setup-journey.md)) |
| 9 | Sends it | "Sent to Victoria", "You’ll hear back in Messages, usually within a day. The exact address is shared once they accept." and "How easy was it to send your request?" | He scores it 4. First valuable moment: a request sent to a person, with a promise of an answer |
| 10 | On the bus, sees the notification | A message from the host: she would like to talk about the rent before the viewing | He reads it twice. For the first time in two months someone has asked a question back |

## Where it could go wrong

- "Long term" shows nothing he can afford and he does not think to search by key facts. He closes
  the tab and Jubilee becomes one more site that had nothing for him.
- The owner declines with "Something else" and one polite line after reading "employment
  insurance". From a fellow believer that would cut deeper than silence from a stranger.
- The confirmation link expires before his next library visit, and until he confirms he cannot
  send a request at all.
- Photos on space pages use up his data, and he stops browsing on the phone, which is where the
  answers arrive.
