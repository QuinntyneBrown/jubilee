## Story

André grew up in Montréal, the son of parents who came from Port-au-Prince, and moved to Toronto
in 2010 for a warehouse job that became a supervisor's post. He ran a night shift of twenty-two
people. When the company moved its distribution out of the city, the shift went with it.

Two months later the family's landlord gave notice that he is taking the unit back for his own
use. They have 60 days. They need three bedrooms, close enough that the two older children can
stay at their schools, at a rent that employment insurance and Nadège's hours can carry until he
is working again. André has answered dozens of rental advertisements. Most replies stop when he is
asked where he works.

## What he wants from Jubilee

- A home for five, near the children's schools, from an owner who will hear him out.
- To tell his family's story once, not in every message.
- Work. And, while money is short, honest trades and services at fair prices: a mover, someone to
  look at the car, help with his résumé.

## What worries him

- Being judged for being out of work, by people who share his faith, which would be worse than
  being judged by strangers.
- That there will be few "Long term" spaces, and none for a family.
- The data on his phone and the hour on the library's clock.

## How he serves the mission

André owns no business, and the mission is still for him: "for love of Him" is how a household
of faith treats a family that needs a home. His need is not a lack of faith. He leads his children
in prayer each night in a home he has been told to leave, and he is the neighbour Jubilee's owners
are equipped to serve.

## In his words

"I am not asking for charity. I am asking for someone to answer the message."
