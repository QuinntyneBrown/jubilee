## What the profile cannot say

- Which languages she speaks: Ukrainian, Russian and English. It is a sentence in About.
- That existing clients would speak for her. There are no reviews; kudos appear only if a member
  on Jubilee sends them and ticks the box, and her four families are not members.
- Where she really works. The west end of the old city is not one of the areas.
- That weekly cleaning is a standing arrangement. A request is for one visit or one quote.
- That she is also an accountant and hopes to return to bookkeeping. That will be a second
  listing, under "Financial & insurance", when her credentials are recognised.
