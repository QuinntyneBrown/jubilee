## How Jubilee could better suit Olena

| Need | Today | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| Her language | English only; she relies on browser translation. | Keep important messages in plain, literal English that translates well; mark text an owner typed so translation leaves it alone; consider Ukrainian among the first languages added. |
| Safety in strangers' homes | An owner agrees to the statement of faith and to deal honestly. A member confirms an e-mail address; an owner sees a first name, the request and its note. No member profile. | Let an owner ask set questions before confirming a home visit (who will be home, the address), and make declining easy and free of blame. |
| Trust without Canadian references | No reference is asked. No reviews; kudos only from members. | Let an owner invite existing clients to join and send kudos, so two years of good work can be seen. |
| Steady weekly clients | Each request is one booking or one quote. | Let a confirmed arrangement be marked as recurring (weekly, biweekly) so both sides see the next visit. |
| Her real area | Areas served: Scarborough, North York, East York, Etobicoke, Downtown. | Add the west end and York to the areas members filter by. |
| Being paid fairly | Jubilee takes no payment; members pay the business directly. | A line on the confirmed request stating the agreed price and when it is due. |

> These are a persona's suggestions, not requirements. Anything taken up needs a requirement in
> `docs/specs/`, a detailed design and a mock before it is built, and must serve the mission.
