# Miriam Haddad: onboarding journey

Back to [Miriam Haddad](README.md).

## How Miriam finds Jubilee

After a baptism lunch in the parish hall, a guest asks Miriam for a card. She has none. Her
daughter Rita, stacking trays beside her, says, "Mama, this is the fourth person today." That
evening Rita shows her a message forwarded into the parish young adults' WhatsApp group by a
friend who is an insurance broker: the Jubilee pitch and a link. They sit at the kitchen table
with Rita's phone and a plate of leftover baklawa.

## Steps

| Step | What Miriam does (with Rita) | What Jubilee shows | What Miriam thinks or feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rita opens the link and presses "Open Jubilee"; they browse without an account. | `/welcome`: "Do business with Toronto's Christian community." and the eleven categories. | Miriam asks, "Where is the food?" There is no food or catering category. First friction. |
| 2 | Rita searches "catering", then "Lebanese". | Search results over businesses, services, products and spaces. | Few results. Miriam takes this two ways: nobody is here, or there is room for her. |
| 3 | They read "How Jubilee works" and the "Own a business?" panel. | "List your business". | "It is free? And they take nothing?" Rita reads it aloud: free to join, free to list, members pay the business directly. |
| 4 | Rita fills in `/join` in Miriam's name, with Miriam's e-mail. | "Join Jubilee", the fields, and the checkbox "I am 18 or older and agree to Jubilee’s statement of faith and terms." | Miriam has Rita read her the statement of faith. She nods and says the account must be hers, not Rita's. |
| 5 | Miriam finds the confirmation e-mail on her own phone and presses the link. | "E-mail confirmed" with "Start browsing" and "List your business". | Proud to have done that part alone. |
| 6 | On `/list`: "Products" or "Services"? | "Offer both? Pick the main one now and add the other once you're live." | Friction: a tray is a product, but it is cooked to order. "Products" asks for a "Store address", and her store is her kitchen. They choose "Services". |
| 7 | Chooses a category. | The eleven categories. | Friction: nothing fits. They settle on "Community", because most of her work is for church gatherings. |
| 8 | Reaches the agreement and stops. | "I agree to Jubilee's statement of faith and to deal honestly with every customer." | "Honestly" makes her think of the food rules. She has Rita save the draft, and waits two days. Nothing on the form mentions them. |
| 9 | Ticks the agreement and presses "Publish listing". | "Miriam Haddad Lebanese Trays is live. Members can find you in Community now." "How easy was it to list your business?" | Rita taps 2. Miriam dictates: "No place for food. I did not know if I am allowed." |
| 10 | Rita adds five photos of trays from Miriam's phone in "Edit listing". | "Listing updated. Your changes are live now." | Relief so great she laughs. Photographs of food she has in hundreds. Nobody asked about her kitchen. |
| 11 | A week later, a member asks for a quote: lunch for 40 after a funeral, Saturday. | The quote request, with the member's phone number. | The first valuable moment. She does not type a reply. She telephones, as she always has. |

## Where it could go wrong

- Her unease about home-kitchen rules is never answered, in either direction, and she never
  publishes the draft.
- A member searching for food looks under "Shops" and never opens "Community".
- A request arrives while Rita is at work; Miriam sees a notification she cannot read quickly and
  the member, who needed lunch for Saturday, goes elsewhere.
- Rita moves out, and the listing loses the one person who was comfortable answering it.
