## What she hesitates over

- **Who lives in the home.** The hint says "Renters choose people as much as rooms. A few honest
  lines go a long way." She first writes "Widow, living alone." Mark asks her to take it out. She
  writes about her days instead, and it is still honest.
- **The price.** Rooms nearby are listed at $1,000 and more. She chooses $800 because a student
  can manage it, and wonders if a low price will make people think that something is wrong.
- **Minimum stay.** "4 months" matches a school term. "Month to month" frightens her less if the
  person turns out badly, but she does not want a new stranger every month.
- **A preference.** She would be more at ease with a woman. There is no field for it, and she does
  not know if she is allowed to write it. Mark has read that the rules differ when the owner
  shares a kitchen or bathroom with the renter, but neither of them is sure, so she writes nothing.
- **The basement.** It has its own bathroom but no kitchen and no separate entrance. She cannot
  tell whether it is a "Room" or "Long term", or whether she is allowed to rent it at all.

## What the profile cannot say

- What she hopes to learn about a renter before a viewing: where they study or work, and someone
  who knows them.
- That she would rather meet first at the parish hall or over a video call with her son present.
- That the rent is kept low on purpose, for a student or a newcomer.
- That Mark helps her and may be the one answering messages.
