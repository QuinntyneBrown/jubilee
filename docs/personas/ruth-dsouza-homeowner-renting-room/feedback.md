## How Jubilee could better suit Ruth

| Need | Today | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| Words that include her | "List your business", "My business", "How easy was it to list your business?" appear to an owner of a space | Where the listing is a space, say "space" or "listing" in headings, tabs and the check-in |
| Agree to a viewing without giving her address | "Confirm" releases the exact address at once | For "Room" and "Roommate", a first step such as "Let's talk first", with the address released only when the owner chooses |
| Know what she may ask and must do | The agreement names "Ontario’s tenancy rules" and links nowhere | A short guide for people renting a room in their own home: what the law covers when the kitchen or bathroom is shared, what may be asked, and a simple written agreement |
| Know something about a renter | A request carries a name, a move-in date, a length and an optional note | Let a renter add a line on who they are and a reference, and suggest to hosts that a first meeting be somewhere public or with family present |
| Help writing "Who lives in the home" | One hint: "A few honest lines go a long way." | Two examples under the field, and a caution not to say that you live alone |
| Let her son help | One owner account per listing | A helper the owner names, who can read and answer messages for her |

> These are a persona's suggestions, not requirements. Anything taken up needs a requirement in
> `docs/specs/`, a detailed design and a mock before it is built, and must serve the mission.
