# Ruth's onboarding journey

[Back to Ruth D'Souza](README.md)

## How Ruth finds Jubilee

At a funeral lunch, while they wrap sandwiches, a friend from the Legion of Mary says that her
nephew found his electrician on "that Christian directory, Jubilee" and that people rent rooms on
it too. She writes "jubilee.to" on the back of a Mass card. Ruth types it into her iPad that
evening with Mark on the phone.

## Steps

| Step | What Ruth does | What Jubilee shows | What Ruth thinks or feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reads the marketing site | "Toronto’s Christian businesses, in one place.", "Run a Christian-owned business? Be found." | Friction: "I don't have a business." She nearly closes it. Mark tells her to press "Open Jubilee" |
| 2 | Finds "Rooms & rentals" among the categories on `/welcome` and opens it | Space cards with "Room", a rent "/ month" and a neighbourhood; at the end, "Have a spare room, suite or hall?", "Rent it to someone from the community. Free to list." | "A spare room. That is me." With her large text the cards stack in one column and nothing is cut off |
| 3 | Presses "List a space" and is sent to `/join` | "Join Jubilee", "Free for everyone in Toronto’s Christian community." "Continue with Google" first | She does not know if she has a Google account. Mark says to fill in the form |
| 4 | Types her details; the password takes three tries | "At least 10 characters." and the show-password button | The button helps: she can see what she typed. She fills in Home church because it is "Shown to no one." |
| 5 | Ticks "I am 18 or older and agree to Jubilee’s statement of faith and terms." | "Check your e-mail" | She opens Mail, finds the link and presses it within the hour. "E-mail confirmed", "List your business" |
| 6 | On `/list?kind=space`, chooses "Room" | "A private room in a home you live in." and the field "Who lives in the home" | Friction: she stops here for ten minutes (see [profile setup](profile-setup-journey.md)) |
| 7 | Reaches "Exact address" | "Only the renter whose request you accept sees it." | She reads it aloud to Mark twice. Then she types it |
| 8 | Presses "Save draft" and sleeps on it | The draft is kept; it had been saving as she typed | Next morning it is all still there. She trusts it a little more |
| 9 | Ticks the agreement and presses "Publish listing" | "I agree to Jubilee’s statement of faith, and confirm I’m allowed to rent this space and will follow Ontario’s tenancy rules." then "Sunny room in a quiet East York home is live. Renters can find it in Rooms & rentals now." | Friction: "Ontario’s tenancy rules." Which rules? She ticks it and is uneasy at having promised something she has not read |
| 10 | Adds three photos from My business with Anita's help on a video call | "Up to 6 photos, JPG or PNG, 5 MB each. The first is your cover." | She leaves out any photo that shows the front of the house |
| 11 | That week a nursing student sends "Request a viewing" | "Request a viewing · Sunny room in a quiet East York home", "Move in Fri 1 Jan · 4 months", "Decline" and "Confirm" | First valuable moment, and a fright: "Confirm" reads "Grace gets your exact address when you confirm." She wanted to talk first |

## Where it could go wrong

- The words "business", "List your business" and "My business" tell her at every turn that this
  is not for her.
- To arrange a viewing she must confirm, and confirming gives her address to someone she has only
  seen as a name. She does not know that she can use Messages first.
- She writes "widow living alone" under "Who lives in the home" and it is public.
- She declines everyone, out of caution, and the room stays empty.
