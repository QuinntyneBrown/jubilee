# Luis Herrera: onboarding journey

Back to [Luis Herrera](README.md).

## How Luis finds Jubilee

On a Saturday afternoon, between a brake job and a tire change, Luis's phone buzzes on the bench.
Andrés, a bookkeeper from his church who does the garage's year-end, has sent him the one-page
elevator pitch on WhatsApp with the message that goes with it: "I thought of you straight away."
Luis reads it with one clean knuckle. He does not open the link until 10:40 that night.

## Steps

| Step | What Luis does | What Jubilee shows | What Luis thinks or feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reads the pitch and taps the link to the marketing site. | "Toronto’s Christian businesses, in one place." and "Free for members · Every business verified". | "Verified by who?" He scrolls on. |
| 2 | Scrolls to the owners' section and presses "List your business free". | "Run a Christian-owned business? Be found." The button opens `/join`, and will return him to `/list`. | Glad the word "free" is said twice. He has been burned by directories that bill later. |
| 3 | Fills in "Join Jubilee" by e-mail and password; skips the Google button because the garage Gmail is shared with his apprentice. | First name, Last name, Email, Password ("At least 10 characters."), Home church (optional, "Shown to no one. Helps us recommend businesses near you."), the 18-or-older and statement of faith checkbox. | Friction: ten characters on a cracked screen. The show-password button saves him a third try. He reads the statement of faith before ticking. |
| 4 | Sees "Check your e-mail", but his e-mail is on the office desktop. | A confirmation link that works once, for 24 hours. In the app, the banner "Confirm your e-mail to message businesses. We sent a link to luis.herrera@example.com." | Friction: he goes to bed. He confirms the next morning at the garage, inside the 24 hours without knowing there was a limit. |
| 5 | Opens the link, presses "List your business". | "E-mail confirmed" with "Start browsing" and "List your business". | Straightforward. |
| 6 | On `/list`, chooses "Services" and looks for his category. | "What do you offer?" with "Products", "Services" and "A space". Eleven categories; none is automotive. | Friction, and the sharpest of the journey. He picks "Home & property" because the other trades are there, and feels like a guest at the wrong table. |
| 7 | Adds four services, is called out to a customer, comes back that night. | "Save draft" and the autosave; the draft restored with every value. | Relief. Nothing lost. |
| 8 | Ticks the agreement and presses "Submit for verification". | "We review your listing. Within two business days". Then "How easy was it to list your business?" | Scores it 3. Under "What got in the way?" he writes: "No category for a garage." |
| 9 | Is asked for a change: a photo of his work. Adds three from "Edit listing". | The change needed, the reviewer's first name, and "Edit listing". | Fair enough. He photographs the bays, the hoist and a worn rotor beside a new one. |
| 10 | Opens My business two days later. | "Herrera Auto Service is verified and live. Members can find you in Home & property." | He laughs at "Home & property", then shows Marcela the Verified mark. |
| 11 | Answers his first quote request at 11:20 p.m.: a grinding noise on a 2014 sedan. | The request under Requests, and the conversation, where the member adds a photo of the wheel. | The first valuable moment. The member is from a church across the city he has never visited. |

## Where it could go wrong

- The 24-hour confirmation link expires while his e-mail sits unread on the office desktop, and he
  does not come back.
- The verification team's check "Category fits what they offer" cannot pass honestly for a garage
  in any of the eleven categories; a request to change category would have no good answer.
- A member in the wrong category never finds him: nobody looks for a mechanic under
  "Home & property", so only keyword search leads to him.
- A member with a breakdown messages him at noon, waits, and concludes he does not reply.
