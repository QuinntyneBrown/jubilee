## What he hesitates over

- **The agreement.** He has to "confirm I’m allowed to rent this space". He was not, yet. He
  saves the draft and asks his landlord, which costs him a day and a half that he does not have.
- **The price.** Half of $2,900. A friend says that he could ask $1,600 for the larger room. He
  keeps it at half: he is sharing the rent, not making money from it.
- **Minimum stay.** He wants someone until April at least. "4 months" is the nearest; "6 months"
  might put off a student on a co-op rotation.
- **Who he is looking for.** He would like to say "a guy, around my age". He writes "student or
  new grad" and leaves the rest for when they meet.
- **The summer sublet.** May to August, his own room, while he is away. "Roommate" is wrong
  because he will not be there. "Room" is "a private room in a home you live in", and he will not
  be living in it. "Long term" is a whole apartment. "Short stay" is priced per night. "4 months"
  exists as a minimum stay, but nothing lets him give an end date. He leaves it for the spring.

## What the profile cannot say

- That the room is free from May to August only, with a date on which it ends.
- That his landlord has agreed in writing, which would reassure a careful roommate.
- That he is a student, at which university, and that he is looking for another student.
- His age and his habits as things a person could filter on, not only read in a paragraph.
