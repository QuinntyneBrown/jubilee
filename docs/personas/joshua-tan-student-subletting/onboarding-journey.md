# Joshua's onboarding journey

[Back to Joshua Tan](README.md)

## How Joshua finds Jubilee

On a Tuesday night he posts in the fellowship's group chat that he needs a roommate soon. A staff
worker replies with a link to Jubilee's "Rooms & rentals" page filtered to roommates
(`/c/rooms-rentals?term=roommate`) and "try listing here, it's free". He opens it on the
streetcar home from his shift.

## Steps

| Step | What Joshua does | What Jubilee shows | What Joshua thinks or feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opens the link; his phone is in dark mode | "Rooms & rentals", the filter with "Roommates" pressed, three cards with the badge "Roommate wanted" | It opens dark. Good. Three listings, none near campus |
| 2 | Scrolls to the end | "Have a spare room, suite or hall?", "Rent it to someone from the community. Free to list.", "List a space" | Taps it |
| 3 | Lands on `/join` | "Continue with Google" first | One tap with his university account. He ticks "I am 18 or older and agree to Jubilee’s statement of faith and terms." No e-mail to wait for |
| 4 | Arrives on `/list?kind=space` | "List a space"; the note above still says "Free for Christian-owned businesses in Toronto." | Friction: "I'm not a business." He goes on because the staff worker sent him |
| 5 | Chooses "Roommate" | "Share a place you rent with someone new." and "Who you are, and who you’re looking for" | That is his situation in one sentence. He types fast (see [profile setup](profile-setup-journey.md)) |
| 6 | Looks for where to add photos | "Add photos from My business once you submit. The first photo is your cover." | Friction: the preview "How renters see it" shows a tile and no photo. He wants to see the real card before it goes live |
| 7 | Reaches the agreement | "I agree to Jubilee’s statement of faith, and confirm I’m allowed to rent this space and will follow Ontario’s tenancy rules." | Friction: he stops. Is he allowed? He presses "Save draft" and texts his landlord |
| 8 | Waits a day and a half for the landlord's answer | The draft, as he left it | The landlord agrees to a roommate, in writing. Twelve days of rent money left |
| 9 | Ticks the box and presses "Publish listing" | "Room in a 2-bedroom near Kensington Market is live. Renters can find it in Rooms & rentals now.", with "View public listing" and "Go to My business" | Relief: it is Thursday evening and he is live. But the card has no photo. He scores the check-in 3 and writes "photos" |
| 10 | Shares the link in the group chat at once, then adds five photos from My business | His public page, live, with a tinted tile where the photo should be | For twenty minutes his friends see a room with no picture. He files a bug |
| 11 | On Sunday a second-year student sends "Meet the household" | "Meet the household · Room in a 2-bedroom near Kensington Market", "Move in Tue 1 Dec · 8 months", "Decline" and "Confirm" | First valuable moment. They meet for bubble tea on Monday |

## Where it could go wrong

- He ticks the agreement without asking his landlord, because he is in a hurry and the box is the
  only thing between him and "Publish listing".
- He shares the link before the photos are up, and the first students who open it see a room with
  no picture and move on to a classified site.
- The people looking are few: members who are students have no way to say what they need, so his
  listing waits for someone to happen upon it.
- He tries to list the summer sublet as well, finds no term that fits, and gives up on it.
