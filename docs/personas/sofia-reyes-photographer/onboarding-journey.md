# Sofia's onboarding journey

[Back to Sofia Reyes](README.md)

## How Sofia finds Jubilee

At a wedding in October the florist, whom Sofia has worked beside for years, shows her a quote
request that came in that morning through Jubilee. On Monday the florist sends Sofia her own
listing's share link (L2-038) and writes: "You should be on here. It's free." Sofia opens it on
her phone outside the school at 3:20 p.m.

## Steps

| Step | What Sofia does | What Jubilee shows | What Sofia thinks or feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opens the florist's link as a Visitor | The shop page, with its cover photo and products | She looks at the photos first. They are sharp and load quickly on mobile data |
| 2 | Goes to `/welcome` | "Do business with Toronto's Christian community.", "Join free", "Browse businesses", the eleven categories, "Own a business?" with "List your business" | She taps Media to see who else is there. Four businesses. Room for her |
| 3 | Taps "List your business" | Asked to join first; `/join` with "Sign up with Google" | One tap with the account already on her phone |
| 4 | Accepts the agreement | The statement of faith and terms agreement | She reads the statement of faith in the school yard and agrees. The bell rings; she closes the phone |
| 5 | That night, opens `/list` again | "List your business", "Free for Christian-owned businesses in Toronto. Your listing goes live as soon as you publish it.", "What do you offer?" | She picks "Services" |
| 6 | Types her name, one line and category | The preview "How members see you", below the form on her phone, marked "Not published yet" | Friction: the preview shows a tinted tile where her photo should be. A photographer's card with no photo feels wrong |
| 7 | Looks for where to add photos | Nothing on this form. Photos are under Listing in My business | Friction: she scrolls the form twice looking for them |
| 8 | Adds four services; the youngest wakes up | The draft autosaves after she stops typing | She comes back forty minutes later and everything is still there. Grateful |
| 9 | Presses "Publish listing" | "Sofia Reyes Photography is live. Members can find you in Media now.", with "View public listing", "Go to My business" and "Add more services" | Check-in: she answers 3 and writes in "What got in the way?". She is live, with no photo yet |
| 10 | Adds photos in My business | "Up to 6 photos, JPG or PNG, 5 MB each. The first is your cover." | Friction, twice: only six, and her full-size exports are 14 MB each and are refused. She exports smaller copies on the desktop the next day |
| 11 | Once the photos are up, shares her listing | Her public listing, with her cover photo | She shares the link in her parish mothers' group |
| 12 | Eight days later, confirms a booking for a Christmas mini session | The request, with "Decline" and "Confirm" | The first valuable moment: a family from Scarborough she has never met. She confirms from the car, then messages them to ask for an e-transfer deposit |

## Where it could go wrong

- Step 10 is where she could have given up. A photographer asked to choose six pictures, and then
  told they are too large, has reason to think the product was not made for her.
- A member books a Saturday that is free on Jubilee and taken in her own calendar. She has to
  decline, and a decline in her first month feels like a bad start.
- She asks for a deposit by message and the member, who read that nothing is charged, is
  surprised. Nothing was hidden, but nobody said it either.
- During a full-day wedding she replies eleven hours late and her reply time on the listing
  suffers for it.
