## What she hesitates over

- **Which six photos.** One per kind of work means no kind is shown well. She puts the full
  galleries behind the Website link and hopes members follow it.
- **Hours.** She shoots when the light and the school day allow, which is not a fixed timetable.
  She enters a wide window and expects to decline some times.
- **"Book a time" for a baptism.** The parish sets the date, not her. She chooses "Ask for a
  quote" so that the family tells her the date first.
- **Area.** She shoots weddings anywhere in the city. Etobicoke is where the studio is.
- **Prices.** She gives her own parish a lower price in person. She lists the full price and
  leaves the rest to conversation.

## What the profile cannot say

- Her work at the size it deserves: a gallery per kind of session, not six pictures in all.
- Which dates are already taken.
- That a deposit holds the date, as a term a member sees before they ask, and not only in About.
- Seasonal offers with their own dates, such as three Saturdays of Christmas mini sessions.
- What past families thought, other than kudos a member chooses to show with their first name.
