## How Jubilee could better suit Sofia

| Need | Today | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| To show a portfolio | "Up to 6 photos, JPG or PNG, 5 MB each." | A higher limit for listings in Media, or a small gallery per service |
| To add photos from her phone | A listing photo over 5 MB is refused; a message photo is shrunk on the device | Shrink listing photos on the device as messages already do |
| To see her photos while she lists | Photos are added from My business; the form's preview shows the category tile | Let an owner add the cover photo on `/list`, so the preview is true from the start |
| To keep taken dates from being booked | Bookable times come from the listing's hours | Let an owner block dates, and show "Add to calendar" to the owner too |
| To hold a date with a deposit | Jubilee takes no payment; she asks by message | A line an owner can set on a service, such as "Deposit asked to hold the date, paid to the business", shown in the booking dialog. No payment through Jubilee is needed for this |
| To offer seasonal sessions | Services have no dates of their own | Let a service be offered for a set period and leave the list by itself afterwards |

> These are a persona's suggestions, not requirements. Anything taken up needs a requirement in
> `docs/specs/`, a detailed design and a mock before it is built, and must serve the mission.
