# Amara Mensah: profile setup journey

Back to [Amara Mensah](README.md).

Amara lists no business, so her profile is the member profile and her notification settings. She
fills them in the morning after her first request, on her phone, while her son is in the swing.

## Member profile, `/account`

| Field | What Amara enters | Note |
|---|---|---|
| First name, Last name | Amara, Mensah | From her Google account. |
| Phone | 416-555-0164 | She reads the hint twice: "Shared with a business only when you send a request." Then she tries nine digits to see the error, and corrects it. |
| Postal code | M1J 2H4 | She tries it without the space as well. |
| Home church | The name of her church | "Shown to no one." She believes it because the help pages say the same thing. |
| Home church address | Left empty | She notes that leaving it empty is allowed, and that saving works. |
| Pronouns | she/her | |

She presses "Save profile", then goes back and changes one field and tries to leave, to see
whether she is warned about unsaved changes. She is.

She hesitates over one thing only: the phone. She would rather a daycare had it and a cleaner did
not. The profile holds one number for every request, so she decides to clear it request by request
where a form lets her.

## Notifications, `/account/notifications`

| Switch | Her choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| New messages | On | |
| Request updates | On | These are what she joined for. |
| Meeting reminders | On | She has missed two appointments since July. |
| New businesses | On | She is still looking for a daycare. |
| Tips for business owners | Off | Not for her. |

"How often": this is the long hesitation. "As it happens" means an e-mail buzzing during a feed.
"One daily summary" is sent at 7:00 a.m. the next morning, the hour her son finally sleeps, and it
would hold back the e-mail about a booking she is waiting on. She chooses "As it happens" and
silences her phone at night instead.

## Account housekeeping

She opens the data download and the "delete account" screen without using either, to see that they
are there and what they say. She finds "Set a password", offered because she joined with Google,
and sets one, in case she ever loses the Google account.

## What the profile cannot say

- That she has a baby with her. A cleaner or photographer coming to the home would want to know;
  she retypes it in every request note.
- When she can be reached. She would like to say "messages, not calls" and "afternoons".
- Which number goes to which kind of business.
- A quiet time for notifications. The choice is each e-mail as it happens or one summary at
  7:00 a.m.
- Anything about her as a customer. There is no member photo, bio or public member page, which she
  is content with as a member and would question as an owner letting a stranger into a home.
