## Story

Amara was born in Toronto to parents from Kumasi and grew up in Scarborough. She has spent eight
years finding the faults in banking apps before customers do, and she is good at it: patient,
exact, and kind in how she writes a defect up.

Her son arrived in July. Since then her list has grown faster than she can work through it: a
will, now that there is a child; a daycare place for next summer; someone to photograph the baby
before he stops being small; and help cleaning the apartment. She does her errands on her phone
during night feeds, with the brightness down and one thumb free.

## What she wants from Jubilee

- A family lawyer for a will, a daycare, a baby photographer and a cleaner, each of them someone
  she can trust in her home or with her family's affairs.
- To send a request at 3 a.m. and have it answered in business hours, without a phone call.
- Software that behaves: clear errors, nothing lost when the signal drops in the nursery.

## What worries her

- Who stands behind a listing, exactly. Nobody checks a business before it goes live, so she will
  want to read how Jubilee keeps listings honest before she relies on one.
- That a listing is all she can go on: there are no reviews or star ratings of businesses.
- That her feedback will vanish into a form, as it does in most apps.

## How she serves the mission

Amara is the neighbour on the other side of the counter: by choosing to hire within the household
of faith, and by telling Jubilee plainly what is broken, she helps "Christian business people in
Toronto" be found and be better served.

## In her words

"I am not being difficult. I am being specific. It is the kindest thing a tester can do."
