# Amara Mensah: onboarding journey

Back to [Amara Mensah](README.md).

## How Amara finds Jubilee

In the mothers' WhatsApp group from her church, Amara asks whether anyone knows a lawyer for a
simple will. Abena, who had her own done in the spring, replies with a link: a shared listing for
a family lawyer in Scarborough, and "She was lovely. It's on Jubilee." Amara opens it at 3:10 a.m.
on a Tuesday, with her son asleep on her left arm.

## Steps

| Step | What Amara does | What Jubilee shows | What Amara thinks or feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opens the shared link as a visitor. | The lawyer's listing page: the Verified mark, services, "Book a meeting", "Message". | No wall, no pop-up asking her to join first. She approves. |
| 2 | Scrolls to the footer and opens "Help" to find what "Verified" means. | The help pages, and the statement of faith linked beside them. | She wants to know who checks and what they check. She reads for six minutes. |
| 3 | Goes back and presses "Book a meeting". | The sign-in prompt, with "Sign in" and "Join free". | Expected. She checks that the prompt opens as a sheet she can reach with her thumb. It does. |
| 4 | Joins with Google, one-handed. | An account from her Google name and e-mail, already confirmed, then the agreement to the statement of faith and terms. | The right choice at 3 a.m.: no password to type. She reads the terms later, properly. |
| 5 | Returns to the listing and presses "Book a meeting" again. | "Ask for a time. {Owner} confirms it or suggests another; nothing is charged." Service, Day, How (video call or in person), Time, "What would you like to talk about?" | She tries a Sunday first, to see what happens. It cannot be selected. Good. |
| 6 | Picks a video call on Thursday at 1:30 p.m., nap time, and writes two lines about a will and guardianship. | The request sent, with "Close" and "View request", and "How easy was it to send your request?" | Scores it 5. Then she opens "View request" to check that it says what she sent. It does. |
| 7 | Loses signal in the nursery and keeps browsing. | The banner "You're offline. Showing the directory saved at {time}. Search and messages will work again when you're connected." | Delighted, professionally. She writes a message to the lawyer on purpose; it is marked "Waiting to send" and goes when she walks to the hallway. |
| 8 | Wakes to an e-mail and a notification. | The booking confirmed, with "Add to calendar". | The first valuable moment. A lawyer booked without one phone call, by a mother who cannot make phone calls. |
| 9 | That week: saves two daycares in "Health & family" and a photographer in "Media"; asks a cleaner in "Home & property" for a quote. | Her Saved list; "Tell {owner} what you have in mind. There's no obligation to buy." | She is now a regular. She has also started a note titled "Jubilee findings". |

## Where it could go wrong

- She cannot tell a daycare's quality from a listing. With no reviews, she falls back on asking
  the WhatsApp group, and Jubilee becomes only the place she sends the request.
- A request sits unanswered for days. The app shows it as open; nothing tells her whether the
  owner has seen it.
- One real bug at 3 a.m. with nothing saved, and a tester's trust is gone for good.
- Notifications as they happen wake the baby; she silences Jubilee altogether and misses a
  confirmation.
