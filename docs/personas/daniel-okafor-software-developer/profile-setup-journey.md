# Daniel's profile setup journey

## Member profile at `/account`

Google filled in his name. Daniel fills in the rest in one sitting on his laptop.

| Field | What he enters | Why, or why he hesitates |
|---|---|---|
| First name, Last name | Daniel, Okafor | From Google; unchanged |
| Phone | 416-555-0134 | The hint "Shared with a business only when you send a request." is enough for him |
| Postal code | M6K 3P6 | He checks the privacy page first to see what a business sees of it |
| Home church | The name of his church | Optional and "Shown to no one." He fills it in to see whether recommendations change |
| Home church address | Left empty | He does not see what it adds once the name is there |
| Pronouns | he/him | No hesitation |

At `/account/notifications` he leaves "New messages", "Request updates" and "Meeting reminders"
on, turns on "New businesses", and keeps "As it happens". In the account menu he sets the theme
to System.

## The listing, four months later

After the accountant and the mover both go well, Daniel opens `/list` on a Sunday afternoon.

| Field | What he enters |
|---|---|
| What do you offer? | Services |
| Business name | Okafor Web Studio |
| One line on what you do | Fast, accessible websites for small businesses and churches. One developer, evenings. |
| Category | Business & tech |
| Area you serve | Toronto, remote |
| Service 1 | "Website check-up", "45 minutes by video. Bring your site address.", Free, Book a time |
| Service 2 | "Small business website", "Five pages, mobile first, two to four weeks.", Ask for a quote |
| Service 3 | "Care plan", "Updates, backups and small edits, per month.", Fixed price $120, Ask for a quote |

He ticks "I agree to Jubilee's statement of faith and to deal honestly with every customer." and
presses "Publish listing". The page says "Okafor Web Studio is live. Members can find you in
Business & tech now." The check-in asks "How easy was it to list your business?" and he answers 4.

In My business, under Listing, he adds:

- **Photos:** six screenshots of sites he has built, each with alt text. He exports them as PNG
  because the first three were WebP and were refused.
- **About:** "I'm Daniel. By day I build software for a logistics company; in the evenings I build
  websites for small businesses, churches and charities. I work alone, I answer messages after
  6 p.m., and I take on one project at a time so that yours gets finished."
- **Hours:** Monday to Thursday 7:00 to 9:30 p.m., Saturday 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
- **Website:** `https://okaforweb.example.com`
- **Highlight:** Free 45-minute site check-up

## What he hesitates over

- **"Christian-owned businesses".** Is an unincorporated side business a business? He decides it
  is: he invoices, he pays tax on it, and the accountant he found on Jubilee says so.
- **Hours.** Bookable times come from the listing's hours, so he must publish his evenings. He
  would rather offer the times his calendar shows as free.
- **"Area you serve".** His clients are anywhere; the work is remote. The field is free text, so
  he writes "Toronto, remote" and wonders which area chip on the category page will find him.
- **Price.** He nearly leaves the care plan as "Ask", then decides a plain price is more honest.

## What the profile cannot say

- Which technologies he works in, as tags a member could search.
- More than one link: there is one Website field and no place for a code repository or case study.
- More than six pieces of work, or a before and after of the same site.
- When he is truly free: his real calendar is not connected, and "Add to calendar" is one way.
- That he builds one church or charity site a year without charge, other than in the About text.
