## How Jubilee could better suit Victoria

| Need | Today | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| Let her assistant work the listings | One owner account per listing; no staff or delegate accounts | A delegate the owner invites, who can edit the listing and answer messages and requests, with the owner's name still on the agreement |
| Choose between renters fairly | A space request carries move-in, staying and an optional note; no application or references | A short, optional set of questions the owner writes once and every renter answers, with a note on what Ontario law does not let an owner ask |
| Offer one unit at a lower rent without a spectacle | One public price per space | Let an owner mark a space "rent by arrangement", or share a private link to an unlisted space with a pastor or a settlement worker who knows a family |
| Know the rules on choosing tenants | The agreement names "Ontario’s tenancy rules" only | A plain page for owners on the Residential Tenancies Act and the Human Rights Code, linked from the agreement and from "List a space" |
| More than five homes, in words that fit | "You can list up to 5 businesses. Write to help@jubilee.example for more." | Say "listings" in that message, and tell owners of spaces what the team needs to raise the limit |
| Not be put on display | The host card shows the owner's name on each space | Nothing new: keep counts of an owner's spaces off every public page |

> These are a persona's suggestions, not requirements. Anything taken up needs a requirement in
> `docs/specs/`, a detailed design and a mock before it is built, and must serve the mission.
