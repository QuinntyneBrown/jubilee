## How Jubilee could better suit Devon

| Need | Today | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| To know whether he may list at all | "Free for Christian-owned businesses in Toronto."; Help does not cover employees | Decide it, and say it in Help and on `/list`: who may list, in a sentence, with the case of a Christian working in a business owned by someone else named |
| A way to be listed as what he is | A listing is a business with one owner account | If the answer is yes, a kind of listing for a professional within a business, which names the business, says plainly that it is not Christian-owned or not on Jubilee, and says plainly what the person is |
| A category for his trade | Eleven categories; none for vehicles | Consider a category for vehicles (sales, repair, body work), or say which existing one such trades belong in |
| To ask before he publishes | "Publish listing" puts the listing live at once; the only way to ask is by e-mail | Let an owner ask the Jubilee team a question from the draft when they are unsure they may list |
| To show he is registered, given his trade | No licence or credential fields | Where a trade has a public register of registered salespeople, let the owner give the registration with a link a member can follow to check it |
| A clear answer | Help does not say who may list; a listing that misrepresents a business is taken down after it is live | When a question of who Jubilee is for comes up, answer it by a person before the owner publishes, not by a takedown afterwards |

> These are a persona's suggestions, not requirements. Anything taken up needs a requirement in
> `docs/specs/`, a detailed design and a mock before it is built, and must serve the mission.
