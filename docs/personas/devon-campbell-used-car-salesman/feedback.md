## How Jubilee could better suit Devon

| Need | Today | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| To know whether he may list at all | "Free for Christian-owned businesses in Toronto."; Help does not cover employees | Decide it, and say it in Help and on `/list`: who may list, in a sentence, with the case of a Christian working in a business owned by someone else named |
| A way to be listed as what he is | A listing is a business with one owner account | If the answer is yes, a kind of listing for a professional within a business, which names the business, says plainly that it is not Christian-owned or not on Jubilee, and is verified on the person |
| A category for his trade | Eleven categories; none for vehicles | Consider a category for vehicles (sales, repair, body work), or say which existing one such trades belong in |
| To answer a change request he cannot meet | "Edit listing" and "Resubmit" | Let an owner reply to the reviewer from My business when a requested change is not possible |
| To be checked, given his trade | No licence or credential fields | Where a trade has a public register of registered salespeople, check it and show that it was checked |
| A clear decision | Approve, request changes or decline | When a listing raises a question of who Jubilee is for, answer the question first and by a person, before any decision is recorded |

> These are a persona's suggestions, not requirements. Anything taken up needs a requirement in
> `docs/specs/`, a detailed design and a mock before it is built, and must serve the mission.
