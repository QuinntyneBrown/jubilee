# Rajan's profile setup journey

[Back to Rajan Thomas](README.md)

Rajan is a Member and lists nothing, so his profile is the account page, the notification
settings and the choices in the account menu. He treats each field as a question: who sees this,
and what is it for?

## Member profile at `/account`

| Field | What he enters | Why, or why he hesitates |
|---|---|---|
| First name, Last name | Rajan, Thomas | The terms ask for a real name. He gives it |
| Phone | Left empty | "Shared with a business only when you send a request." He prefers to add it request by request, where the quote dialog says it is shared for that request only |
| Postal code | M2N 5X2 | He enters it after reading that a business sees at most the neighbourhood and the first three characters |
| Home church | Left empty | "Shown to no one" satisfies him on privacy. He leaves it out because recommendations are not a purpose he needs |
| Home church address | Left empty | The same |
| Pronouns | Not set | The hint says it is used in messages written about him. None are likely for a member who lists nothing |

He presses "Save profile" and sees "Profile saved. Saved today at 9:52 a.m."

## Notification settings at `/account/notifications`

| Setting | His choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| New messages | On | A lawyer's reply should reach him |
| Request updates | On | The same |
| Meeting reminders | On | He would set his own, but one more does no harm |
| New businesses | Off | He will search when he needs something |
| Tips for business owners | Off | He is not one |
| How often | One daily summary | One e-mail at 7:00 a.m. suits him better than several during work |

## Other choices

- **Theme:** Dark, from the account menu. It is remembered on his desktop and in his account.
- **Sign-in:** e-mail and password, from a password manager. He looks for a second step at
  sign-in and does not find one.
- **Data download:** requested on his first day, to see what is kept. The archive holds his
  profile, saved list, requests, messages and notification settings.
- **Saved list:** an eavestrough installer in North York, a roofer, the lawyer, and a shop that
  sells hand-bound journals, for Anita's birthday.

## What he hesitates over

- **The postal code.** It is the only field he fills in that he was not required to. He does it
  so that "Serves North York" means something when he filters Home & property.
- **Whether to tell a business anything before it has earned it.** His first quote request to
  the eavestrough installer gives the house's age and the length of the run, and no phone number.

## What the profile cannot say

Rajan is glad there is no public member page, no photo and no bio. What he misses is on the other
side of the conversation:

- He cannot tell a business how he prefers to be reached, or at what hours.
- He cannot leave an account of work done that the next member can read. Kudos shows praise with
  a first name if he ticks the box; it has no place for a measured or a critical word.
- He cannot see, for a listing, when it was last checked and against what.
