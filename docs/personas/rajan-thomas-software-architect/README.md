## Story

Rajan is Malayali, from Kottayam in Kerala. He came to Canada in 2001 with a master's degree and
one suitcase, started as a contract programmer, and has spent twenty years at the same bank,
where he now signs off the design of systems that hold other people's money.

The house in Willowdale is thirty years old and needs something every season. He and Anita have
also reached the age of wills, powers of attorney and a parent's care, and he would rather hire
people who share his faith when the matter is that close to the family. He is not looking for a
discount. He is looking for a reason to trust someone before the first meeting.

## What he wants from Jubilee

- Tradespeople for the house: eavestroughs this autumn, a furnace next year.
- A lawyer for two wills and powers of attorney, and later an adviser for retirement.
- Gifts from shops he would be glad to support.
- Evidence. Who checked this business, what did they check, and what have others found?

## What worries him

- That a listing is only its owner's own words, with nothing checked behind them.
- What Jubilee keeps about him, who on staff can read it, and whether deleting means deleting.
- That fellow feeling stands in for due diligence. A shared faith is not a reference.

## How he serves the mission

Rajan is not a business person; he is the neighbour through whom Jubilee "helps Christian
business owners in Toronto". Every roof, will and gift he buys through it is work given to one
of them, and his hard questions are a service to the rest.

## In his words

"Trust me because we pray alike is not an argument I accept at work, and I won't accept it for
my roof. Show me what you checked."
