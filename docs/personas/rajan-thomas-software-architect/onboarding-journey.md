# Rajan's onboarding journey

[Back to Rajan Thomas](README.md)

## How Rajan finds Jubilee

Anita asks a friend at church who drew up her will. The friend sends a shared listing link
(L2-038) for Northgate Wills & Estates, and Anita forwards it to Rajan by e-mail with "Can you
look into this?" He opens it at his desk on a Saturday morning. Before he looks at the lawyer he
looks at who is hosting the page.

## Steps

| Step | What Rajan does | What Jubilee shows | What Rajan thinks or feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opens jubilee.to in a second tab | "Toronto’s Christian businesses, in one place." and "Free for members · Free to list" | "Free to list" makes him ask who keeps a fraud out. He goes looking for the answer |
| 2 | Opens the app's Help page and expands "How does Jubilee keep listings honest?" | The answer, and "Still stuck? Write to us and a person answers within one business day." | He learns that nobody checks a listing before it goes live: every owner agrees to deal honestly, members can report a listing, and the Jubilee team takes down any that misrepresent a business. Honest, he thinks, and thin |
| 3 | Reads the privacy page and the terms | "What we keep", "Your choices", the privacy contact; "Jubilee does not take payment or hold funds; you pay the business directly." | Better than most. No payment data is the best security decision on the site |
| 4 | Returns to the lawyer's listing as a Visitor and tabs through it | "Legal & immigration", services with prices, "Book a meeting", "Message", "Report this listing" | Everything is reachable by keyboard and the focus is always visible. He notices, and it raises his opinion of the whole product |
| 5 | Presses "Book a meeting" | The sign-in prompt, with "Sign in" and "Join free" | Fair. Browsing was open; contact needs an account |
| 6 | Joins with e-mail and a generated password | "Join Jubilee". He passes over the Google button and leaves both home church fields empty | He does not link accounts across services. "At least 10 characters." is a reasonable minimum |
| 7 | Ticks the agreement and confirms his e-mail | "Check your e-mail", then "E-mail confirmed" with "Continue where you left off" | The link works once and for 24 hours. He approves of both limits |
| 8 | Sets the theme to Dark in the account menu | The theme changes without a reload | Small, and done properly |
| 9 | Books a meeting with the lawyer | "Ask for a time. Miriam confirms it or suggests another; nothing is charged." He chooses "In person" and writes one line: two wills, two powers of attorney | He leaves the phone out. The lawyer can reach him through messages |
| 10 | Reads the confirmation | "Request sent to Northgate Wills & Estates." and "How easy was it to send your request?" | He answers 4. Easy; he is still not sure whom he has booked |
| 11 | Checks the lawyer against the public register of the law society, on his own | Nothing: this step happens outside Jubilee | Friction, and the heart of his complaint. Jubilee gave him a name; he did the checking |
| 12 | The same afternoon, requests his data download from the account page | An e-mail within 24 hours with a link to a JSON archive that needs him signed in and works for 7 days | He opens the file, finds what he expects and nothing he does not. This is his first valuable moment as much as the booking is |

## Where it could go wrong

- If the privacy page had been vague, he would not have joined, and Anita would have phoned the
  lawyer directly.
- If he later hires a tradesperson through Jubilee who does poor work, there is nowhere to say
  so that the next member will see. He will say it at church.
- If he finds one listing whose claims are plainly untrue, and it is still up days after he
  reports it, he will stop trusting all of them.
