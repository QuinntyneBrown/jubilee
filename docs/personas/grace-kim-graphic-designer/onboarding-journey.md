# Grace Kim: onboarding journey

Back to [Grace Kim](README.md).

## How Grace finds Jubilee

After the English ministry's Sunday service, Grace is pinning the new bulletin to the board when
Joon, who runs a small print shop and prints it for her, hands her a business card with
"jubilee.to" on it. "You should be on this," he says. "I am." She puts the card in her phone case
and opens the site on the subway home, mostly to see who designed it.

## Steps

| Step | What Grace does | What Jubilee shows | What Grace thinks or feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opens the marketing site on her phone. | "Toronto’s Christian businesses, in one place.", "Start browsing", "Watch the film". | She notices the wordmark first: lowercase, wide, tightly tracked. "Someone cared." |
| 2 | Watches the 1:04 film with captions, sound off. | The pitch film. | It moves her more than she expected. She is wary of anything that sells with faith, and this does not. |
| 3 | Presses "Open Jubilee" and browses as a visitor. | `/welcome`: "Do business with Toronto's Christian community.", the eleven categories, "How Jubilee works". | She looks for "Design". There is "Media" and there is "Business & tech". Friction: she is not sure which she is. |
| 4 | Opens three listings in "Media" to see how photographers and videographers present themselves. | Listing pages with the Verified mark, services and prices. | She studies the cards as a designer: the photo, the one line, the green highlight. Some prices say "Ask". She notes that. |
| 5 | Presses "Message" on Joon's print shop to say hello. | The sign-in prompt, with "Sign in" and "Join free". | Fair. She joins that evening on her laptop. |
| 6 | Joins with Google. | An account from her Google name and e-mail, already confirmed, then the statement of faith and terms agreement. | Fast. She reads the statement of faith properly; her church would agree with it. |
| 7 | Opens "List your business" from the "Own a business?" panel. | `/list`: "Free for Christian-owned businesses in Toronto. Every listing is verified before it goes live." | "Business" feels large for one person at a bedroom desk. She goes on anyway. |
| 8 | Chooses "Services", types her name and one line, and watches the preview. | "How members see you", updating as she types, marked "Pending verification". | The best part of the form. She rewrites the one line four times against the card. |
| 9 | Chooses "Media", adds four services, submits. | "We review your listing. Within two business days". "How easy was it to list your business?" | Scores it 4. The category choice cost her ten minutes. |
| 10 | Is approved the next afternoon; adds photos and her website in "Edit listing". | "Grace Kim Design is verified and live. Members can find you in Media." | Proud, and already rearranging her six photos. |
| 11 | Receives a booking for her free intro call from a couple opening a bakery. | The request under Requests; she confirms it. | The first valuable moment. They found her, they did not ask for a discount, and they came with a budget. |

## Where it could go wrong

- She picks the wrong one of two plausible categories and members searching the other never see
  her.
- The first three messages are requests for free work "for the ministry", and she pauses her
  listing.
- She joins hoping to meet peers, finds only a directory of businesses and customers, and treats
  Jubilee as one more place to be listed, nothing more.
