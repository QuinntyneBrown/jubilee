- **Hours**: Monday to Friday 9 to 5, by appointment.
- **Website**: `https://gracekimdesign.example.com`, where the full portfolio lives.
- **Highlight**: Free 30-minute intro call

## What the profile cannot say

- A portfolio. Six photos and one website link, with no case studies, no link per project and no
  place for her Instagram or Behance pages.
- Who she is. There is no owner photo or bio apart from the About text.
- That she works with clients anywhere in Toronto.
- That she wants to meet other owners. Nothing on a listing says "open to collaborating".
- Her pricing policy: one clear line that she charges churches and businesses the same rate.
