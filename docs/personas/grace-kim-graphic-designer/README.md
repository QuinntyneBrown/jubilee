## Story

Grace was born in Toronto to parents who run a dry-cleaning shop, and she grew up doing their
signs. She studied design, spent three years at an agency making campaigns for brands she did not
care about, and left to work for the kind of client she grew up around: small shops, churches and
non-profits that have never had a designer.

Two years in, the work is steady but lonely. She works from her bedroom desk, and most of the
business owners she knows are her parents' age. She would like to know other Christians who are
building something, and not only as customers.

## What she wants from Jubilee

- Clients who value design and will pay for it.
- A listing that looks as considered as her own work.
- To meet other Christian business people: peers, collaborators, a printer she can trust.

## What worries her

- Showing prices. In her experience, "you're from church" is often the opening line of a request
  for a discount, and she finds it hard to say no.
- That six photos and one line cannot carry a portfolio.
- Being placed in a category where nobody looks for a designer.

## How she serves the mission

Grace reads "equips Christian business people in Toronto" as being about people like her parents:
her craft is what lets a faithful small business be seen clearly. The bulletin she designs each
week, unpaid, is her own small way to "resource His Kingdom".

## In her words

"A good logo will not make anyone's business faithful. But a faithful business deserves not to
look like an afterthought."
