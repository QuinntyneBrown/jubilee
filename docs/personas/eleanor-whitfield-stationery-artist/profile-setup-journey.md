  text, written carefully: "Five watercolour greeting cards with garden flowers".
- **About**: "I taught art to children for thirty-one years and now letter and paint at my dining
  table in the Beaches. Every card is drawn and painted by hand, then printed in small numbers on
  heavy paper. I am glad to make something for a wedding, a baptism or a funeral. Please ask."
- **Hours**: By appointment
- **Website**: left empty.
- **Highlight**: Hand-lettered in the Beaches
- **A service**, added because "Offer both? Pick the main one now and add the other once you're
  live.": "Wedding invitations, hand-lettered", short detail "Allow eight weeks. Tell me the
  date and the number.", pricing "Ask for a quote".

## What the profile cannot say

- That she works from home and has no shop to visit. "Store address" assumes a store.
- What posting costs and how long it takes, in a place she could find. The product editor asks for
  a photo, name, price, stock and description only.
- How she is paid. She accepts e-transfer and cheques, and types that into every message.
- That a product is seasonal: "Back for Advent" instead of "Sold out".
- Where to find her in person: the dates of the bazaars and craft fairs she still attends.
