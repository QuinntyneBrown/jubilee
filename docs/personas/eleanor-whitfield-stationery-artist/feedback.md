## How Jubilee could better suit Eleanor

| Need | Today | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy for a home-based maker | A Products listing asks for a "Store address", with no word on who sees it. | Let a home-based owner give a neighbourhood only, and say on the form who sees the address. |
| Knowing what to do after "Confirm" | The owner confirms or declines; the toast says "Message him about payment and pickup." No payments, deposits or invoices. | A short plain-words guide on the confirmed request: agree how to pay, agree how it will arrive, then send. Offer a message she can send with one tap. |
| Posting her cards | An order request offers the shop's delivery methods, but she could not find where to say what posting costs. | A clear "How do you deliver?" step in listing a shop: pick-up, post, a flat price. |
| Seasonal products | A product is "Listed" or, at stock 0, "Sold out", with an optional restock note. | Let a product be marked seasonal with a "Back in" month, shown in place of "Sold out". |
| Not missing requests | In-app notifications and e-mail; her daily summary arrives the next morning. | Explain the delay beside "One daily summary", and offer a telephone or text alert for new requests. |
| Help at her pace | A "Help" link in the footer and the help address. | A printable one-page guide to listing a shop, and larger-print step-by-step help for the first order. |

> These are a persona's suggestions, not requirements. Anything taken up needs a requirement in
> `docs/specs/`, a detailed design and a mock before it is built, and must serve the mission.
