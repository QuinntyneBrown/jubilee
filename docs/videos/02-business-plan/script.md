# 02 · The Jubilee plan

The narration, scene by scene. The windows each line is spoken in are in `narration.json`; this file
is the transcript. About 170 words over 80 seconds. The wording is a first draft and has not been
signed off.

## 01 Toronto

Every week in Toronto, believers ask the same question: who can we trust to do the work?

## 02 Dealing honestly

Jubilee is Toronto's Christian marketplace. Every owner is a Christian who agrees to deal honestly with every customer.

## 03 Find, message, book

Members find a business, message it, book a meeting or ask for a quote, then pay the business directly.

## 04 Launch

Two hundred founding businesses open the beta on February first. Everyone else joins on April sixth, twenty twenty-seven.

## 05 How businesses pay

A basic listing is free, forever. Plus is twenty-nine dollars a month. Pro, seventy-nine. Founding businesses get Plus free for a year.

## 06 Homes

In September, homes arrive: rooms, apartments and sublets. Free for renters. Landlords pay thirty-nine dollars a listing.

## 07 Stays

In May twenty twenty-eight, short-term and monthly stays follow, booked and paid through Jubilee, for an eleven percent fee.

## 08 Growth

Revenue grows to three point one million dollars by twenty thirty-one. We break even in twenty thirty.

## 09 Raise and roadmap

We're raising a seven hundred and fifty thousand dollar pre-seed. Next come Jubilee Pay, events, and cities beyond Toronto.

## 10 Close

Jubilee. Your community is open for business.
