# 05 · Journey 2: view a business and its products

The narration, scene by scene. The windows each line is spoken in are in `narration.json`; this file
is the transcript. About 180 words over 80 seconds. The last scene is the mission, word for word
(`docs/specs/L0.md`).

## 01 Title

Journey two: view a business and its products. Here is what the app does now.

## 02 The owner is told

When an owner sets a product's stock to zero, a toast says it is sold out, and that members can still ask to be told when it's back.

## 03 Sold out, in words

On the shop page, the card of that product says: Sold out. In words, on a desktop and on a phone.

## 04 A member asks

On the product page, a member presses: Tell me when it's back. The page answers: We'll tell you when it's back.

## 05 Told in the app

When the stock returns, each member who asked is notified once: a toast with View in shop, and a line in their notifications.

## 06 And by e-mail

They get one e-mail too, unless they turned Request updates off. Jubilee takes no payment. The shop confirms stock and says how to pay.

## 07 The preview status

An owner who opens a listing that is not public sees a preview, with its status. So does the verification team.

## 08 Close

Abiding in Jesus and for love of Him, Jubilee equips Christian business people in Toronto to prosper with purpose and resource His Kingdom.
