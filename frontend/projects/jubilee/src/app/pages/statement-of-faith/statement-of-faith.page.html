Statement of faith

Jubilee is open to everyone as a customer. Businesses that list here agree to the commitments below, and a pastor or elder confirms them.

What we believe

We believe in one God — Father, Son and Holy Spirit — and that Jesus Christ is Lord. We hold the Bible to be God’s word and our guide for faith and for work.

How we do business

  • We tell the truth about what we sell, what it costs and when it will be ready.
  • We treat every customer with the same care, whatever their background or belief.
  • We keep our word, and we put it right when we fall short.
  • We see profit as something to steward: for our families, our churches and our city.

How a listing is confirmed

Each owner names a pastor or elder who knows them. We contact that person privately before the listing goes live. Read more in Help.
